Northstar Remote Work Policy

Remote work is available where business and security requirements permit.

Eligibility

Employees become eligible after completing 120 days of continuous employment and receiving manager approval.

Working schedule

Approved employees may work remotely up to two days each week. Core collaboration hours are 10:00–16:00.

Information security

Employees must use a company-managed device and connect through the corporate VPN.

Questions should be sent to the People Operations team.
