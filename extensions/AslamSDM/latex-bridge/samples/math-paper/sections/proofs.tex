\section{Proofs}
\label{sec:proofs-body}

\subsection{The localized Poincar\'e inequality}

We first record the analytic ingredient. The following lemma is essentially
due to Diaconis and Saloff-Coste; we include a self-contained proof.

\begin{lemma}
\label{lem:local-poincare}
Let $G$ be finite with symmetric generating set $S$. For every $f \in \ell^2(G)$,
\[
\lVert f - \mathbb{E} f \rVert_2^2
\;\le\; \frac{d^2}{h(G,S)^2}
\sum_{s \in S} \lVert f - f_s \rVert_2^2 .
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Fix a geodesic path from $x$ to $y$ in $\Gamma(G,S)$ and telescope the
differences $f - f_s$ along it; summing over $y$ and using the local
expansion bound gives the claim. The constant $\frac{d^2}{h(G,S)^2}$ is
sharp for the two-step random walk, as shown by \cite[Prop.~2.7]{DiaconisSaloffCoste}.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Interpolation and the spectral radius}

Let $\sigma(P)$ denote the spectrum of the transition operator, and write
$\sigma(P) \setminus \{1\}$ for its nontrivial part. The following estimate
is classical.

\begin{equation}
\label{eq:interp}
\rho_{n} \;:=\; \max\bigl\{ |\lambda| \;:\; \lambda \in \sigma(P) \setminus \{1\} \bigr\}
\;\le\; \left( 1 - \frac{h(G,S)^2}{2 d^2} \right)^{1/n}
\end{equation}

Combining Lemma~\ref{lem:local-poincare} with \eqref{eq:interp} and
Corollary~\ref{cor:explicit} yields Theorem~\ref{thm:gap}. The verification
that the constants compose is a matter of arithmetic:
\[
\frac{c\, h(G,S)^2}{d^2} \;\ge\; \frac{h(G,S)^2}{64\, d^2}
\]
with equality only in the degenerate case $|S| = 1$.
