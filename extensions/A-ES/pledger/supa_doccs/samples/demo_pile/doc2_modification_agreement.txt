THIS LOAN MODIFICATION AGREEMENT ("Modification Agreement") is entered into on this 9th day of October, 2024, by and between SURYODAYA MICROFINANCE PRIVATE LIMITED (the "Lender") and Mrs. Lakshmi Devi Patil (the "Borrower"), parties to the original Loan Agreement No. MFI-2024-00187 dated 14th March 2024 (the "Original Agreement").

RECITALS

WHEREAS the Borrower availed a loan of INR 1,85,000 under the Original Agreement at an interest rate of 26.00% per annum on reducing balance, repayable in 24 EMIs of INR 8,976 each, as more particularly described in the Original Agreement;

WHEREAS the Borrower has maintained a satisfactory repayment record for the first 6 (six) instalments, with zero instances of delayed payment, as verified from the Lender's collection records;

WHEREAS, as part of the Lender's Diwali 2024 "Loyal Borrower Rate Benefit Scheme" (Circular No. SMPL/CREDIT/2024/091 dated 1st October 2024) extended to borrowers with an unblemished repayment record for the first two quarters of their loan tenure, the Lender has agreed to reduce the interest rate applicable to the Borrower's outstanding loan;

WHEREAS the Borrower has additionally requested, and the Lender has agreed to grant, a one-time moratorium on the instalment falling due in November 2024, on account of the Borrower's representation of a temporary cash-flow disruption caused by unseasonal rainfall affecting local market demand for tailoring services during the festival season;

NOW THEREFORE, the parties agree to modify the Original Agreement as follows, with all other terms and conditions of the Original Agreement remaining in full force and effect except as expressly modified herein:

1. REVISED RATE OF INTEREST

1.1 Effective from the instalment due on 14th October 2024 (i.e., from Instalment No. 7 onward), the Rate of Interest applicable to the outstanding Principal shall stand REDUCED from 26.00% per annum to 22.50% (Twenty-Two point Five percent) per annum, computed on a reducing balance basis.

1.2 The revised Annual Percentage Rate (APR) disclosure, incorporating the reduced interest rate and the residual amortized impact of fees already paid at origination, is as follows:

    (a) Interest component (reducing balance), revised: 22.50% p.a.
    (b) Residual amortized impact of processing fee already paid: 0.40% p.a.
    (c) Residual amortized impact of insurance premium already paid: 0.25% p.a.
    (d) REVISED TOTAL ANNUAL PERCENTAGE RATE (APR): 23.15% p.a.

1.3 No further processing fee, modification fee, or other charge is levied on the Borrower in connection with this rate revision. This is a benefit extended at the Lender's discretion under the referenced scheme and does not constitute a fresh loan origination.

2. REVISED EMI AND OUTSTANDING PRINCIPAL AS OF MODIFICATION DATE

2.1 As per the Lender's collection ledger, the outstanding Principal as of 14th September 2024 (after adjustment of Instalment No. 6) stood at INR 1,53,503 (Indian Rupees One Lakh Fifty-Three Thousand Five Hundred Three Only).

2.2 Basis the revised interest rate of 22.50% p.a. and the residual tenure of 18 (eighteen) months (Instalment Nos. 7 to 24), the revised EMI applicable from Instalment No. 7 onward shall be INR 8,714 (Indian Rupees Eight Thousand Seven Hundred Fourteen Only), as per the revised amortization schedule at Schedule A-1 to this Modification Agreement.

3. MORATORIUM

3.1 The instalment originally scheduled for 14th November 2024 (which, under the revised schedule, would be Instalment No. 8) is hereby WAIVED IN TIMING ONLY — i.e., no EMI collection shall be attempted in November 2024, and interest shall continue to accrue on the outstanding Principal during this period at the revised rate of 22.50% p.a.

3.2 The tenure of the Loan stands correspondingly extended by 1 (one) month, from the original 24 months to 25 months, with the final instalment now falling due on 14th April 2026 instead of 14th March 2026.

3.3 This moratorium is granted as a one-time exception and shall not be construed as a waiver of the Lender's rights to enforce strict repayment discipline for any subsequent default.

4. NO OTHER CHANGES

4.1 Save as expressly modified in Clauses 1, 2, and 3 above, all other terms and conditions of the Original Agreement, including but not limited to the penal charge structure under Clause 6 of the Original Agreement, the prepayment terms under Clause 7.2, the grievance redressal mechanism under Clause 10, and the governing law under Clause 11, shall continue to apply unchanged.
