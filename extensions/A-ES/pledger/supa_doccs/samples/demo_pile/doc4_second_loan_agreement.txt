1. LOAN PARTICULARS

1.1 Sanctioned Loan Amount: INR 3,50,000 (Indian Rupees Three Lakh Fifty Thousand Only)

1.2 Purpose: Expansion of Borrower's hardware and building-materials retail shop, "Yadav Hardware Store," including purchase of additional inventory and a delivery two-wheeler.

1.3 Tenure: 18 (eighteen) months from date of disbursement.

1.4 Rate of Interest: 32.00% (Thirty-Two percent) per annum on a flat basis for the entire tenure, computed upfront on the full Sanctioned Amount and added to the Principal to determine the total repayable amount.

2. COMPUTATION OF TOTAL REPAYABLE AMOUNT

2.1 Interest for the full tenure, computed on flat basis: INR 3,50,000 x 32.00% x 1.5 years = INR 1,68,000

2.2 Total amount repayable over the tenure: INR 3,50,000 + INR 1,68,000 = INR 5,18,000

2.3 Monthly Instalment: INR 5,18,000 / 18 = INR 28,778 (rounded)

2.4 The Company's internal credit team confirms that the flat rate of 32% p.a. stated in Clause 1.4, when converted to an annualized reducing-balance equivalent for regulatory disclosure purposes, corresponds to an effective annualized rate of approximately 58.4% p.a. This conversion is recorded in the Company's internal loan-file worksheet (Form LF-9) but is not separately disclosed to the Borrower in this Agreement or in any accompanying document provided to him at signing.

3. CHARGES

3.1 A documentation and processing charge shall be recovered from the disbursement amount at the time of loan disbursal. The exact quantum of this charge is determined by the Regional Credit Head at the time of final sanction based on the Borrower's risk category and is reflected only in the internal disbursement voucher retained at the branch; it is not separately itemized or communicated to the Borrower in writing prior to disbursement.

3.2 A mandatory bundled insurance product ("Suraksha Cover Plus") is included in this loan, with the premium adjusted against the disbursement amount. Enrollment in this product is a precondition of loan sanction and is not optional for the Borrower.

4. LATE PAYMENT CHARGES

4.1 In the event of delay in payment of any monthly instalment beyond the due date, the Borrower shall be liable to pay a late payment charge calculated at 8% (eight percent) of the overdue instalment amount for each month of delay, in addition to continued accrual of interest on the outstanding balance at the contracted rate.

4.2 In addition to the late payment charge under Clause 4.1, a fixed cheque/NACH bounce charge of INR 500 per instance shall apply where a scheduled auto-debit fails, irrespective of the instalment amount.

5. SECURITY

5.1 The Borrower shall provide two guarantors acceptable to the Company. Mr. Deepak Yadav (brother of Borrower) and Mr. Prakash Wagh (business associate) have executed personal guarantees in favour of the Company, copies of which are annexed.

6. DEFAULT AND RECOVERY

6.1 On default of two consecutive instalments, the Company reserves the right to recall the entire outstanding amount and initiate recovery proceedings, including through its empanelled recovery agency, in accordance with applicable law.

7. GOVERNING LAW

7.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of India, with courts at Nashik having jurisdiction.
