NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 "Sanctioned Amount" means the Principal sum of INR 1,85,000 (Indian Rupees One Lakh Eighty-Five Thousand Only) sanctioned to the Borrower under this Agreement.

1.2 "Disbursement Date" means the date on which the Sanctioned Amount, or any part thereof, is credited to the Borrower's savings bank account.

1.3 "EMI" means the Equated Monthly Instalment payable by the Borrower as detailed in Schedule A.

1.4 "Tenure" means the period of 24 (twenty-four) months from the Disbursement Date within which the Loan is to be fully repaid.

1.5 "Rate of Interest" means the rate at which interest is charged on the outstanding Principal, computed on a reducing balance basis, as specified in Clause 3.

1.6 "Group" refers to the Joint Liability Group (JLG) "Saraswati Mahila Bachat Gat" of which the Borrower is a member, comprising 9 (nine) other members who have provided peer guarantee as per the Lender's group-lending methodology.

2. LOAN AMOUNT AND PURPOSE

2.1 The Lender agrees to lend and the Borrower agrees to borrow the Sanctioned Amount of INR 1,85,000 (Indian Rupees One Lakh Eighty-Five Thousand Only) for the specific purpose of purchasing two industrial sewing machines, fabric inventory, and working capital for the Borrower's tailoring business.

2.2 The Sanctioned Amount shall be disbursed in a single tranche within 7 (seven) working days of execution of this Agreement, subject to completion of all documentation and compliance with pre-disbursement conditions listed in Schedule C.

2.3 Any diversion of the loan proceeds for a purpose other than that stated in Clause 2.1 shall constitute an Event of Default under Clause 9.

3. INTEREST RATE AND ANNUAL PERCENTAGE RATE DISCLOSURE

3.1 The Loan shall carry interest at the rate of 26.00% (Twenty-Six percent) per annum, calculated on a reducing balance basis on the outstanding Principal amount.

3.2 In compliance with the Reserve Bank of India's Master Direction on Regulation of Interest Rates by NBFC-MFIs, the Lender hereby discloses the Annual Percentage Rate (APR) applicable to this Loan, inclusive of interest and all other charges, as follows:

    (a) Interest component (reducing balance): 26.00% p.a.
    (b) Processing fee amortized over tenure (equivalent annualized impact): 1.15% p.a.
    (c) Insurance premium amortized (equivalent annualized impact): 0.65% p.a.
    (d) TOTAL ANNUAL PERCENTAGE RATE (APR): 27.80% p.a.

3.3 The Borrower confirms having been read out and explained the APR disclosure in Clause 3.2 in Marathi, her preferred language, by the Lender's field officer, Mr. Suresh Kadam (Employee ID: SMPL-2291), prior to signing this Agreement, and the same is acknowledged by her thumb impression / signature on the Fair Practices Code acknowledgment attached as Annexure I.

4. FEES AND CHARGES

4.1 Processing Fee: A one-time, non-refundable processing fee of INR 1,850 (equivalent to 1% of the Sanctioned Amount) shall be deducted from the disbursement amount. The Borrower acknowledges receipt of a fee breakup sheet at the time of loan sanction, disclosing this fee in writing prior to disbursement, in compliance with the Lender's Fair Practices Code.

4.2 Credit Life Insurance Premium: A one-time premium of INR 1,200 towards a group credit-life insurance policy (Policy No. GCL-88213, underwritten by Bharat Suraksha Life Insurance Co. Ltd.) covering the outstanding loan amount in the event of the Borrower's death, shall be deducted from the disbursement amount. Insurance is optional; the Borrower has opted in via the separate Insurance Consent Form (Annexure II).

4.3 No charges other than those explicitly disclosed in this Clause 4 and in the APR disclosure at Clause 3.2 shall be levied on the Borrower, and any such additional charge, if introduced in future, shall require fresh written consent from the Borrower.

4.4 Stamp duty and documentation charges, if any, as applicable under Maharashtra state law, shall be borne by the Lender.

5. REPAYMENT SCHEDULE

5.1 The Loan, together with interest thereon, shall be repaid in 24 (twenty-four) equal monthly instalments of INR 8,976 (Indian Rupees Eight Thousand Nine Hundred Seventy-Six Only) each, as per the amortization schedule set out in Schedule A to this Agreement.

5.2 The first EMI shall fall due on the 14th of April 2024, and subsequent EMIs shall fall due on the 14th day of every succeeding month until the Loan is fully repaid.

5.3 Repayment shall be made through the Lender's designated collection mechanism, being either (a) cash collection at the fortnightly Group meeting by the field officer against an official receipt, or (b) auto-debit from the Borrower's linked bank account via National Automated Clearing House (NACH) mandate, at the Borrower's election. The Borrower has elected option (a) for the first 6 months and option (b) thereafter, as recorded in Annexure III.

6. LATE PAYMENT AND PENAL CHARGES

6.1 In the event any EMI is not paid on or before its due date, a penal charge of 2% (two percent) per month, or part thereof, shall be levied on the overdue instalment amount only (and not on the entire outstanding Principal), calculated from the due date until the date of actual payment.

6.2 The maximum penal charge applicable in respect of any single overdue instalment shall in no circumstance exceed 5% (five percent) of the outstanding amount of that instalment, in accordance with the Lender's Board-approved policy on penal charges and applicable RBI guidelines.

6.3 Penal charges shall be reasonable and commensurate with the level of default, shall not be capitalized (i.e., no further interest shall be computed on penal charges), and shall be clearly disclosed to the Borrower in the monthly repayment statement referred to in Clause 8.

7. MORATORIUM AND PREPAYMENT

7.1 No moratorium period is applicable to this Loan as of the date of this Agreement, save and except as may be granted in writing by the Lender pursuant to a written request from the Borrower under circumstances of genuine hardship, subject to a separate Moratorium Addendum being executed by both parties.

7.2 The Borrower may prepay the entire outstanding Loan amount at any time after completion of 6 (six) EMIs, without any prepayment penalty, in accordance with RBI's guidelines prohibiting foreclosure charges on floating-rate loans to individual/micro borrowers.

8. STATEMENTS AND DISCLOSURES

8.1 The Lender shall provide the Borrower with a monthly repayment statement reflecting the EMI paid, principal and interest breakup, outstanding Principal balance, any penal charges levied, and the effective interest rate applicable for that period.

8.2 The Borrower has the right to obtain a loan card, in the local language, containing all details of the loan, including the effective rate of interest charged, as mandated under the RBI Fair Practices Code for NBFC-MFIs.

9. EVENTS OF DEFAULT

9.1 The occurrence of any of the following shall constitute an "Event of Default": (a) non-payment of any two consecutive EMIs; (b) diversion of loan proceeds contrary to Clause 2.1; (c) any material misrepresentation made by the Borrower in the loan application; (d) the Borrower's insolvency or death (subject to the credit-life insurance proceeds under Clause 4.2 being applied towards outstanding dues).

9.2 Upon occurrence of an Event of Default, the Lender may, after issuing a written notice of 15 (fifteen) days to the Borrower and affording an opportunity to cure the default, declare the entire outstanding amount due and payable, without prejudice to the peer-guarantee mechanism of the Group under Clause 1.6.

10. GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL

10.1 Any grievance regarding this Loan or its terms may be addressed to the Lender's Grievance Redressal Officer at grievance@suryodayamfi.example.in or by calling the toll-free number 1800-XXX-4521, and shall be resolved within 30 days. Unresolved grievances may be escalated to the RBI Ombudsman for NBFCs.

11.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and courts at Pune, Maharashtra shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any disputes arising herefrom, subject to the Lender's right to pursue recovery through any other legally permissible forum including Lok Adalat.
