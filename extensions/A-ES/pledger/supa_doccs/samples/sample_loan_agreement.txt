SECTION 3: SECURITY AND COLLATERAL

SECTION 4: PENAL PROVISIONS

4.1 Late Payment Penalty: 2% per month on the overdue EMI amount.
    Grace period: 5 calendar days from EMI due date.

4.2 Cheque Bounce Charges: INR 750 per instance plus applicable bank charges.

4.3 Penal Interest on Overdue Principal: Base Rate (22.5%) + 6% per annum = 28.5% p.a.
    Applied on the overdue portion only from the date of default.

4.4 Legal Recovery Charges: Actual costs incurred, to be borne by the Borrower
    in case of initiation of recovery proceedings under SARFAESI Act or civil suit.

---

SECTION 5: PREPAYMENT AND FORECLOSURE

5.1 Prepayment Lock-in Period: First 12 months from disbursement date.
    No prepayment (partial or full) permitted during lock-in.

5.2 Prepayment Penalty (after lock-in):
    - Months 13-24: 4% of prepaid amount
    - Months 25-36: 2% of prepaid amount
    - Months 37-48: NIL (no penalty)

5.3 Minimum prepayment amount: INR 25,000 or 3 EMIs (whichever is higher)

5.4 Foreclosure (full prepayment): Subject to prepayment penalty as per 5.2
    plus administrative charges of INR 2,000.

---

SECTION 6: DEFAULT AND ACCELERATION

6.1 Events of Default:
    a) Non-payment of 3 consecutive EMIs
    b) Misrepresentation of income or KYC details
    c) Insolvency or bankruptcy proceedings against Borrower
    d) Material adverse change in Borrower's financial condition
    e) Breach of any covenant under this agreement

6.2 Upon occurrence of an Event of Default, the Lender may:
    a) Declare the entire outstanding loan (principal + accrued interest) immediately
       due and payable (acceleration clause)
    b) Adjust any deposits or credit balances held by the Lender
    c) Initiate legal proceedings for recovery
    d) Report the default to credit bureaus (CIBIL, Equifax, Experian, CRIF)

6.3 Cure Period: The Borrower shall have 30 days from written notice of default
    to remedy the breach before acceleration takes effect.

---

SECTION 7: MORATORIUM AND RESTRUCTURING

7.1 Moratorium: Not applicable under standard terms.

7.2 Restructuring: The Lender may, at its sole discretion, offer restructuring
    of the loan under the following circumstances:
    a) Documented medical emergency affecting Borrower's earning capacity
    b) Natural disaster affecting Borrower's business/residence
    c) Government-mandated lockdown exceeding 30 days

7.3 Restructuring, if granted, may involve:
    a) Extension of tenure by up to 12 months
    b) Temporary reduction of EMI for up to 6 months
    c) Capitalization of accrued interest (no waiver)
