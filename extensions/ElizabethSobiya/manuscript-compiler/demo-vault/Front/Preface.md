# Preface

I began this book because of a footpath that did not exist.

It was drawn on a sheet I had trusted for eleven years, a solid dashed line running
north from a farm gate across two fields and into a wood. I had walked past that gate
perhaps forty times. In 2019 I finally went through it, and found a hedge, then
another hedge, then a farmer who told me, with the patience of a man who has said it
before, that there had never been a path there in his lifetime or his father's.

The path was a mistake. Not a lie, not a trap, not a joke: a mistake, made in 1971 by
a surveyor who was tired [@barrow1971], copied forward through four revisions because
nobody had a reason to doubt it, and finally removed in 2021 without ceremony.

What follows is seven maps and one argument. The argument is that a map's errors are
not noise around a signal. They are the most honest record we have of how the map was
made, by whom, under what pressure, and with what left out.
