# What the Survey Missed

This chapter is a list, and I have tried to make it a boring one.

Between 1971 and 2019 the Northern Survey area was walked in full exactly twice. The
gap between those two walks contains everything this book has been circling.

## The tally

Of 412 features on Sheet 4 that the 2019 resurvey checked against the ground:

| Category | Count | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Confirmed as drawn | 351 | No action |
| Position corrected under 25 m | 38 | Within tolerance for the series |
| Position corrected over 25 m | 14 | Reported |
| Present on ground, absent on map | 6 | Added |
| Present on map, absent on ground | 3 | Removed, including the Cowley Bottom path |

![[Figures/resurvey-track.png|GPS track of the 2019 resurvey across the interior of Sheet 4.]]

Three features in fifty years. It is a very small number, and I want to be careful
about what it does and does not show.

It does not show that the map was 99.3 per cent correct. It shows that of the features
somebody thought worth checking, three were wrong. The 340 square kilometres nobody
entered in 1971 were not checked in 2019 either, because the resurvey followed the
existing features, and you cannot check a feature that was never drawn.^[This is the
same selection effect that makes any audit of a record look better than the record is:
you audit what is written down.]

The resurvey track in [[Figures/resurvey-track.png]] is, in its own way, another blank
interior.
