# Mean-value lab note (synthetic)

## Abstract

We record a toy sample mean for a simulated time series collected under laboratory conditions that do not correspond to any real experiment. This paragraph is slightly wordy on purpose so SuperDocs can tighten the prose without touching the estimator in Methods.

## Methods

Let the sample be \( x_1, \ldots, x_n \). The sample mean is

$$
\bar{x} = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i
$$

No other estimators are used. The equation above is the only estimator in this note.
