Proposal for Regional Emergency Response Partnership

About Northwind Relief Alliance

Northwind Relief Alliance fields a team of twelve hundred staff members across its programmes, working alongside local partners to deliver rapid and sustained relief.

Our teams operate in fourteen nations, delivering programs to local partners and communities affected by crisis.

Track Record

We've reached 3.2m people to date through our relief and recovery programmes, building lasting partnerships in every region we serve.

Budget Overview

The proposed initiative includes a $3.2 million budget for regional program expansion, covering staffing, logistics, and monitoring costs.
