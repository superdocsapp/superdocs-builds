Northwind Relief Alliance — Annual Report Summary

Who We Are

Northwind Relief Alliance is a global humanitarian organisation working across 14 countries to deliver emergency relief and long-term recovery programmes.

Our People

Northwind Relief Alliance employs 1,200 permanent staff dedicated to programme delivery, logistics, and community engagement.

Our Impact

In the most recent reporting period, Northwind Relief Alliance reached 3.2 million beneficiaries through its emergency and recovery programmes.

Quality and Certification

Northwind Relief Alliance's quality management system was renewed in March 2023 under ISO 9001.
