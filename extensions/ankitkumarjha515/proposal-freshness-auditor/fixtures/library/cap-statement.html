Northwind Relief Alliance — Capability Statement

Organisational Overview

Northwind Relief Alliance is a humanitarian relief and development organisation delivering emergency response, recovery, and long-term development programmes. The organisation currently delivers programmes across 17 countries of operation, coordinating with local partners to reach communities where support is needed most.
