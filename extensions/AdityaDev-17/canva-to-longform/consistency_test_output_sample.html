Company Overview

Our Sourcing Story

Our coffee journey begins in the high-altitude, shade-grown forests where we partner directly with small-scale farmers who share our commitment to regenerative agriculture. Every bean is hand-selected to ensure optimal quality and flavor profile, reflecting the unique characteristics of the soil and climate in which it was grown. By prioritizing ethical partnerships and fair-trade practices, we ensure that every batch of ROOTED Cold Brew supports both the environment and the farming communities that fuel our trail-ready ambition.

ROOTED Cold Brew was founded at the intersection of rugged endurance and artisanal craft. We believe that athletes pushing their limits on the trail deserve a fuel that is as pure and persistent as their ambition. ROOTED provides clean, high-performance energy derived from ethically sourced, shade-grown coffee beans, designed specifically for the unique metabolic and endurance needs of trail runners.

Our commitment is simple: we eliminate the synthetic jitters and sugar-crashes common in sports supplements, replacing them with a steady, sustained flow of natural caffeine and antioxidants.

Mission Statement

To fuel the world's most demanding runners with the clean, natural energy they need to explore further and run longer.

Core Brand Values

  • Authenticity: We honor the wild spaces we run in.
  • Persistence: Like the trail, we never quit.
  • Purity: No synthetics, no fillers, just coffee.
