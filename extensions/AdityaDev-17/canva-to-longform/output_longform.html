Product Overview

ROOTED is more than just coffee; it’s a high-performance energy solution crafted specifically for those who thrive in the wild. Our small-batch, slow-steeped cold brew provides clean, sustained energy without the jitters or crash associated with synthetic boosters. Designed for the trail, the mountain, and everything in between.

Why Trail Runners Choose ROOTED

  • Sustained Energy: Naturally occurring caffeine released slowly to power long endurance runs.
  • Clean Ingredients: Organic beans, pristine mountain water. No sugars, no additives, no compromises.
  • Portable & Rugged: Convenient, lightweight packaging designed to fit in any hydration vest or trail pack.
  • Recovery-Friendly: Packed with natural antioxidants to help combat oxidative stress post-run.
