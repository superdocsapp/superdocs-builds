ROOTED Cold Brew Brand Document

Company Overview

ROOTED Cold Brew is a performance-focused coffee company born on the trails. We believe in the power of natural energy to fuel human endurance. Founded by a team of ultra-marathoners and coffee enthusiasts, ROOTED was created to address a specific gap in the market: the need for a clean, high-performance energy source that fits seamlessly into the active lifestyles of trail runners, hikers, and outdoor adventurers.

Our Sourcing Story

Our journey begins in the high-altitude volcanic soils of the Huila region, where the air is thin, the mornings are crisp, and every bean is nurtured by the same elements that fuel your toughest runs. We don't just source coffee; we partner with small-batch growers who share our commitment to clean, sustainable agriculture. By prioritizing regenerative farming practices, we ensure that every cup of ROOTED Cold Brew supports not only your daily endurance but also the vitality of the earth it comes from. Every harvest is hand-picked at peak ripeness, ensuring the clean energy boost you rely on starts with the highest-quality origin.

Our cold brew is crafted from 100% organic, shade-grown Arabica beans, steeped for 24 hours to achieve a smooth, low-acid profile that is gentle on the stomach—essential for athletes on the move. We utilize a proprietary extraction process that preserves the natural caffeine and antioxidant content without the additives, sugars, or synthetic stimulants found in traditional energy drinks.

At ROOTED, we are committed to sustainability and the trails we run. Our packaging is fully compostable, and we partner with trail conservation initiatives to ensure the spaces where we train remain pristine for generations. ROOTED is more than just coffee; it is a movement to reconnect with the wild, fuel with intention, and push beyond the perceived limits of human endurance.

Our Sourcing Story

At ROOTED, our journey begins in the high-altitude cloud forests of the Huila region, where we build direct-trade relationships with smallholder farmers. By bypassing traditional commodity markets, we ensure that every bean is traceable to a specific plot of land and that every farmer receives a premium well above fair-trade minimums. This transparency allows us to select only the highest-quality harvests, ensuring a flavor profile that is as consistent as it is exceptional.

Sustainability Commitment

ROOTED is dedicated to minimizing our environmental footprint from soil to cup. We partner with cooperatives that utilize regenerative farming techniques to protect biodiversity in the Huila region. Our packaging is 100% compostable, and we operate on a carbon-neutral shipping model, ensuring that every run is fueled by coffee that respects the planet as much as it respects your performance.

Sustainability Commitment

At ROOTED, our dedication to the environment is as strong as our brew. We are committed to a sustainable supply chain that honors the landscapes our customers explore. Our initiatives include sourcing 100% organic beans from rain-forest-friendly cooperatives, utilizing compostable packaging for all retail shipments, and maintaining a carbon-neutral production facility. As of August 17, 2026, we have pledged to donate a portion of all proceeds to trail restoration and land conservation projects.

Retail Partner Program

ROOTED is expanding our footprint by partnering with premium outdoor retailers and gyms that share our commitment to clean energy and adventure. Our partner program offers specialized inventory support, co-branded marketing materials, and dedicated training for staff on our performance-focused cold brew. We prioritize partners who curate products for trail runners and active lifestyles.
