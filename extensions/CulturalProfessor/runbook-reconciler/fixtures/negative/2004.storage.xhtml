When this fires

The LabelPrinterQueueDepth alert fires when the queue holds more than 500 jobs for 10 minutes.

Background

The label printers are a ThermalTransferPrinter fleet behind a QueueDepthCollector sidecar. Neither of those is an alert; they are the names of things in the vendor's own documentation, and a checker that reads every CamelCase word as an alert name will have opinions about both.

Historically we ran 3 printers per depot and the queue drained in under 4 minutes. Those two numbers describe the world, not any threshold, and there is nothing in the estate for them to disagree with.

Triage

The deployment is label-printer in namespace fulfilment and runs 3 replicas.

Escalation for this service sits with the Fulfilment rota, and the head of operations picks it up after 20 minutes.

Mitigation

Run the deploy-production job once the backlog is drained.
