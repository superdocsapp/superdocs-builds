When this fires

The ManifestWorkerStalled alert fires when no batch has completed for 30 minutes.

Triage

The deployment is manifest-worker in namespace fulfilment and runs 2 replicas.

bash

A stalled worker is nearly always the broker's endpoint rejecting a malformed consignment. The batch stops rather than skipping, on purpose.

Mitigation

Fix or remove the offending consignment, then run the replay-failed-batch job in the deploy workflow.

Escalation

Page the Fulfilment rota, then the head of operations after 20 minutes.
