Scope

Recovery of the payments datastore, meridian-payments-prod, in region eu-west-1. The instance is multi-AZ, so a single availability zone failure is not a disaster recovery event.

Objectives and retention

Measure

Committed value

RPO

15 minutes

RTO

4 hours

Automated backup retention

7 days

Snapshot window

03:00 UTC daily

Restore procedure

Restore from the most recent automated backup into a new instance, then repoint the application. Never restore over the running instance.

bash

Archived manifests

Consignment manifests are archived to meridian-manifest-archive and expire after 90 days. Anything older than that is not recoverable and the customs broker's own copy is the record.

Escalation

Declare through the Payments Core rota. The head of platform owns the decision to fail over.
