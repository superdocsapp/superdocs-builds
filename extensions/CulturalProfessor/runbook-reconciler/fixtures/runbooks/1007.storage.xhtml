Objectives

These are the committed objectives for payments-api. The alerting rules are derived from them, so the two are supposed to move together.

Latency

p99 request latency stays below 800ms. The paging alert is PaymentsApiHighLatency and fires after 10 minutes above the objective.

Availability

The 5xx rate stays below 5% over any 30 minute window. Sustained burn above that is the PaymentsApiErrorBudgetBurn alert.

Ownership

Owned by team-payments. Tier 1: this service is on the critical path for every shipment booked.
