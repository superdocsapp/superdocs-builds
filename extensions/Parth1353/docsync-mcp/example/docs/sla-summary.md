# Service level summary

*Synthetic example document for the docsync extension. Not any real company's
service level agreement.*

This is the customer-facing summary. The contractual language lives in the
master services agreement; where the two disagree, the agreement wins.

## Availability

The provisioning service is contracted at 99.95% availability, measured
monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least five working days
in advance.

## Response times

| Severity | Definition | Response |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Service unavailable, or provisioning stopped entirely | 15 minutes |
| P2 | Degraded, with a workaround | 60 minutes |

Response means a named engineer has picked the incident up, not that it has
been resolved.
