# Launch Plan: Architecture Handover Pack (Extra Credit Build)

## 1. The Target Community
**Boutique AEC Firms (Architecture, Engineering, Construction)**
Specifically, Principal Architects and Technical Project Managers at mid-sized studios. These professionals manage extremely complex, high-stakes documents where a single parameter drift (e.g., changing a material spec but forgetting to update the budget) can result in a massive liability lawsuit or contractor dispute.

## 2. The Hook and Positioning
**The Pain Point:** Legacy AEC industries are slow to adopt generalized AI chatbots because they don't integrate into their complex document workflows. 
**The Hook:** *"Last-minute material changes cause 40% of contractor disputes. Here is the Pre-Flight Handover Audit framework we use to catch material drift before sending PDFs to the General Contractor."*
**The Strategy:** We do not market SuperDocs as a generic AI. We market the *resource* (the Handover Pack) as a liability-prevention tool. 

## 3. Where It Gets Introduced (Channels)
*   **LinkedIn AEC Groups:** Highly professional, liability-conscious discussions.
*   **ArchDaily Forums:** Technical discussions on material sourcing and project management.
*   **Procore / Autodesk Community Boards:** Where technical PMs complain about workflow friction.

## 4. The Execution (Rules of Restraint)
*   **What is said:** "I built this checklist to prevent material mismatch lawsuits. You can use it manually, or if you drop it into SuperDocs, the AI will audit the 3 sections for you."
*   **What is unsaid:** We will not aggressively shill SuperDocs features, pricing, or "AI hype." 
*   **Affiliation Disclosure:** Clearly stated at the top of the resource: *"Created by Rohit. Built using SuperDocs."*

## 5. Follow-Up Plan for Questions
When community members ask, *"How does the automated sync prompt work?"*, the response will be:
*"The prompt at the bottom of the template uses a document-native AI editor called SuperDocs. You highlight the three sections of your handover pack, run the prompt, and the AI flags any material discrepancies between your design narrative and your budget. You can use the checklist without the AI, but the automation saves about 5 hours of manual proofreading."*
