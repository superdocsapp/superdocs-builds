ACME AI — LEGAL AND SECURITY REVIEW

Corporate Structure:
Acme AI Private Limited is incorporated in India.

Material Litigation:
The company is not currently involved in any material litigation.

Intellectual Property:
The company owns the source code for its primary software platform.
Employee IP agreements are signed by all full-time engineering employees.

Security:
The company completed a SOC 2 Type I audit in October 2025.
SOC 2 Type II certification has not yet been completed.
The company uses encryption at rest and in transit.
Customer data is logically isolated between customer accounts.

Known Security Issue:
A minor authentication vulnerability was identified during an internal security review in November 2025. The issue was patched within seven days and no customer data exposure was identified.

Privacy:
The company maintains a GDPR data processing policy for applicable customers.
