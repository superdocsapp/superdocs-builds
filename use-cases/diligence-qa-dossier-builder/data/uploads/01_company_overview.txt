ACME AI PRIVATE LIMITED — COMPANY OVERVIEW

Company Name: Acme AI Private Limited
Founded: 2021
Headquarters: Bengaluru, India
Business: AI-powered workflow automation software for mid-market companies.

Founders:
- Rahul Mehta — Co-Founder & CEO
- Priya Sharma — Co-Founder & CTO

Employees: 86 as of December 2025.
The company operates as a B2B SaaS business and sells annual and monthly subscriptions.
As of December 2025, Acme AI had 120 paying customers.
The company reported Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) of $4.2 million for FY2025.
FY2025 revenue was $3.8 million.
The company raised a Series A financing round of $8 million in March 2025.
The lead investor was Northstar Ventures.
The company has offices in Bengaluru and Singapore.
The company does not currently operate offices outside India and Singapore.
