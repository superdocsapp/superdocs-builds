# Task 3: SuperDocs Core Use Cases

This document lists 10 realistic use cases where SuperDocs can automate due diligence, compliance, and auditing workflows.

---

### 1. Venture Capital Startup Diligence
* **Target User**: Venture Capital investment associates.
* **Problem**: Reviewing hundreds of pitch decks, financial sheets, and legal agreements to verify founder claims.
* **Automation**: SuperDocs ingests the startup data room, matches claims against actual documents, and automatically flags financial conflicts (e.g. ARR claims in pitch deck vs bank statement).

### 2. Legal Merger & Acquisition (M&A) Audit
* **Target User**: Corporate lawyers and M&A advisory firms.
* **Problem**: Cross-referencing warranties, change-of-control clauses, and liability limits in hundreds of client agreements.
* **Automation**: SuperDocs maps legal risk concepts, retrieves contract terms, and lists compliance gaps (e.g. contracts lacking standard security clauses).

### 3. SOC 2 & ISO 27001 Compliance Audit
* **Target User**: Internal security compliance officers.
* **Problem**: Auditing security policies, disaster recovery plans, and penetration test reports to verify adherence to compliance frameworks.
* **Automation**: SuperDocs checks compliance questions, cross-references files, and flags evidence gaps (e.g. missing employee training logs).

### 4. Technical Due Diligence
* **Target User**: CTOs or technical consultants auditing a target acquisition.
* **Problem**: Evaluating open-source license risks, architectural decisions, and dependency vulnerabilities across technical readmes and reports.
* **Automation**: SuperDocs scans technical documents, flags license conflicts, and highlights key operational/infrastructure risks.

### 5. Private Equity Portfolio Health Check
* **Target User**: Portfolio operations managers.
* **Problem**: Compiling and verifying quarterly financial performance, headcount metrics, and gross margins across portfolio companies.
* **Automation**: SuperDocs parses mixed-format reports, extracts key metrics, and flags operational deviations or reporting discrepancies.

### 6. Loan Underwriting Commercial Audits
* **Target User**: Bank underwriters and credit risk officers.
* **Problem**: Auditing corporate tax filings, balance sheets, and cap tables to check debt-to-equity ratio calculations.
* **Automation**: SuperDocs extracts and verifies metrics, cross-references equity statements, and identifies mathematical or structural conflicts.

### 7. Intellectual Property (IP) Freedom-to-Operate Reviews
* **Target User**: Patent attorneys.
* **Problem**: Cross-referencing patent filings, search reports, and invention disclosures.
* **Automation**: SuperDocs retrieves patent citations, checks claims alignment, and identifies potential coverage gaps.

### 8. Vendor Risk Assessment (Vendor Diligence)
* **Target User**: Procurement managers.
* **Problem**: Checking vendor security assessments, SLA guarantees, and liability limits before onboarding.
* **Automation**: SuperDocs extracts SLA targets, flags missing security certifications, and verifies service liability limits.

### 9. SEC Form 10-K Filing Verification
* **Target User**: Financial auditors.
* **Problem**: Verifying public financial claims in annual reports against internal spreadsheets and ledger exports.
* **Automation**: SuperDocs matches public reporting clauses against internal raw financials, flagging accounting conflicts.

### 10. Franchise Agreement Compliance Checks
* **Target User**: Franchise operations managers.
* **Problem**: Auditing franchisee reports, lease documents, and royalty statements to verify compliance with brand guidelines.
* **Automation**: SuperDocs scans franchisee submissions, cross-references lease dates, and calculates royalty margins, flagging non-compliance issues.
