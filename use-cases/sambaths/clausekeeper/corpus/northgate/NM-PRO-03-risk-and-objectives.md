# Procedure NM-PRO-03: Risk Management and Quality Objectives

Document code: NM-PRO-03 · Revision B · Owner: Director of Quality · Status: Approved

## 1 Risk register

QA maintains the standing risk register covering process, supply, staffing, and compliance risks. Each entry records the issue, likelihood and severity on a 5×5 scale, existing controls, and residual rating. The register is reviewed at the monthly quality business meeting; new entries may be raised by anyone through the improvement intake form.

## 2 Risk treatment

Every high or medium residual risk carries a treatment plan with an owner and due date. Treatments can be controls added to a procedure, monitoring, or accepted-with-rationale entries signed by the Director of Quality. Closure requires evidence that the control is operating (for example, two clean inspection cycles after adding a check).

## 3 Opportunities

Improvement opportunities identified from scorecard trends, suggestion cards, and management review are logged alongside risks with the same owner/due-date discipline. An opportunity becomes a planned action when it supports a quality objective.

## 4 Quality objectives

Annual objectives are set at the January management review. Current cycle:

| Objective | Measure | Target | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reduce final-inspection escapes | Escapes per inspection sample | < 0.5% | QA Manager |
| Improve delivery reliability | On-time delivery | ≥ 96% | Operations Manager |
| Cut complaint response time | Days to first response | ≤ 5 days | QA Manager |

Progress is charted monthly on the scorecard; any objective off-track for two months triggers a recovery plan owned by the objective owner.

## 5 Records

Register snapshots, treatment plans, acceptance rationales, and objective trend charts are quality records retained per NM-QM-01.
