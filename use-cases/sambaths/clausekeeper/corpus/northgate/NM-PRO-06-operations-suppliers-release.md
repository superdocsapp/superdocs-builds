# Procedure NM-PRO-06: Operations, Suppliers and Release

Document code: NM-PRO-06 · Revision E · Owner: Operations Manager · Status: Approved

## 1 Operational planning

Weekly production plans set lot sizes, line assignments, and staffing against the demand schedule. Each work order carries the released traveler listing required materials, in-process checks, and equipment calibration status. Lines may not start a work order with an expired calibration or an unreleased document revision.

## 2 Order requirements review

Before order confirmation, the account manager and Operations review the customer requirements: specifications, labeling, delivery date, and any statutory constraints for the destination market. Conflicts (specification vs. capacity, special packaging) are resolved with the customer before commitment; the reviewed requirement set is attached to the work order.

## 3 Supplier selection and evaluation

Suppliers of components affecting product quality are qualified before first order: quality system questionnaire, sample evaluation against drawing requirements, and where warranted on-site assessment. Qualified suppliers appear on the approved vendor list with their rating. Scorecards run quarterly using incoming PPM, on-time performance, and NCR history; two consecutive failing quarters trigger corrective action request or removal from the list. Contract sterilization is controlled as an externally provided process with annual assessment and batch conformance-record review before release.

## 4 Purchasing controls

Purchase orders reference drawing revision and specification codes explicitly; verbal changes are prohibited. Incoming inspection samples per the sampling plan by component criticality; supplier conformance records are verified against PO and lot. Nonconforming receipts follow NM-PRO-04.

## 5 Production control and traceability

Assembly follows standard work at controlled stations. Lot traceability links device lots to component lots, operators, equipment, and inspection results through the MES record. Product is protected through handling, storage in monitored areas, and preservation rules for moisture-sensitive components.

## 6 Release

No lot ships until final inspection passes all acceptance criteria and the QA inspector — or the QA Manager as escalation authority — signs the release record. Release records show the lot number, inspection results, signer identity, and date. Held or quarantined product cannot be picked for shipment while its hold status stands.
