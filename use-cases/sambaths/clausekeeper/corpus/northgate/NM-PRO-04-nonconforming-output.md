# Procedure NM-PRO-04: Nonconforming Output and Corrective Action

Document code: NM-PRO-04 · Revision F · Owner: QA Manager · Status: Approved

## 1 Identifying and containing nonconforming output

Any employee who finds suspect product (wrong component, failed test, damaged packaging, unlabeled lot) immediately tags it with a red HOLD label, moves it to the locked quarantine cage in MES as quantity on hold, and raises an NCR in the quality system the same shift. The QA inspector verifies containment before end of day; quarantine access is limited to QA.

## 2 Investigation and classification

QA classifies each NCR within two business days: use-as-is (requires documented justification and customer concession where applicable), rework per an approved instruction, return to supplier, or scrap. Classification records who decided and on what evidence. Suspect product already shipped triggers the complaint-and-recall route instead of the standard NCR flow.

## 3 Disposition of nonconforming devices

Dispositions are decided by the QA Manager or deputized approver listed on the signature sheet:

- Use-as-is requires engineering evaluation showing the deviation does not affect fit, function, or safety, plus written customer concession when contractually required.
- Rework follows a routed, approved rework instruction; reworked units repeat the full final inspection, never a shortened path.
- Scrap is witnessed by two people and recorded with quantity and reason; scrap cages are reconciled weekly.
- Return-to-supplier entries feed the supplier scorecard in NM-PRO-06.

Every disposition entry captures the NCR number, quantity affected, decision maker, date, and disposition evidence attachments.

## 4 Corrective action

NCRs meeting any trigger — repeat occurrence within 90 days, customer complaint linkage, escaped defect found downstream, or regulatory reportability — open a corrective action. The owner performs root-cause analysis using five-why or fishbone methods documented in the CAPA record, defines corrective actions addressing the cause (not the symptom), and states how effectiveness will be verified. Effectiveness checks run no sooner than 30 days after implementation and require objective evidence (metric movement, verification result) before closure. Failed effectiveness checks reopen the CAPA with escalated ownership.

## 5 Feeding the system

CAPA lessons update procedures, training content, and the risk register where warranted; the change-management route in NM-PRO-02 carries any document changes.
