# Northgate Medical Devices Quality Manual

Document code: NM-QM-01 · Revision C · Owner: Director of Quality · Status: Approved

Northgate Medical Devices (fictional demo company) manufactures Class II patient-monitoring accessories at its Riverbend site.

## 1 Organizational context

Riverbend leadership reviews the internal and external issues that shape our quality system twice a year: regulatory changes in our markets, supplier concentration on single-source electronic assemblies, staffing depth in final inspection, and evolving customer reporting expectations from hospital networks. The review output is an issues register that feeds risk planning and management review.

## 2 Interested parties

We track four groups and what they need from us:

| Party | Requirement we manage | How monitored |
|---|---|---|
| Hospital customers | Delivery reliability, complaint response within 5 business days | Account reviews, complaint metrics |
| Regulators (FDA, notified body) | Registration, MDR/AER obligations | Regulatory affairs calendar |
| Employees | Safe workplace, clear work instructions | HR surveys, safety board |
| Suppliers | Forecast visibility, payment terms | Supplier scorecards |

## 3 Scope of the quality management system

The scope covers the design and manufacture of reusable patient-monitoring cables, leads, and sensors at the Riverbend site. Contract sterilization is outsourced and controlled per NM-PRO-06. No exclusions are claimed; design is performed in-house under the oversight described in section 8.

## 4 Process map

Our system runs through five core process groups, each with a named owner and measures reviewed monthly:

1. Realization: order intake → purchasing → assembly → final inspection → release (owner: Operations Manager)
2. Control: document control, change management, nonconforming output, CAPA (owner: QA Manager)
3. People: hiring, training, competence verification (owner: HR Lead)
4. Assurance: monitoring metrics, assessment results, management review inputs (owner: Director of Quality)
5. Improvement: objective setting, improvement actions (owner: Director of Quality)

Process performance uses the measures listed in section 11; targets sit in the annual quality plan.

## 5 Leadership and commitment

The executive team holds a monthly quality business meeting where complaints, CAPA aging, and delivery metrics are standing agenda items. The Director of Quality has direct escalation access to the CEO and authority to stop shipments. Management provides budget for inspection equipment calibration and backfill for inspectors attending training.

## 6 Quality policy

Northgate builds monitoring accessories that clinicians can trust. We meet applicable regulatory requirements, keep our promises to customers, train every person to their procedure, and treat every defect report as a chance to improve. The policy is posted in production areas, covered in new-hire orientation, and re-signed by staff at each revision.

## 7 Roles, responsibilities and authority

- Director of Quality: owns the manual, approves procedures, chairs management review, holds stop-ship authority.
- QA Manager: owns inspection decisions, NCR disposition, supplier quality escalations.
- Operations Manager: owns production scheduling, line documentation adherence, tooling maintenance.
- HR Lead: owns training matrix execution and competence records.
- All staff: required to raise nonconformities immediately and follow released documents.

Deputized approvers are named on the signature list maintained by QA.

## 8 Design and development oversight

New cable and sensor designs follow the stage-gate described in the design plan template: requirements definition, architecture selection, prototype verification against the requirement set, process validation on pilot tooling, and a transfer review before routine production. Each gate requires QA sign-off; design changes after transfer enter the change-management route in NM-PRO-02. Design files, test reports, and gate minutes are retained in the design history file for each product family.

## 9 Monitoring and measurement

Monthly quality scorecards track first-pass yield, on-time delivery, complaint rate per thousand units shipped, CAPA closure age, and supplier PPM. Trend rules escalate any metric breaching its alert limit for two consecutive months into the corrective action system. Scorecards are produced by QA from the MES and complaint log and are standing inputs to the quality business meeting.

## 10 Management review

Management review runs quarterly with required inputs: scorecard trends, assessment program status, complaint and CAPA summaries, supplier performance, resource adequacy, and improvement progress. Minutes record decisions with owners and due dates; open actions carry into the next review until closed.

## 11 Continual improvement

Improvement candidates come from scorecard trends, CAPA root causes, shop-floor suggestion cards, and post-market feedback. Each quarter the executive team selects up to three improvement actions tied to quality objectives and resources them explicitly. Completed improvements are checked for effectiveness at the following quarter's review.
