# Procedure NM-PRO-05: Competence, Training and Awareness

Document code: NM-PRO-05 · Revision C · Owner: HR Lead · Status: Approved

## 1 Competence requirements

Every role with quality impact has a defined competence profile in the training matrix: required education or experience, procedure training, and any formal qualification records (for example, solder-inspection qualification for final inspectors). The matrix is owned by the HR Lead and reviewed with managers each quarter.

## 2 Training execution

New hires complete orientation (policy, quality system basics, their role's procedures) before unsupervised work. Procedure training uses read-and-discuss with a qualified trainer plus observed practice; sign-off requires the trainer's confirmation that the person performed the task correctly. Re-training triggers automatically from document change requests (affected roles list) and from CAPA actions that identify knowledge gaps.

## 3 Verification of effectiveness

Competence is verified, not assumed: line leads observe each operator against the standard work at least twice yearly using the observation checklist; inspectors pass an annual blind-sample test with a 95% threshold. Failed verifications pull the person from the task until re-trained and re-verified.

## 4 Awareness

Managers cover three awareness points in team meetings each quarter: why the quality policy matters to our customers, how each person's work affects product safety, and what happens when procedures are not followed (real anonymized examples from our own NCR log). New policy revisions are briefed within two weeks of release with acknowledgment recorded.

## 5 Records

Training files, observation checklists, blind-test results, and acknowledgments are retained per NM-QM-01 and are available for review on request.

## 6 Infrastructure and tools

Training rooms, ESD-safe assembly benches, torque tools, and the blind-sample inspection kits used for verifier testing are inventoried annually by the HR Lead together with Operations. Damaged or out-of-calibration training equipment is tagged and pulled from use until repaired or replaced, and replacement needs are raised as budget items at the quarterly management review.
