# Procedure NM-PRO-02: Document Control and Change Management

Document code: NM-PRO-02 · Revision D · Owner: QA Manager · Status: Approved

## 1 Purpose and system of record

All controlled documents live in the QMS document vault. Each document carries a document code, revision letter, owner, approval signatures, and effective date. Only the vault copy is authoritative; printed copies are stamped UNCONTROLLED and expire on print date.

## 2 Creation and approval

New documents are drafted by the owner in the standard template, checked by a second qualified reader for clarity, and approved electronically by the process owner plus QA. A document is not effective until both signatures are recorded and the vault shows the release date.

## 3 Version control and distribution

Revisions increment the letter suffix. Superseded versions are moved to the archive folder within one business day of release; controlled-distribution lists are updated so affected roles receive the new version. Watermarking prevents accidental use of archived copies.

## 4 Planning changes to documents or processes

Before any change to a released document or a production process is made, the requester completes a change request stating the purpose, what is affected downstream (forms, training, labels, supplier instructions), resource impact, and how effectiveness will be confirmed. The change board — QA, Operations, and the owning manager — approves or defers with reasons. Changes that alter inspection criteria require a re-validation check before release. Training assignments are generated automatically from the change request's "affected roles" list.

## 5 Retention and obsolescence

Quality records retain per the retention schedule (minimum: device history 7 years, training records employment + 5 years, design files life of product + 5). Annual spot checks confirm archive integrity.

## 6 Records of this procedure

Change requests, signature logs, distribution acknowledgments, and archive checks are quality records filed under the document code.
