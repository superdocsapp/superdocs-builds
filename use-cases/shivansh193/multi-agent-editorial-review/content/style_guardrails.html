Internal style checklist for reviewing the launch brief. Synthetic, for demonstration purposes only.

  1. Executive Summary length — must be 80 words or fewer. Flag and trim if longer.
  2. No passive voice in Technical Specifications — every sentence in that section must have an explicit, active-voice subject performing the action. Flag and rewrite any passive construction.
  3. Risk Assessment mitigations — every listed risk must end with an explicit mitigation sentence. Flag any risk that doesn't have one.
  4. Market Analysis sourcing — any market-size or share figure must be presented as a specific number, not a vague range. Flag any figure that isn't specific.
