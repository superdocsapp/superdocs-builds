Payment Terms

  • Opening ask: Net 60 days.
  • Walk-away ceiling: Net 30 days. Never agree to anything shorter than this.
  • Step size per round: 15 days.

Limitation of Liability

  • Opening ask: a cap equal to five (5) times fees paid in the preceding twelve months.
  • Walk-away ceiling: three (3) times fees paid in the preceding twelve months. Never agree to a cap lower than this.
  • Step size per round: one (1) times fees.
