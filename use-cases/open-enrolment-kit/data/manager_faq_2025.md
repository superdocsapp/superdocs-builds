# Manager FAQ: 2025 Open Enrolment

## When is open enrolment?
Open enrolment runs from November 1st to November 15th, 2024. All elections take effect on January 1, 2025.

## Have premiums increased?
For 2025, the Employee Only premium is $100/month and the Family premium is $300/month. 

## What should I tell employees about changes?
Remind employees that deductibles for health insurance are $1,000 for individuals. Dental max is $1,500.
