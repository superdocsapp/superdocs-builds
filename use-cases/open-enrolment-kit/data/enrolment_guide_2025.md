# 2025 Open Enrolment Guide

Welcome to the 2025 Open Enrolment period! This is your opportunity to review and update your benefits for the upcoming year.

## Health Insurance

We are proud to offer health insurance through BlueCross. 
- Individual Deductible: $1,000
- Family Deductible: $2,000
- Individual Out-of-Pocket Max: $3,000
- Premium (Employee Only): $100/month
- Premium (Family): $300/month

## Dental and Vision

- **Dental**: Provided by Delta Dental with an annual maximum benefit of $1,500.
- **Vision**: Provided by VSP with a standard $10 copay for exams.
