# Project memo on status report consolidation

## Current reporting burden

We examined our internal operations during 12 weeks of analysis ending on 13 August 2026. Teams currently produce three separate project updates every week. Project Managers spend 4 hours per week compiling overlapping metrics for senior leadership. This redundant effort costs approximately USD 45,000 annually across 12 active accounts. 

The current process creates fatigue among staff members. We noted low accuracy in the third update due to duplicate data entry. Project leaders ignore redundant fields because the metrics repeat across documents. We must streamline our communication channels immediately.

## Proposed consolidation plan

We propose replacing the existing reports with a single unified dashboard. The new format requires fewer inputs from operational teams. You will save 3 hours per week under this new structure. 

* Reduced administrative overhead
* Clearer metrics for stakeholders
* Faster decision-making cycles

Our recent study shows that single-source reporting improves data reliability by 35 percent (Vance, 2025). The transition requires minimal training for current staff members. We will test the unified format on four pilot accounts next month.

Please review this proposal and share your feedback by 20 August 2026. We will schedule a brief meeting to discuss implementation steps after you respond.
