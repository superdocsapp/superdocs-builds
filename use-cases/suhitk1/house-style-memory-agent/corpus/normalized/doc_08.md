# Internal staffing review for delivery pods

## Overview of findings

We examined allocation patterns across pod alpha and pod beta on 12 October 2026. The analysis highlights distinct operational gaps in our current resourcing model. Pod alpha currently operates at 95 percent capacity. Pod beta struggles with high burnout risks due to inefficient scheduling. 

Both teams manage excessive administrative overhead during active sprints. This administrative burden consumes 15 hours each week per consultant. Consequently, billable output falls short of our quarterly targets. We note similar challenges in earlier internal audits (Vance, 2025).

## Recommendations for resource allocation

You must rebalance workloads immediately to protect staff retention. We propose three structural changes to address these imbalances:

* Reallocate four senior analysts from pod alpha to pod beta
* Implement automated time tracking tools across all active projects
* Reduce weekly internal status meetings by 50 percent

These adjustments require an estimated budget of USD 4,500 for training. We expect full recovery of this investment within 3 months. 

Please review these proposals before our leadership meeting on 18 October 2026. We welcome your feedback on this operational strategy.
