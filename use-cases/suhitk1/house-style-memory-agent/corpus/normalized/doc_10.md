# Customer satisfaction survey results for support desk

## Executive summary

We analysed the recent customer feedback data for the client support desk on 12 August 2026. The evaluation covered 450 support tickets closed during the previous quarter. Overall satisfaction reached 82 percent across all client segments.

The assessment highlighted clear operational strengths alongside specific areas for improvement. Clients praised the technical competence of the agents. However, respondents reported frustration with initial wait times before reaching an advisor.

## Key metrics and analysis

The survey data shows a notable gap between resolution quality and speed. Advisors resolved technical issues accurately on the first contact in 88 percent of cases. Long queues—often exceeding 20 minutes—drove down the overall score.

- Slow initial response times
- High agent technical competence
- Clear communication during ticket resolution

We evaluated the financial impact of these delays using internal client models (Mercer, 2025). Prolonged queues risk increasing customer churn by 4 percent over the next 12 months.
