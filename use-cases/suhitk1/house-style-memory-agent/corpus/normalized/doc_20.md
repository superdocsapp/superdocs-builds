# Tooling overlap assessment across delivery teams

## Overview of findings

We examined our delivery operations on 14 October 2026 to identify redundant software licenses. Delivery teams currently maintain four distinct platforms for task management and client reporting. This fragmentation costs the firm USD 45,000 annually in excess subscription fees.

Teams duplicate effort because individual business units adopted separate tools before leadership standardized our core stack. Staff members switch between multiple interfaces daily to update project tracking boards. Such friction wastes roughly 4 hours per employee each week (Vance, 2024).

## Consolidation strategy

We recommend migrating all projects to a single enterprise workspace by the end of next quarter. This transition eliminates duplicate licensing costs and simplifies cross-team collaboration. You must review your current software subscriptions immediately.

Please complete the following actions before 1 November 2026:

* Audit active user accounts
* Export historical project data
* Cancel redundant software subscriptions

We will track migration progress through weekly status reports. Please contact the internal operations group if you encounter technical barriers during the transition.
