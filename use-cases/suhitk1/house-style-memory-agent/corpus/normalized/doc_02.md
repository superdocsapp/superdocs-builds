# Discovery sprint results for apex retail

## Overview of findings

We completed the two-week discovery sprint on 14 November 2026. The team analyzed current operations and identified three primary bottlenecks—supply chain delays, manual inventory audits, and legacy payment systems. These bottlenecks cost the business approximately USD 450,000 annually. 

Data shows that inventory discrepancies reach 12 percent across regional warehouses (Chen, 2024). Store managers spend 15 hours weekly reconciling stock levels by hand. Customers experience frequent checkout failures because the legacy payment software drops transactions during peak traffic hours. 

## Recommended next steps

We advise you to authorize a 12-week implementation phase to address these structural issues. This phase will modernize your technical infrastructure and streamline warehouse operations. The proposed scope includes the following workstreams:

- Automated inventory tracking systems
- Cloud-based payment gateway integration
- Supplier contract renegotiation protocols

We project a full return on investment within 9 months of deployment. Please review this report before our alignment call on 20 November 2026.
