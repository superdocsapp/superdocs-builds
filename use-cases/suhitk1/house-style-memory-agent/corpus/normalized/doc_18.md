# Project memo: updating client reporting cadences

## Proposed cadence changes

We evaluated our current client reporting schedules during the last 30 days. The data shows that weekly updates consume 15 percent of billable hours across active projects. We spent USD 45,000 on redundant status meetings last quarter alone. 

Moving to bi-weekly reports will reduce overhead costs significantly. Clients often ignore weekly emails because the incremental progress remains minimal. We can redirect those saved hours into core analysis and strategic advisory work.

## Implementation plan

You will test the new schedule with 12 selected accounts starting on 1 October 2026. This pilot program lasts for 90 days. We must measure client satisfaction scores closely during this period.

* Reduced administrative overhead for consulting staff
* Clearer milestones between major deliverable drops
* Higher engagement during scheduled strategic syncs

Internal feedback indicates that junior consultants welcome this shift (Smith, 2025). We will review the pilot metrics on 15 January 2027 and adjust the policy if necessary.
