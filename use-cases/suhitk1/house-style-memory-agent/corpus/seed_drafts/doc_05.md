# Review of delivery practice meeting load

## Findings and analysis

On 14 October 2026, we completed an internal audit of calendar data across the delivery practice. Consultants spent 24 hours per week in internal discussions during the prior quarter. This excessive schedule reduced billable output and lowered team morale. 

Client engagements generated significant value, but internal status updates drained critical capacity. We quantified the financial impact based on average billing rates. The lost productivity cost USD 4,500 per employee each month. 

## Action plan and next steps

We must streamline internal coordination immediately to protect our profit margins. Department leadership formulated three corrective actions to reduce meeting frequency. Please review these operational changes before our next leadership call.

- Implement mandatory meeting-free Fridays
- Reduce standard sync durations to 15 minutes
- Require clear agendas for every calendar invite

We expect these measures to reclaim 10 hours of weekly billable time per consultant. Previous research confirms that schedule interventions improve overall employee retention (Smith, 2024). 

Please direct your questions regarding these schedule adjustments to the operations group. We appreciate your cooperation as we optimize our daily delivery workflows.
