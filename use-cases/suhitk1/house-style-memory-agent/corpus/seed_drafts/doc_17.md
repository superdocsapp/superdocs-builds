# Estimation accuracy workshop results

## Overview of findings

We held our internal estimation workshop on 14 August 2026. The leadership team examined data from 35 recent engagements across 3 industry sectors. We found systemic variance between initial proposals and final project costs.

Project managers routinely underestimated scope creep during the discovery phase. This miscalculation inflated average delivery budgets by 18 percent. Furthermore, senior partners missed critical technical dependencies in 12 client portfolios (Vance, 2024).

Our pricing models currently fail to capture the true cost of stakeholder alignment. Teams spent 14 days more than planned on feedback loops. Consequently, gross margins dropped below the target threshold of USD 45,000 per engagement.

## Corrective actions

We must standardize our scoping methodology across all practice areas. Department heads will implement stricter review gates before signing statements of work. These controls will protect our profitability on fixed-price contracts.

* Require dual sign-off on technical estimates
* Update baseline labor rates for junior analysts
* Introduce mandatory risk buffers into every budget

You play a vital role in executing these changes. Please review the updated guidelines before your next client pitch.
