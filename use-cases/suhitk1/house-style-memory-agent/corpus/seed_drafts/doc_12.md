# Onboarding analysis for new analysts

## Background and current metrics

We completed a review of onboarding times for new analysts in the practice on 13 August 2026. You requested this assessment to measure the time required for recent hires to reach billable capacity. We examined internal records from the past 12 months. The data shows clear trends across all regional offices.

New analysts spend an average of 42 days in training before joining client projects. This duration exceeds our target of 30 days by 40 percent. The extended timeline costs the firm approximately USD 12,500 per analyst in unbilled salary and trainer overhead. We found several bottlenecks in the current curriculum.

## Findings and next steps

The practice currently lacks clear guidance on core technical competencies. Trainers spend excessive time repeating foundational concepts. We surveyed 24 analysts regarding their initial weeks. The respondents highlighted three main issues with the current schedule:

* Outdated software tutorials
* Unclear expectations for independent study
* Limited access to senior mentors

We must address these delays immediately to protect our profit margins. The team proposes the following changes for the upcoming quarter.

We will update all technical manuals and introduce digital learning modules. These adjustments should reduce onboarding duration to 25 days. We invite your feedback on this proposal before we finalize the budget.
