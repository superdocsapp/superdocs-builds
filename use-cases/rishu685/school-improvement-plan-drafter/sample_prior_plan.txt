SCHOOL IMPROVEMENT PLAN 2025 - PINEWOOD ELEMENTARY

Section 1: Needs Assessment
Pinewood Elementary currently serves 420 students. 
- Math Proficiency: 58% of students meeting grade-level standards.
- Reading Proficiency: 62% of students meeting grade-level standards.
- Chronic Absenteeism: 14% of student body.

Section 2: Goals & Targets
- Goal 1 (Math): Increase math proficiency to 65% by end of year.
- Goal 2 (Reading): Increase reading proficiency to 70% by end of year.
- Goal 3 (Attendance): Reduce chronic absenteeism rate below 10%.

Section 3: Strategies & Resource Allocation
- Strategy 1: Implement daily 30-minute math intervention blocks. Allocate $15,000 for tutoring resources.
- Strategy 2: Adopt new phonics reading curriculum. Allocate $12,000 for teacher training.
- Strategy 3: Create attendance reward program. Allocate $2,000 for incentive rewards.

Section 4: Monitoring Schedule
Progress checks will occur mid-year in January and final review in June.
