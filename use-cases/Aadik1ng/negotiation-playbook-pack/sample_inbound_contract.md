HeliosGrid Compute — GPU Cluster Services Agreement (Draft, Customer Copy)

1. Service Availability
HeliosGrid targets 99.5% monthly uptime across the fleet in aggregate,
measured against HeliosGrid's internal status dashboard.

2. Remedies
If HeliosGrid misses the uptime target in a given month, the customer's
sole remedy is a service credit equal to 4% of that month's invoice,
applied to the following month's bill only. Credits do not carry forward.

3. Claims
Customer must submit any SLA credit claim in writing within 10 calendar
days of the end of the affected month.

4. Liability
HeliosGrid's total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not
exceed the fees paid in the preceding two (2) months, without exception.

5. Termination
Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon 60 days written
notice. Prepaid committed-use fees for the remainder of the term are
non-refundable.

6. Capacity
Reserved GPU capacity purchased under a committed-use plan is guaranteed
for the duration of the term and will not be reclaimed or reduced without
customer consent.

7. Pricing
Quoted per-GPU-hour pricing is fixed for the initial 12-month term.
HeliosGrid may pass through documented increases in underlying power and
colocation costs with 60 days notice to customer.

8. Data Handling
Upon termination, HeliosGrid will delete customer data from production
systems within 60 days, and will provide written certification of
deletion upon customer request.
