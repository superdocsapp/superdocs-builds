# MR Credit: Construction and Demolition Waste Management

**Rating system:** LEED v4.1 Building Design and Construction
**Category:** Materials and Resources
**Points available:** 1-2

## Intent

To reduce construction and demolition waste disposed of in landfills and
incineration facilities by recovering, reusing, and recycling materials.

## Requirements

Recycle and/or salvage nonhazardous construction and demolition materials.
Calculate the percentage of the total construction and demolition waste
diverted from disposal, by weight.

Excavated soil and land-clearing debris must not be included in the
calculation. Materials diverted must be diverted to legitimate recycling or
salvage facilities.

Points are awarded according to Table 1.

### Table 1. Points for waste diversion

| Diversion requirement                                    | Points |
|----------------------------------------------------------|--------|
| Divert 50% of total waste, at least three material streams | 1    |
| Divert 75% of total waste, at least four material streams  | 2    |

## Required documentation

1. The construction waste management plan.
2. Monthly waste hauler reports showing tonnage by material stream and
   destination facility.
3. The final waste diversion calculation summarising total waste generated,
   total diverted, and the diversion percentage.
4. Documentation confirming the receiving facilities are legitimate recycling
   or salvage operations.
