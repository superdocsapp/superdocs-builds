## 1. Event Overview

### Observed

The August Monsoon Depression began affecting the Barakar Valley Power Distribution Limited network at 14:05 on 11 August 2026, when the first outages were recorded on feeder 11 kV JMR-07 [EL-0004]. Consumers without supply peaked at 33,400, which is 9.8 per cent of the 342,000 consumers served, at 17:00 on 11 August [EL-0008]. Full restoration took 36 hours 35 minutes from the first recorded outage to the final consumer restored at 02:40 on 13 August [EL-0004, EL-0019].

### Assessed

The response is judged to have met its restoration objective, with no service interruption extending beyond the operating standard [assessed].

## 2. Timeline of Events

### Observed

* At 09:15 on 11 August: IMD Kolkata warned of a deep depression over Gangetic West Bengal, with extremely heavy rainfall and gusty winds of 55 to 65 km/h expected over Paschim Bardhaman from 14:00 [EL-0001]
* At 10:00 on 11 August: The storm control room was activated at the Asansol divisional office, and ten line gangs were placed on standby [EL-0002]
* At 13:20 on 11 August: Sustained winds of 58 km/h were recorded at the Burnpur monitoring mast, with rainfall of 84 mm reported over six hours at Asansol [EL-0003]
* At 14:05 on 11 August: The first outages occurred on the 11 kV Jamuria feeder JMR-07 following a tree contact in the Nunia canal corridor [EL-0004]
* At 15:30 on 11 August: Outages spread across the Raniganj and Jamuria sections due to waterlogging at a distribution substation [EL-0006]
* At 16:10 on 11 August: All standby line gangs were dispatched, and tree-cutting gangs were mobilised to the Nunia canal corridor [EL-0007]
* At 17:00 on 11 August: Peak consumers without supply reached 33,400 across 24 feeders [EL-0008]
* At 18:30 on 11 August: Water ingress was reported at the 33/11 kV Raniganj substation, leading to the precautionary isolation of affected equipment [EL-0009]
* At 22:00 on 11 August: Mutual aid was requested from the Durgapur distribution circle [EL-0011]
* At 02:15 on 12 August: Gangs from the Durgapur circle arrived on site [EL-0013]
* At 15:20 on 12 August: Remaining outages were identified as individual service connections requiring separate gang visits [EL-0017]
* At 08:00 on 13 August: The outage management system link held throughout the event. The manual fallback capture procedure remained undeveloped and was not exercised [EL-0020]
* At 09:00 on 13 August: A post-event review confirmed the replacement of 14 poles, 22 spans and 5 distribution transformers [EL-0021]

### Assessed

The response is judged to have met its restoration objective, with no service interruption extending beyond the operating standard [assessed]
## 3. Crew Mobilisation

### Observed

8 crews totalling 44 field personnel were mobilised for the event [EL-0002, EL-0007]. 1 mutual-aid crew totalling 10 personnel was requested from Durgapur Circle [EL-0011, EL-0013]. Crews worked approximately 1,050 personnel-hours on the event. This figure covers 7 of 8 crews; the remaining 1 has incomplete records and is excluded [EL-0002, EL-0007].

### Assessed

Mutual aid was requested at an appropriate point in the event, and earlier escalation would not have materially shortened the restoration [assessed].

### What the records do not show

Crew CR-B08 (10 personnel from Durgapur Circle) has no mobilisation time in the records, so its hours cannot be computed and are excluded from the personnel-hours total. [no record]

## 4. Restoration Performance

### Observed

Fifty per cent of affected consumers were restored within 13 hours of peak outage, by 06:00 on 12 August when 10,200 consumers remained without supply [EL-0008, EL-0014]. Ninety per cent of affected consumers were restored within 25 hours of peak outage, by 18:00 on 12 August when 1,340 consumers remained without supply [EL-0008, EL-0018]. Nine consumer counts were recorded across the event, from 14:05 on 11 August to 02:40 on 13 August [EL-0004, EL-0019].

### Assessed

Restoration sequencing prioritised transmission-level faults and high-count feeders correctly, and the resulting curve is consistent with the damage sustained [assessed].

## 5. Consumer Communications

### Observed

The first consumer notification was issued at 14:43 on 11 August, 38 minutes after the first recorded outage [EL-0004, EL-0005]. Five consumer communications were issued across five channels (IVR, SMS, WhatsApp, email, web), reaching 59,860 recipients in total [EL-0005, EL-0015, EL-0022].

### Assessed

Message cadence was appropriate to the restoration profile, and consumers received revised estimates at the points where the estimates materially changed [assessed].

### Raised by this event

LL-B1 — Tree contact in the Nunia canal corridor initiated the event. The first outage and the largest single outage cluster both originated from tree contact in the Nunia canal corridor, which was last cleared in 2023. Owner: Line Clearance Wing. [EL-0004, EL-0007]
