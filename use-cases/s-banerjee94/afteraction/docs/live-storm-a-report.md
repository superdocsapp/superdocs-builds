## Executive Summary

| The Kalbaisakhi Squall affected the Neamatpur 33/11 kV power network from 24 April 2026 17:00 to 26 April 2026 14:00, impacting 28,400 consumers. At the peak of the event at 19:52 on 24 April, 17,790 consumers were without supply. Damage confirmed across the substation area included 9 conductor spans, 6 insulators, 4 poles, 4 cross arms, and 2 transformers. The response mobilized 8 crews totaling 42 personnel, with 2 mutual-aid crews and 12 additional personnel requested from Durgapur. Restoration was efficient, with 50 percent of affected consumers restored within 24 hours 18 minutes and 90 percent within 38 hours 23 minutes. Progress was challenged by a 3 hour 45 minute interruption in consumer counting and unavailable hours for one crew. Consumers were informed via 7 notifications across 4 channels, reaching 71,910 total recipients. The report concludes that the response met its restoration objective, with service interruptions remaining within operating standards and the damage profile consistent with wind and tree contact. |

## 1. Event Overview

### Observed

The Kalbaisakhi Squall began affecting the Neamatpur 33/11 kV network at 18:22 on 24 April 2026, when the first outages were recorded on feeder 11 kV BRK-03 [TEL-0001]. Consumers without supply peaked at 17,790, which is 62.6 per cent of the 28,400 consumers served by the substation, at 19:52 on 24 April [TEL-0005]. Full restoration took 42 hours 58 minutes from the first recorded outage to the final consumer restored at 13:20 on 26 April [TEL-0001, TEL-0016].

### Assessed

The response is judged to have met its restoration objective, with no service interruption extending beyond the operating standard [assessed].

## 2. Timeline of Events

### Observed

* **WINDOW 2 — 24 April, 18:00–20:00** The squall line reached Neamatpur, recording a gust of 96 km/h at the Sitarampur monitoring mast alongside heavy rain and hail at Mohishila. [WEA-0002] BRK-03 tripped on an earth fault and failed to reclose, leaving the Barakar Bazaar section out. [TEL-0001] One pole at the Barakar level crossing was damaged, snapped at the base with the conductor across the approach road. [DMG-0001] A line gang of 6 from Neamatpur was mobilised to 11 kV BRK-03 but held at the crossing until the section was confirmed dead. [CRW-0001] MHS-07 tripped on overcurrent, leaving the Mohishila section out. [TEL-0002] A live conductor was found across the approach road, leading to area cordoning and isolation before any gang approached. [FLD-0001] A line gang of 6 from Neamatpur was mobilised to 11 kV MHS-07. [CRW-0002] Three poles at Mohishila Colony were tilted after a tree fell along the line route. [DMG-0002] KLT-02 tripped after cascading faults on the Kulti trunk. [TEL-0003] Seven conductor spans were downed between poles 14 and 21 on the Kulti to Sitarampur trunk. [DMG-0003] Patrol confirmed seven spans down between poles 14 and 21. [FLD-0002] A line gang of 5 from Kulti was mobilised to 11 kV KLT-02. [CRW-0003] STP-05 lost supply to Sitarampur laterals. [TEL-0004] Two distribution transformers at Sitarampur DTR-14 and DTR-22 failed on overcurrent. [DMG-0004] SRN-01 tripped, leaving the Srishti Nagar section out. [TEL-0005]
* **WINDOW 3 — 24 April, 20:00–22:00** Four cross-arms were broken in Srishti Nagar Phase 2, though insulator strings remained intact. [DMG-0005, FLD-0003] A tree-cutting gang of 4 from Neamatpur was mobilised to 11 kV MHS-07 to clear a fallen tree blocking the line route before line work could start. [CRW-0004, FLD-0004] Peak consumers without supply reached 17,790 across five feeders. [TEL-0006] A line gang of 5 from Sitarampur was mobilised to 11 kV STP-05, though release was not recorded. [CRW-0005] One pole at Mohishila Bazaar was reported damaged with a large tree trunk resting against it, but no gang has inspected it for confirmation. [DMG-0006] Rainfall reached 88 mm over five hours at the Neamatpur substation gauge. [WEA-0003]
* **WINDOW 4 — 24 April, 22:00–00:00** MHS-07 was restored after the faulted section was isolated at the Mohishila sectionaliser. [TEL-0007] Night working was suspended on the Kulti section due to standing water around pole bases and a live upstream trunk. [FLD-0005]
* **WINDOW 5 — 25 April, 00:00–02:00** The outage management system link to the Neamatpur RTU was lost, halting consumer count recording. [TEL-0008]
* **WINDOW 6 — 25 April, 02:00–04:00** A second line of thunderstorms passed over Kulti with gusts reaching 62 km/h, though no further squall was recorded. [WEA-0004]
* **WINDOW 7 — 25 April, 04:00–06:00** The outage management system link was restored after 3 hours 45 minutes, resuming counts. [TEL-0009] The first consumer count was taken after the outage management system link was restored. [TEL-0010]
* **WINDOW 8 — 25 April, 06:00–08:00** Rainfall stopped and winds fell below 20 km/h across the substation area. [WEA-0005] A transformer gang of 4 from Asansol was mobilised to 11 kV STP-05 for transformer replacement at Sitarampur. [CRW-0007]
* **WINDOW 9 — 25 April, 08:00–10:00** Six insulators were confirmed cracked by hail in the Kulti section between poles 8 and 12 during patrolling. [DMG-0007, FLD-0006] BRK-03 was re-energised following pole replacement near the Barakar level crossing. [TEL-0011]
* **WINDOW 10 — 25 April, 10:00–12:00** Two conductor spans were confirmed downed in a flooded field in Sitarampur Ward 6, with access delayed until water receded. [DMG-0008] A lineman sustained a minor laceration while clearing a fallen span, though it was treated on site with no lost time. [FLD-0007]
* **WINDOW 11 — 25 April, 12:00–14:00** Flooding reached knee depth, preventing the gang from reaching the downed spans and deferring work until the water receded. [FLD-0008]
* **WINDOW 12 — 25 April, 14:00–16:00** SRN-01 was restored, returning supply to Srishti Nagar. [TEL-0012]
* **WINDOW 13 — 25 April, 20:00–22:00** KLT-02 was restored to the Kulti trunk, though Sitarampur laterals remained out. [TEL-0013]
* **WINDOW 14 — 26 April, 04:00–06:00** Remaining outages consisted of individual service connections on the Sitarampur laterals. [TEL-0014]
* **WINDOW 15 — 26 April, 06:00–08:00** Water receded overnight, restoring access and allowing conductor stringing to resume. [FLD-0009]
* **WINDOW 16 — 26 April, 10:00–12:00** STP-05 laterals were energised following transformer replacement at Sitarampur. [TEL-0015] All snapped-conductor sites were cleared and released for normal operation. [FLD-0010]
* **WINDOW 17 — 26 April, 12:00–14:00** The final consumer was restored and the event closed. [TEL-0016]

## 3. Damage Assessment

### Observed

* Confirmed damage across the substation area: 9 conductor spans, 6 insulators, 4 poles, 4 cross arms, 2 transformers [DMG-0001, DMG-0002, DMG-0003, DMG-0004, DMG-0005, DMG-0007, DMG-0008]
* 8 damage records were raised: 3 by a consumer contact, 3 by a crew on site, 2 by network telemetry [DMG-0001, DMG-0002, DMG-0003, DMG-0004, DMG-0005, DMG-0006, DMG-0007, DMG-0008]

### Assessed

The damage profile is consistent with wind and tree contact rather than with any underlying asset condition, and no pattern of premature failure is apparent [assessed]

### What the records do not show

1 pole at Mohishila Bazaar (DMG-0006) was reported by customer and never inspected, so the records establish neither that it is damaged nor that it is sound, and it is excluded from the confirmed totals above [no record].

## 4. Crew Mobilisation

### Observed

8 crews totalling 42 field personnel were mobilised for the event [CRW-0001, CRW-0002]. 2 mutual-aid crews totalling 12 personnel were requested from Durgapur [CRW-0006, CRW-0008]. Crews worked approximately 534 personnel-hours on the event [CRW-0001, CRW-0002, CRW-0003]. This figure covers 5 of 8 crews; the remaining 3 have incomplete records and are excluded [CRW-0001, CRW-0002, CRW-0003].

### Assessed

Mutual aid was requested at an appropriate point in the event, and earlier escalation would not have materially shortened the restoration [assessed].

### What the records do not show

* Crew CRW-0005 (5 personnel from Sitarampur) has no release time in the records, so its hours cannot be computed and are excluded from the personnel-hours total. [no record]
* Crew CRW-0006 (6 personnel from Durgapur) has no mobilisation time in the records, so its hours cannot be computed and are excluded from the personnel-hours total. [no record]
* Crew CRW-0008 (6 personnel from Durgapur) has no mobilisation time in the records, so its hours cannot be computed and are excluded from the personnel-hours total. [no record]

## 5. Restoration Performance

### Observed

* 50 per cent of affected consumers were restored within 24 hours 18 minutes of peak outage, by 20:10 on 25 April when 3,400 consumers remained without supply [TEL-0005, TEL-0013]
* 90 per cent of affected consumers were restored within 38 hours 23 minutes of peak outage, by 10:15 on 26 April when 430 consumers remained without supply [TEL-0005, TEL-0015]
* 14 consumer counts were recorded across the event, from 18:22 on 24 April to 13:20 on 26 April [TEL-0001, TEL-0016]
* 4 access constraints were recorded during restoration, each one holding work on a section until it cleared [FLD-0004, FLD-0005, FLD-0008, FLD-0009]

### Assessed

Restoration sequencing prioritised the highest-count feeders correctly, and the resulting curve is consistent with the damage sustained and the access available [assessed].

## 6. Consumer Communications

### Observed

The first consumer notification was issued at 20:35 on 24 April, 2 hours 13 minutes after the first recorded outage [TEL-0001, COM-0003]. 7 notifications were issued to consumers across 4 channels (IVR, SMS, WhatsApp, web), reaching 71,910 recipients in total [COM-0003, COM-0004, COM-0007]. 855 consumer contacts were received across 4 channels (SMS, WhatsApp, call centre, email) in 8 batches [COM-0001, COM-0002, COM-0005]. 809 of those contacts were resolved, averaging 18 hours 44 minutes from contact to resolution, of which 140 contacts closed later than the 24-hour target [COM-0001, COM-0002, COM-0006].

### Assessed

Message cadence was appropriate to the restoration profile, and consumers received revised estimates at the points where the estimates materially changed [assessed].

### What the records do not show

* 34 consumer contacts received by WhatsApp at 21:12 on 24 April (“Tree trunk resting against pole, Mohishila Bazaar”, COM-0005) carried no resolution time when the event closed, 40 hours 48 minutes later. The records do not show whether it was answered. [no record]
* 12 consumer contacts received by call centre at 09:20 on 26 April (“Individual service connection still out, Ward 6”, COM-0014) carried no resolution time when the event closed, 4 hours 40 minutes later. The records do not show whether it was answered. [no record]
* 7 messages (COM-0002, COM-0005, COM-0006, COM-0009, COM-0010, COM-0012, COM-0014) carry no reference to a telemetry or field record, so they cannot be corroborated against the rest of the event. [no record]

## 9. Lessons Learned

### Raised by this event

LL-A1 — Mutual-aid gang mobilisation times are not captured. Two mutual-aid gangs from the Durgapur circle worked the event and both were released against a recorded time, but neither carries a mobilisation time, so mutual-aid gang-hours cannot be computed from the records. Owner: Distribution Operations, Neamatpur Section. [CRW-0006, CRW-0008]

LL-A2 — Time to first consumer notification exceeded the two-hour target. The first outage was recorded at 18:22 and the first consumer notification was issued at 20:35, an interval of 2 hours 13 minutes against a two-hour internal target. Owner: Consumer Services Wing. [TEL-0001, COM-0003]

LL-A3 — Outage management system link was lost with no fallback capture. The link to the Neamatpur RTU was unavailable for 3 hours 45 minutes and no manual consumer counts were taken during the gap, leaving a hole in the restoration curve that cannot be reconstructed. Owner: SCADA and IT Wing. [TEL-0008, TEL-0009]

LL-A4 — A consumer-reported pole was never inspected. A consumer reported a tree trunk resting against a pole at Mohishila Bazaar on the first evening. No gang was assigned, the report was still unresolved when the event closed, and the pole has never been confirmed either damaged or sound. Owner: Distribution Operations, Neamatpur Section. [DMG-0006, COM-0005]
