## 1. Event Overview

### Observed

Kalbaisakhi Squall began affecting the Barakar Valley Power Distribution Limited network at 16:52 on 24 April 2026, when the first outages were recorded on feeder 11 kV BNP-04 [EL-0004]. Consumers without supply peaked at 74,600, which is 21.8 per cent of the 342,000 consumers served, at 20:15 on 24 April [EL-0008]. Full restoration took 43 hours 28 minutes from the first recorded outage to the final consumer restored at 12:20 on 26 April [EL-0004, EL-0030].

### Assessed

The response is judged to have met its restoration objective, with no service interruption extending beyond the operating standard [assessed].

## 2. Timeline of Events

### Observed

At 13:30 on 24 April: IMD Kolkata issued a thunderstorm warning with squall for Paschim Bardhaman district, with gusts of 70 to 90 km/h expected from 16:00 [EL-0001]. At 14:15 on 24 April: The storm control room was activated at the Asansol divisional office, with twelve line gangs placed on standby across the Hirapur, Kulti, and Raniganj sections [EL-0002]. At 16:40 on 24 April: The squall line reached Asansol; gusts of 96 km/h were recorded at the Burnpur monitoring mast, with heavy rain and hail reported at Jamuria [EL-0003]. At 16:52 on 24 April: The first outages were recorded on the 11 kV Burnpur feeder BNP-04 after a tree fell across the conductor near Burnpur bazaar [EL-0004]. At 17:10 on 24 April: Line gangs 1 to 6 were dispatched to the Hirapur and Burnpur sections [EL-0005]. At 17:45 on 24 April: Cascading outages occurred across the Kulti and Barakar sections after multiple poles tilted on the KLT-02 feeder [EL-0006]. At 20:15 on 24 April: Peak consumers without supply were recorded at 74,600 across 41 feeders [EL-0008]. At 20:40 on 24 April: A snapped 11 kV conductor was reported at Neamatpur More; the area was cordoned and the section isolated [EL-0009]. At 21:20 on 24 April: Mutual aid was requested from the Durgapur and Purulia distribution circles [EL-0010]. At 23:05 on 24 April: Damage assessment reported 34 poles snapped or tilted, 52 spans of conductor down, and 9 distribution transformers damaged [EL-0011]. At 01:15 on 25 April: The outage management system link to the Asansol data centre was lost, and consumer counts were not recorded from this point [EL-0013]. At 03:40 on 25 April: Mutual-aid gangs from the Durgapur circle arrived on site [EL-0014]. At 04:45 on 25 April: The outage management system link was restored after 3 hours 30 minutes, and counts resumed [EL-0015]. At 11:20 on 25 April: A lineman at Raniganj suffered a minor laceration while clearing a fallen span; he was treated on site with no lost time [EL-0019]. At 20:30 on 25 April: Tree-cutting gangs were reassigned to the Barakar corridor to clear gang access along the feeder route [EL-0023]. At 04:00 on 26 April: Remaining outages were individual service connections requiring separate gang visits [EL-0025]. At 11:30 on 26 April: All snapped-conductor sites were cleared and released for normal operation [EL-0029]. At 15:00 on 26 April: A post-event review was scheduled, noting that 34 poles, 52 spans, and 9 distribution transformers had been replaced [EL-0031].

### What the records do not show

Consumer counting was interrupted from 01:15 on 25 April (EL-0013) until 04:45 on 25 April (EL-0015), a span of 3 hours 30 minutes. The restoration curve is broken across this window and the records do not support a figure inside it. [no record]

## 3. Crew Mobilisation

### Observed

Eleven crews totalling 61 field personnel were mobilised for the event [EL-0002, EL-0005]. Two mutual-aid crews totalling 18 personnel were requested from the Durgapur and Purulia distribution circles [EL-0010, EL-0014]. Crews worked approximately 1,521 personnel-hours on the event. This figure covers 9 of 11 crews; the remaining 2 have incomplete records and are excluded [EL-0002, EL-0005, EL-0011].

### Assessed

Mutual aid was requested at an appropriate point in the event, and earlier escalation would not have materially shortened the restoration [assessed].

### What the records do not show

Crew CR-A10 (10 personnel from Durgapur Circle) has no mobilisation time in the records, so its hours cannot be computed and are excluded from the personnel-hours total. [no record]

Crew CR-A11 (8 personnel from Purulia Circle) has no mobilisation time and no release time in the records, so its hours cannot be computed and are excluded from the personnel-hours total. [no record]

## 4. Restoration Performance

### Observed

50 per cent of affected consumers were restored within 16 hours 45 minutes of peak outage, by 13:00 on 25 April when 31,500 consumers remained without supply [EL-0008, EL-0020]. 90 per cent of affected consumers were restored within 37 hours 45 minutes of peak outage, by 10:00 on 26 April when 3,600 consumers remained without supply [EL-0008, EL-0028]. 12 consumer counts were recorded across the event, from 16:52 on 24 April to 12:20 on 26 April [EL-0004, EL-0030].

### Assessed

Restoration sequencing prioritised transmission-level faults and high-count feeders correctly, and the resulting curve is consistent with the damage sustained [assessed].

## 5. Consumer Communications

### Observed

The first consumer notification was issued at 19:33 on 24 April, 2 hours 41 minutes after the first recorded outage [EL-0004, EL-0007]. A total of 8 consumer communications were issued across 6 channels (IVR, SMS, WhatsApp, email, press, web), reaching 262,700 recipients in total [EL-0007, EL-0017, EL-0021].

### Assessed

Message cadence was appropriate to the restoration profile, and consumers received revised estimates at the points where the estimates materially changed [assessed].

### What the records do not show

Communication CC-A06 (press, 19:00 on 25 April, “Briefing to local media on restoration progress”) has no corresponding event-log entry, so its issue cannot be corroborated against the log. [no record]
