VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT (SYNTHETIC — for demo purposes only)

Clause 1. Definitions
"Client" refers to Acme Retail Corp. "Vendor" refers to Northwind Logistics LLC.
This Agreement governs the provision of warehousing and fulfillment services.

Clause 2. Scope of Services
Vendor shall provide warehousing, pick-and-pack, and last-mile delivery
coordination for Client's e-commerce operations.

Clause 3. Term
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial
term of two (2) years.

Clause 4. Service Levels
Vendor shall fulfill 98% of orders within 24 hours of receipt.

Clause 5. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep the other's confidential information secret for a
period of 3 years following termination of this Agreement.

Clause 6. Data Protection
Vendor shall process Client customer data solely for the purposes of this
Agreement and in accordance with applicable data protection law.

Clause 7. Payment Terms
Client shall pay all undisputed invoices within 30 days of receipt.

Clause 8. Late Fees
Invoices unpaid after the due date accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

Clause 9. Insurance
Vendor shall maintain commercial general liability insurance with minimum
coverage of $2,000,000 per occurrence throughout the Term.

Clause 10. Indemnification
Vendor shall indemnify Client against third-party claims arising from
Vendor's negligence in performing the Services.

Clause 11. Force Majeure
Neither party is liable for delays caused by events beyond its reasonable
control, including natural disasters, labor disputes, or government action.

Clause 12. Late Payment
Notwithstanding Clause 7, invoices disputed in good faith must instead be
settled within 45 days of receipt, measured from the date the dispute is
resolved.

Clause 13. Termination for Convenience
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience with 90 days'
written notice to the other party.

Clause 14. Termination for Cause
Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice
if the other party materially breaches and fails to cure within 15 days.

Clause 15. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without
regard to its conflict of laws principles.

Clause 16. Limitation of Liability
Neither party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall exceed the
total fees paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

Clause 17. Notices
All notices under this Agreement must be delivered in writing to the
addresses set out in the signature block, by email or courier.

Clause 18. Renewal
This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive terms of twelve
(12) months unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least
60 days before the end of the then-current term.

Clause 19. Assignment
Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent
of the other party, not to be unreasonably withheld.

Clause 20. Renewal Notice Period
Notwithstanding Clause 18, either party may decline to renew this
Agreement by giving 30 days' notice before the end of the then-current term.

Clause 21. Entire Agreement
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and
supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, or agreements.
