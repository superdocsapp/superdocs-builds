# Country-Conditions Evidence Brief

*Synthetic test case — Republic of Salvarion is fictional; all sources are fabricated test data.*


## Particular social group / protected-ground membership

> "Civil Liberties Watch Salvarion's caseworkers documented a consistent pattern across the reports reviewed: complainants who identified themselves as union organizers described municipal police as declining to open an investigation, or opening an investigation that was not meaningfully pursued. We found no case in our 2024 caseload in which a threat against a labor organizer resulted in an identified suspect being charged."
>
> — Civil Liberties Watch Salvarion, “State Response to Threats Against Labor Organizers: Annual Report 2025” (March 2025), SRC-A ¶4

> ""We called the police as soon as it happened. An officer came, took a statement, and that was the last we heard," said union organizing committee member Renata Oskar, one of the three injured. Oskar said she has not been contacted by police since filing her statement on the night of the attack."
>
> — Salvarion Daily Post, “Union Hall Attacked in Rosemont, Organizers Say Police Slow to Act” (16 June 2024), SRC-B ¶2

> "Labor relations. Salvarion's labor movement has grown more active since 2022, with union membership rising across the textile and agricultural sectors. This growth has been accompanied by episodic reports of violence and intimidation directed at organizers, concentrated in the Rosemont and Calderas regions."
>
> — Meridian Country Research Group, “Salvarion Country Conditions Backgrounder, 2025 Edition” (2025), SRC-C ¶1

> "In my professional opinion, labor organizers in Salvarion who are publicly identified as such -- for example, by holding an office in a union's organizing committee, or by being named in press coverage of union activity -- form a socially distinct and recognizable group within Salvarion. Individuals outside the labor movement in Salvarion routinely identify "union organizers" as a distinct category of person, both in ordinary conversation and in how local media and police describe complainants."
>
> — Dr. Elena Voss, Dept. of Comparative Politics, Fictional University of Meridian, “Declaration of Dr. Elena Voss” (2025), SRC-D ¶2


## Past persecution or well-founded fear of future harm

> "Our intake log recorded 61 distinct reports of threats, intimidation, or physical harm directed at individuals identified by the reporter as current or former labor union organizers. Of these, 40 reports indicated the complainant had filed a report with municipal police and received no documented follow-up within 90 days."
>
> — Civil Liberties Watch Salvarion, “State Response to Threats Against Labor Organizers: Annual Report 2025” (March 2025), SRC-A ¶2

> "Three organizers with the Rosemont Textile Workers' Union were injured Friday evening when a group of unidentified men attacked the union's local office, according to union officials and a police report reviewed by this newspaper."
>
> — Salvarion Daily Post, “Union Hall Attacked in Rosemont, Organizers Say Police Slow to Act” (16 June 2024), SRC-B ¶1

> "In response to rising reports of intimidation, the Ministry of Labor announced in September 2024 the formation of a Labor Rights Protection Unit within the national police, tasked specifically with receiving and investigating complaints of threats or violence against labor organizers. The unit's stated mandate includes a 30-day response requirement for complaints referred to it."
>
> — Meridian Country Research Group, “Salvarion Country Conditions Backgrounder, 2025 Edition” (2025), SRC-C ¶2

> "Based on the pattern described in independent reporting -- including the Rosemont union hall attack of June 2024, which by all accounts I have reviewed remains without an identified suspect -- it is my assessment that, at the municipal level in Rosemont specifically, a labor organizer facing a threat in the period surrounding mid-2024 would most likely have had their complaint handled through ordinary police channels rather than by the newly announced unit, given the timing and the unit's apparent lack of municipal-level staffing as of this declaration."
>
> — Dr. Elena Voss, Dept. of Comparative Politics, Fictional University of Meridian, “Declaration of Dr. Elena Voss” (2025), SRC-D ¶4
