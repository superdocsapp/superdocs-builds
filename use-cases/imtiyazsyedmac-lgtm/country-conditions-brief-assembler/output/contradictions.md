# Contradicting Sources Surfaced


## state_protection: SRC-A vs SRC-C

- **SRC-A** (protection unavailable/unproven), SRC-A ¶2: "Our intake log recorded 61 distinct reports of threats, intimidation, or physical harm directed at individuals identified by the reporter as current or former labor union organizers. Of these, 40 reports indicated the complainant had filed a report with municipal police and received no documented follow-up within 90 days."

- **SRC-C** (protection available), SRC-C ¶2: "In response to rising reports of intimidation, the Ministry of Labor announced in September 2024 the formation of a Labor Rights Protection Unit within the national police, tasked specifically with receiving and investigating complaints of threats or violence against labor organizers. The unit's stated mandate includes a 30-day response requirement for complaints referred to it."


Both are kept in the brief and the source packet. Neither is dropped in favor of the other.
