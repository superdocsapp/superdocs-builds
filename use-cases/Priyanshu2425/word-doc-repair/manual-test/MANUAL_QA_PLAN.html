The defect this run found — and what was done about it

The first live styling run came back with the wording rewritten: a four-line recovered report gained three invented paragraphs, a subtotal row, a “Remarks & Disclaimers” section, a signature block and a footer — none of it in the document that was sent. It opens cleanly and it reads better than the plain rebuild, which is exactly why it is the worst output this product could produce: its owner would not notice.

Two changes, in this order. The instruction was tightened to forbid additions by name. Then a guard was added that does not depend on the instruction being obeyed: the words that come back are compared against the words that went out, and any difference at all throws the styled file away with the reason said plainly. On the re-run the same document came back word for word identical — but the instruction is the request and the guard is the promise, and only one of them is a test.
