Notes

What it optimises
People who know the result they want but not the words for it. The controls hand them the sentence; the field lets them disagree with it before it is sent.
The seam is visible on purpose
A control that silently issued an instruction would be a second, hidden conversation. Writing it into the field keeps one record of everything asked for, whether it was typed or clicked.
Pages, not a scroll
Styling changes where things fall. A proof shows that; a continuous column hides it until the file is opened in Word, which is the worst moment to find out.
Refusals name the alternative
“Not that — but here is the nearest thing I can do with what is already here.” A no that ends the conversation is a no the person will work around badly.
When the counter is shut
The controls do not render at all — every one of them is a request that cannot be made — and the rail carries the proof, the history, and one sentence saying why nothing more can be asked today.
Both files stay reachable
“Download this proof” names what you are looking at; the unstyled rebuild sits beside it.
