Notes

What it optimises
Reversibility. Styling is taste, and taste is tried on. Six instructions in, a linear undo is useless — you want the third one gone and the other five kept.
Composer at the top
Because the stack runs newest-first. Writing at the bottom and reading at the top would put the newest slip furthest from the field that made it.
Margin ticks
Hover a slip, its blocks light in the sheet; hover a block, its slip lifts. The ticks use post-blue — the institution's mark — never red, because an amendment is not damage.
A refusal costs nothing
It gets a slip with no number and a red left rule, and says plainly that nothing was counted against the allowance. Being told no should never feel like being charged for it.
When the counter is shut
The rail still renders — the slips already applied are history and stay readable — but the order field is replaced by one sentence saying why nothing more can be asked for today.
Both files stay reachable
The download names its own state (“with 3 amendments”), and the unstyled rebuild sits beside it.
