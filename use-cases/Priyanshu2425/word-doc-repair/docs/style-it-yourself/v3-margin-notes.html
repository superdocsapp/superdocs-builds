Notes

What it optimises
Precision without vocabulary. Nobody knows what to call the thing they dislike; everybody can point at it. Selecting a passage removes the hardest sentence the person has to write.
The rail folds
To 46 px, keeping the note count visible. Reading the document and instructing it are two different postures, and the screen should be able to hold either one whole.
Pins, not highlights
A settled note leaves a numbered post-blue pin and a thin underline. Nothing in the sheet is ever marked in red — red belongs to loss, and a styling note is not a loss.
Threads, not a transcript
Ten instructions over one document produce ten short threads instead of one scroll. You can answer “what did I ask about the table?” without reading anything else.
When the counter is shut
Existing pins stay and stay readable, the callout is not drawn on selection, and the rail's foot carries one sentence saying why. Nothing renders as a button that cannot be pressed.
Both files stay reachable
Unstyled copy beside the download, top right, for as long as the screen is open.
