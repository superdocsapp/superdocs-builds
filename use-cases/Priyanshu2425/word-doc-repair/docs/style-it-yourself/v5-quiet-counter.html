Notes

What it optimises
The document. It gets 1,008 of 1,320 px and the largest type of the five, because the reply to “make the headings smaller” is smaller headings, not a sentence claiming they are smaller.
Receipts, not a transcript
One ruled line per instruction, newest first, the way a statement lists what has happened to an account. The full exchange sits behind “Read what was said, in full” — present for whoever wants it, absent for whoever does not.
Before and after
The toggle is what the saved width buys. Four instructions in, “what did I actually change?” is the real question, and no amount of chat answers it as well as showing the file both ways.
Two kinds of no
Both take the red mark, and the line says which. A no made before anything is sent — asking for words the document does not have — changed nothing and was counted for nothing. A no made on a file that came back wrong was already charged for, and the line says so rather than claiming otherwise.
When the counter is shut
The receipts and the toggle stay — both are about what already happened — and the field is replaced by the one sentence that says why nothing more can be asked for today.
Both files stay reachable
Unstyled copy beside the download, top right, and the toggle makes the difference between them visible before either is taken.
