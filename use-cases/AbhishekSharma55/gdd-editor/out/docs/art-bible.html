Silhouette rules

  • Every hazard must be identifiable as a black shape at 40 px.
  • Traversable geometry is warm; lethal geometry is cold. No exceptions.
  • Detail budget goes to edges, not surfaces.

Palette

RoleHexUsed for
Traversable#E8A33D Ledges, grapple points, anything the player may touch
Lethal#3D6FE8 Coolant, vacuum breaches, crushers
Neutral hull#37474F Everything else
