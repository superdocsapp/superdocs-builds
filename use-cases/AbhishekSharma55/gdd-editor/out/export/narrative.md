# Nebula Drift - Narrative

Vantage Nine Studios - internal - sub-document of the Nebula Drift GDD.

## Premise

The Drift Belt was a shipyard until it was not. Nobody agrees on what
happened and the only person still transmitting has a reason to lie.

## Act I - Salvage

The player learns to move. Vex, on the channel, is helpful and slightly too
well informed about a place she claims never to have visited.

## Act II - The Cartographer

The wreck has been mapped before. The player starts finding the map, and it
is annotated in Vex's hand.

## Act III - Drift

The player can leave or finish the map. Both endings are short; neither is
the "good" one.

## Characters

| Character | Role | Voice |
