SECOND SYNTHETIC MANUSCRIPT
by Verification Author

CHAPTER I.
The Glass Orchard

Mira entered Veyra carrying the Emberglass. The orchard keeper warned her that Emberglass must remain unchanged in every language.

The word _Veyra_ was written in the margin because it named the valley itself.

CHAPTER II.
The Bell Archive

At the Bell Archive, Mira asked for the Night Ledger. The archivist said the Ledger was a protected title and must not be translated.
