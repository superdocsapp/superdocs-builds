# MedSure Glucose Monitoring System — User Agreement and Safety Instructions

## Purpose and Scope
This agreement governs the use of the MedSure Glucose Monitoring System (the "Device"). It applies to all patients and caregivers who operate the Device in accordance with these instructions.

## Calibration Requirements
The user must calibrate the Device at least once every 90 days using an approved calibration solution. Calibration records should be retained for reference.

## Operating Temperature Restriction
The Device must not be operated above 40°C or below 5°C. Operating outside this range may produce inaccurate glucose readings.

## Warranty Claim Submission
If the Device fails to operate as described, the user must submit a warranty claim through the MedSure support portal. Claims submitted without a proof of purchase will not be processed.

## Warranty Claim Deadline
Warranty claims must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the failure being discovered. Claims submitted after this period will be denied.

## Late Payment Fee
If an optional extended-service invoice is not paid by the due date, a late fee of 5% of the invoice amount will be applied for each month the payment remains outstanding.

## Data Retention
MedSure retains Device usage data for at least 24 months to support warranty and safety investigations. Data is deleted afterward unless required by law.

## Safety Incident Reporting
Any safety incident involving the Device must be reported to the Regulatory Authority no later than January 31, 2027. Late reports may result in regulatory penalties.

## Storage Conditions
Store the Device and test strips between 2°C and 8°C when not in use. Do not freeze.

## Complaints and Oversight
Complaints regarding the Device may be filed with the MedSure Compliance Board (MCB) at any time.

## Intended Use Population
The Device is intended for single-patient use only and must never be shared between multiple patients under any circumstances, including after cleaning or disinfection.

## Blood Sample Type
The Device measures glucose levels in capillary whole blood obtained from a fingertip sample.
