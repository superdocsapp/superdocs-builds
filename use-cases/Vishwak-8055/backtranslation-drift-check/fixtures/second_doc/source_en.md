# Acme Corporation — Remote Work Equipment Policy

## Purpose
This policy explains how employees may request reimbursement for home-office equipment. It applies to all full-time remote employees.

## Reimbursement Cap
Employees may be reimbursed for up to $500 per calendar year for approved home-office equipment.

## Submission Deadline
Receipts must be submitted within 14 days of purchase to qualify for reimbursement.

## Approval
The finance team will review and approve eligible requests within five business days.
