How it works

  1. Upload your PDF — LatBuild maps its sections and layout.
  2. Select any section(s) you want to edit.
  3. Give SuperDocs an instruction — "make this more concise", "improve academic clarity", etc.
  4. Review the proposed rewrites side-by-side.
  5. Approve — LatBuild surgically patches only the selected sections into the original PDF.
  6. Download your document — same layout, updated content.

SuperDocs sees only the section text. Your PDF structure is never sent to any AI model.
