MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (MSA) — VERSION 1.0 (BASELINE)

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2026, by and between Alpha Vendor Inc. ("Vendor") and Beta Enterprises LLC ("Client").

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES
Vendor shall provide custom software engineering and infrastructure management services as detailed in executed Statements of Work (SOWs).

2. FEES AND PAYMENT TERMS
Client shall pay all undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days of receipt (Net 30). Late payments shall incur interest at the rate of 1.0% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law.

3. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, each party's aggregate cumulative liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be limited to direct damages and shall not exceed one times (1x) the total fees paid or payable by Client in the twelve (12) months preceding the incident. Neither party shall be liable for indirect, consequential, or punitive damages.

4. INDEMNIFICATION
Each party ("Indemnifying Party") agrees to defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the other party from and against any third-party claims, damages, and reasonable legal expenses arising directly out of the Indemnifying Party's gross negligence, willful misconduct, or material breach of confidentiality obligations.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS
Vendor retains all proprietary rights in its pre-existing tools, libraries, and frameworks. Upon full payment of applicable fees, Client is granted a non-exclusive, perpetual, worldwide license to use all deliverables developed specifically for Client under this Agreement.

6. TERMINATION FOR CONVENIENCE
Either party may terminate this Agreement or any Statement of Work for convenience upon sixty (60) days prior written notice to the other party.

7. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
