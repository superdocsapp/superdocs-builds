# Clinical Operations Lead

## Team
Care Delivery Operations

## Role purpose
Run Meridian's internal care-delivery operations staff who monitor alerts, outreach queues, and escalation procedures for programs Meridian operates on behalf of customers.

## Responsibilities
- Manage a team of six to ten clinical operations associates, including hiring, scheduling, and performance reviews.
- Own staffing grids for weekday clinic hours and weekend coverage.
- Define escalation procedures when an alert suggests a patient safety issue.
- Review queue backlogs daily and reallocate work when a program spikes.
- Partner with nursing informatics on playbook changes after a near-miss.
- Report operational metrics (time-to-outreach, abandoned alerts, staffing variance) to the VP of Clinical Operations.
- Handle employee-relations issues on the operations floor with the People partner.

## Scope and autonomy
You are accountable for the operations floor that executes Meridian-run care programs. You decide shift mix, who is on which queue, and when to call overtime. Clinical protocol content is owned with informatics and the CMO office; you do not invent medical protocols. Product changes are requests, not orders you can ship.

## Leadership and management
This is a people-management role. You have direct reports. You hire, coach, discipline, and write performance reviews for operations associates. You are the manager of record.

## Stakeholders
- Clinical operations associates
- Nursing informatics and the CMO office
- Customer program managers
- People partner for the operations org
- Clinical reliability, during platform incidents that flood queues
- VP of Clinical Operations

## Requirements
- Experience leading an operations team in a care-delivery, call-center, or clinical-support setting.
- Judgment about patient-safety escalations and when to pull a licensed clinician in.
- Workforce planning skill, including weekends and attrition.
- Calm, specific communication during volume spikes.
