# Clinical Product Manager, Care Pathways

## Team
Care Pathways

## Role purpose
Own Meridian's care-pathway product: the structured plans, tasking, and outcome checkpoints that care teams use for conditions such as heart-failure follow-up and post-discharge bundled care.

## Responsibilities
- Set the pathway roadmap from clinician interviews, quality-measure pressure, and engineering capacity.
- Write problem briefs that distinguish a documentation burden from a genuine clinical-workflow need.
- Prioritize pathway templates with the Chief Medical Officer's office and with customer quality leaders.
- Define acceptance criteria with licensed-clinician reviewers before a pathway ships.
- Measure completion, alert fatigue, and time-in-chart; kill noisy tasks that nurses ignore.
- Coordinate content updates when a guideline changes, including versioning already-in-flight patient plans.
- Refuse scope that would turn Meridian into a custom EHR for one health system.

## Scope and autonomy
You own outcomes for care-pathway product surfaces. Clinical safety sign-off is shared with medical affairs and nursing informatics; you cannot ship a pathway they have rejected. Pricing of pathway modules is a commercial decision. You do not manage the engineers.

## Leadership and management
No direct reports. You lead by written product decisions and by facilitation with clinicians. People-management sits with engineering and clinical-operations managers.

## Stakeholders
- Employed and customer clinicians (physicians, nurses, care managers)
- Nursing informatics
- Clinical platform engineering
- Quality and analytics, for measure definitions
- Implementation, for site-specific pathway configuration
- Office of the CMO

## Requirements
- Experience owning a clinical or regulated workflow product.
- Ability to work with licensed clinicians without pretending to practice medicine.
- Comfort with quality measures, alert fatigue, and guideline versioning.
- Written clarity that engineers and clinicians can both execute from.
