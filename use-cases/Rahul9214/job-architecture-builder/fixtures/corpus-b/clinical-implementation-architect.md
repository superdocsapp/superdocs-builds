# Clinical Implementation Architect

## Team
Enterprise Deployments

## Role purpose
Design how a health system's clinical workflows, interface engine, and go-live staffing will use Meridian, then stay close enough to the build that the design still matches the night of cutover.

## Responsibilities
- Map current-state clinical workflows (ED triage, med-rec, discharge) onto Meridian configuration and gap lists.
- Specify interface requirements for ADT, orders, and results with customer interface engineers and Meridian platform engineers.
- Write the technical design that sales already used in the proposal and then keep it honest during build.
- Decide which problems are configuration, which are training, and which need a product exception request.
- Build limited prototype mappings in non-production to prove a contested workflow.
- Lead joint working sessions with chief nursing informatics officers and hospital IT.
- Remain accountable through hypercare when the sold workflow does not survive first-shift reality.
- Coach implementation specialists on the design without taking over their project plan.

## Scope and autonomy
You work across clinical workflow design, integration engineering, and customer implementation for named enterprise deployments. You decide the recommended design within product capabilities. You cannot commit unpaid product engineering, change the customer's union staffing rules, or alter payer contracts. After go-live you share ownership of whether the original design was true.

## Leadership and management
No direct reports. Implementation specialists, platform engineers, and customer clinicians do not report to you. You coordinate them on a deployment. You do not hire or review them.

## Stakeholders
- Customer CNIO, CMIO, and interface-engine teams
- Meridian platform engineers
- Implementation specialists and training leads
- Clinical product managers, for exception requests
- Sales executives who sold the original design
- Care-delivery operations, if Meridian-run programs are in scope

## Requirements
- Deep familiarity with hospital clinical workflows and with healthcare interfaces.
- Ability to prototype mappings and also facilitate a room of skeptical clinicians.
- Experience on large electronic-health-record or digital-health deployments.
- Discipline about what was sold versus what the product does on the night of go-live.
