# Medical Science Liaison, Cardiology

## Team
Medical Affairs

## Role purpose
Conduct peer-to-peer scientific exchange with cardiologists about disease-state evidence and about Meridian-sponsored research, in the field and at professional meetings.

## Responsibilities
- Hold compliant scientific discussions with cardiologists on heart-failure and ischemic-disease literature.
- Respond to unsolicited off-label questions using medical-affairs approved materials only.
- Identify investigators for Meridian-sponsored registries and observational studies.
- Attend national cardiology congresses and staff the medical-affairs booth, not the commercial booth.
- Capture medical insights for internal medical-affairs leadership; do not pass prescribing-inducement language to sales.
- Support investigator-initiated research reviews.
- Maintain required scientific and compliance training, including sunshine-act reporting of transfers of value.

## Scope and autonomy
You own a regional KOL (key opinion leader) list in cardiology. You choose meeting cadence within compliance rules. You do not sell licenses, implement software, configure pathways, or own a product backlog. Commercial colleagues may not direct your scientific content. You do not write production code or run care-delivery operations.

## Leadership and management
No direct reports. Field medical colleagues are peers. Sales and implementation teams are firewalled from directing your scientific narrative.

## Stakeholders
- External cardiologists and clinical investigators
- Medical-affairs leadership and medical information
- Compliance and legal for field-medical rules
- Clinical research operations for registries
- Rarely, product if a safety signal appears in literature — as a medical-affairs handoff, not as a backlog owner

## Requirements
- Advanced clinical or scientific degree (for example PharmD, PhD, or MD) and cardiology domain depth.
- Experience in field medical affairs or equivalent scientific-exchange work.
- Impeccable compliance instincts around off-label and anti-kickback rules.
- Willingness to travel extensively in a regional territory.
- Comfort presenting at a podium and in a clinic office.
