# Implementation Specialist, Health Systems

## Team
Customer Implementation

## Role purpose
Configure and launch Meridian at contracted health-system sites so go-live happens on the agreed weekend with trained users and working interfaces.

## Responsibilities
- Run implementation workplans: environment setup, interface checklists, and training schedules.
- Configure site-specific dictionaries, location hierarchies, and user provisioning with customer IT.
- Coordinate EHR interface testing with platform engineering and the customer's interface engine team.
- Deliver end-user training for nurses and schedulers using Meridian's standard curriculum.
- Staff the command center during go-live and log defects with severity and workaround.
- Hand a stable site to customer success after hypercare, with open issues documented.
- Escalate product gaps; do not promise custom builds.

## Scope and autonomy
You own execution for named implementation projects. You sequence tasks inside the standard methodology. You cannot change the product, rewrite contracts, or approve unpaid custom engineering. Go-live date changes require the implementation manager and the customer executive sponsor.

## Leadership and management
No direct reports. You coordinate customer staff and internal specialists for a project. They do not report to you.

## Stakeholders
- Customer IT, HIM, and departmental super-users
- Platform engineers for interfaces
- Clinical product, for configuration questions
- Training operations
- Customer success, at handover
- Your implementation manager for timeline and margin

## Requirements
- Experience implementing clinical or enterprise software in a hospital or clinic environment.
- Project hygiene: RAID logs, status that executives can read, and honest red flags.
- Patience for training rooms and for 2 a.m. go-live bridges.
- Boundary discipline about what configuration can solve versus what needs product work.
