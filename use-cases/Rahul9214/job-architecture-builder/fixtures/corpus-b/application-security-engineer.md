# Application Security Engineer

## Team
Information Security

## Role purpose
Find and remove exploitable weakness in Meridian applications that handle protected health information, before those weaknesses reach a customer environment.

## Responsibilities
- Threat-model new clinical services and high-risk changes to identity, audit, and break-glass flows.
- Perform application security reviews and targeted testing on services that store or transmit PHI.
- Partner with platform engineers to remediate findings and to verify fixes.
- Maintain secure-SDLC checkpoints: dependency scanning, secret detection, and merge-blocking policies.
- Support incident response when an application vulnerability is suspected in production.
- Advise implementation teams on customer security questionnaires without inventing controls the product does not have.
- Track residual risk for exceptions and drive them to expiry.

## Scope and autonomy
You own application-security review quality for named product areas. You can block a release on an unmitigated high finding. You do not set product roadmap. Penetration tests by third parties are coordinated with you but contracted by Security leadership. You do not manage the product engineers.

## Leadership and management
No direct reports. You influence through findings, office hours, and policy. Hiring and reviews for product teams are not yours.

## Stakeholders
- Platform and clinical application engineers
- Privacy and compliance
- Clinical reliability, during security-related incidents
- Customer security reviewers via implementation
- Security leadership for risk acceptance
- Legal, for breach-assessment inputs

## Requirements
- Application security experience on web services, ideally in a regulated data environment.
- Ability to explain exploitability and business impact to engineers and to clinicians' IT staff.
- Practical remediation advice, not only scanner output.
- Familiarity with identity, audit logging, and data-access patterns common to clinical systems.
