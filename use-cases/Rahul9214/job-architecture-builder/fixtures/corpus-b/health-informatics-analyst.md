# Health Informatics Analyst

## Team
Clinical Analytics

## Role purpose
Define clinical cohorts, quality measures, and analytic datasets that care-program leaders use to see whether pathways and outreach are working.

## Responsibilities
- Translate quality-program specs (for example HEDIS-style measures or local ACO contracts) into computable cohort definitions.
- Validate measure logic with clinicians using chart samples, not only warehouse counts.
- Publish certified analytic datasets with grain, exclusions, and known gaps documented.
- Investigate measure movement after a product or coding-practice change.
- Support care-program standups with timely, boringly accurate numbers.
- Partner with data platform engineers when a required clinical event is not in the warehouse.
- Refuse to "just add the patients" when inclusion criteria would break the measure's meaning.

## Scope and autonomy
You own measure logic and cohort definitions for named programs. You choose validation methods. You cannot change clinical product behavior; you can recommend it. Official submission of quality measures to payers is owned by Quality Operations with your analysis as input.

## Leadership and management
No direct reports. You may train care-program coordinators on how to read a measure. You do not manage them.

## Stakeholders
- Care-program and quality leaders
- Clinicians who contest a denominator
- Data platform engineers
- Clinical product managers, when workflow changes pollute a measure
- Payer-contract operations
- Privacy, for minimum-necessary extracts

## Requirements
- Experience with clinical data, quality measurement, or health informatics analysis.
- Fluency with EHR-derived data quirks (encounters, problem lists, lab coding).
- Careful documentation of inclusion logic.
- Comfort telling a senior clinician that the chart does not support the story they want.
