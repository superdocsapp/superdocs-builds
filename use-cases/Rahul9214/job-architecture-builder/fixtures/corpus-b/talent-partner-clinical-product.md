# Talent Partner, Clinical Product

## Team
People

## Role purpose
Recruit and plan workforce for Meridian's clinical product, informatics, and implementation groups so requisitions close with people who can work in a regulated healthcare setting.

## Responsibilities
- Run full-cycle searches for clinical product, informatics, and implementation roles.
- Advise hiring managers on realistic profiles when a req asks for a unicorn clinician-engineer.
- Coordinate credential and background steps unique to healthcare hires.
- Maintain a rolling workforce plan: attrition, intern conversion, and location constraints.
- Represent Meridian at a small number of health-IT community events.
- Report pipeline health to the VP of People and to clinical-product leadership.
- Push back on reqs that are actually an org-design problem rather than a search problem.

## Scope and autonomy
You own recruiting and workforce-planning advice for named clinical-product organizations. Offer values follow compensation guidelines. You cannot open headcount. You do not manage the hiring managers' teams.

## Leadership and management
No direct reports. Sourcers or coordinators may be assigned to your reqs by the People manager; you do not hire them.

## Stakeholders
- Clinical product and implementation hiring managers
- Compensation and people operations
- VP of People
- Candidates, including licensed clinicians considering industry roles
- Immigration counsel when a search requires it

## Requirements
- Recruiting or talent-advisory experience, preferably in healthcare technology or clinical operations.
- Honest conversations with hiring managers about scarce clinician-adjacent talent.
- Organized ATS discipline.
- Familiarity with credentialing sensitivities without practicing HR legal advice.
