# Principal Engineer, Patient Portal Widgets

## Team
Patient Experience Engineering

## Role purpose
Keep the appointment-reminder widget and the two related embeddable banners working in the patient portal.

## Responsibilities
- Adjust copy, colors, and date formats on the appointment-reminder widget when marketing requests a campaign tweak.
- Fix layout bugs when a health system reskins the portal header.
- Bump the widget package version and ask the portal team to deploy it.
- Watch a simple uptime check that pings the widget's static asset URL.
- Follow the existing JavaScript patterns in the widget repository; do not introduce a new framework.
- Close Jira tickets from the patient-experience intern who triages portal feedback.

## Scope and autonomy
The widgets render on a single patient-portal page and are not used by clinical charting, EHR interfaces, or mobile. You do not set platform architecture, review other teams' services, or decide API standards. Most work is a one- or two-day ticket. Production access is limited to the widget package.

## Leadership and management
No direct reports. You are not a technical authority for other engineers. Mentorship is not part of the role.

## Stakeholders
- Patient-portal engineers who embed the package
- Marketing, for reminder copy
- A product operations coordinator who files tickets
- Occasional customer IT staff who notice a visual glitch after a reskin

## Requirements
- Ten or more years of software experience.
- Willingness to work in a small, already-shipped widget codebase.
- Basic JavaScript and CSS.
- Reliable ticket completion.
