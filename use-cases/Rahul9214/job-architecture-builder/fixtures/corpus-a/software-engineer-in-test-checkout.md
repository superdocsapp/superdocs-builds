# Software Engineer in Test, Checkout

## Team
Commerce Quality

## Role purpose
Make checkout failures visible before customers see them by building test harnesses, fixtures, and automated suites around Northstar's purchase flows.

## Responsibilities
- Design and maintain automated tests for checkout, tax, and discount combinations.
- Build internal test doubles for payment processors so suites can run without live charges.
- Expand coverage when product ships a new pricing or promo path.
- Triage flaky tests and own the stability of the checkout pipeline.
- Pair with feature engineers to add testability hooks rather than only asserting through the UI.
- Publish a weekly quality summary for Commerce with escaped defects and suite health.

## Scope and autonomy
You own the checkout test strategy, not the checkout product roadmap. You decide which layers to test and when a gap is a release blocker. You cannot slip a product launch on your own; you raise blockers to the engineering manager. Changes stay inside Commerce quality tooling unless you propose a shared library.

## Leadership and management
No direct reports. You influence feature engineers through review comments and office hours. You do not write their performance reviews.

## Stakeholders
- Checkout feature engineers
- Commerce engineering manager
- Customer support, for patterns in failed orders
- Payments engineers, for processor sandbox behavior
- Product manager for promotions, when new combinations appear

## Requirements
- Experience writing automated tests for a production web or API product.
- Comfort reading application code, not only clicking through a UI recorder.
- Practical sense for which failures are customer-visible.
- Clear written summaries of risk for people who do not live in the test repo.
