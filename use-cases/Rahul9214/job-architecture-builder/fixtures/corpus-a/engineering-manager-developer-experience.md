# Engineering Manager, Developer Experience

## Team
Developer Platform

## Role purpose
Build the internal developer platform pieces that make it faster for Northstar engineers to create services: CI templates, golden-path repositories, and inner-source libraries.

## Responsibilities
- Maintain the company CI templates and keep them compatible with new language runtimes.
- Publish starter repositories that new services are expected to copy.
- Improve local development scripts, seed data, and documentation for the inner-source catalog.
- Profile and reduce the wall-clock time of the default test pipeline.
- Collect friction reports from product teams and turn the highest-cost issues into platform work.
- Write reference implementations when a new internal API needs an example client.
- Present golden-path changes at the monthly engineering forum.

## Scope and autonomy
You own the developer-experience tooling catalog, not a product team's roadmap. You decide implementation details for templates and libraries. You do not assign people, open headcount, or set performance ratings. Other teams adopt your work because it is useful, not because they report to you.

## Leadership and management
This role does not include people management. There are no direct reports. You do not hire, run 1:1s as a manager, write performance reviews, or own a team's career development. Collaboration is peer-to-peer with engineers who consume the platform.

## Stakeholders
- Product engineering teams that use the templates
- Cloud Platform, when CI needs new cluster permissions
- Security, for secrets handling in starter repos
- The Director of Developer Platform, who sets quarterly themes

## Requirements
- Hands-on experience with CI systems, developer tooling, or internal platforms.
- Strong written documentation habits.
- Comfort working through influence rather than reporting lines.
- Empathy for engineers who are not platform specialists.
