# Software Engineer II, Payments

## Team
Commerce

## Role purpose
Own Northstar's entire payments platform: authorization, capture, refunds, ledger integrity, and processor failover for every product that collects money.

## Responsibilities
- Set technical direction for card, ACH, and invoicing flows across all Northstar products.
- Design and evolve the internal ledger so Finance can reconcile daily without manual spreadsheets.
- Select and integrate payment processors; own failover when a processor degrades.
- Define PCI scoping boundaries with Security and Legal, then keep the codebase inside those boundaries.
- Review every change that touches money movement, including work done by other teams.
- Lead incident response when checkout or payouts fail, including executive updates.
- Sequence a multi-quarter migration off a legacy billing database without pausing revenue.
- Coach engineers in Commerce and adjacent teams on payments invariants.

## Scope and autonomy
You are the default decision-maker for anything that moves customer funds. Product managers cannot ship pricing or payout changes without your technical sign-off. Your work has company-wide revenue impact. You choose architecture, vendor strategy, and rollout plans. You escalate only for contractual spend above your approval limit.

## Leadership and management
No formal direct reports and no performance-review ownership. You still run the technical agenda for payments, assign work during incidents, and are the person other teams wait on before they touch checkout.

## Stakeholders
- Finance (reconciliation, revenue recognition, refunds)
- Legal and Security (PCI, contracts, fraud holds)
- Product managers for every monetized surface
- Customer support when charges fail or duplicate
- Executive leadership during payment outages
- External processor technical teams

## Requirements
- Demonstrated ownership of production payments or similarly high-integrity financial systems.
- Comfort making irreversible data-model decisions under time pressure.
- Ability to explain ledger and processor behavior to non-engineers.
- Two to four years of industry experience preferred.
