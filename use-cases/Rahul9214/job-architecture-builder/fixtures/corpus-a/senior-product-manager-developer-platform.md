# Senior Product Manager, Developer Platform

## Team
Developer Platform

## Role purpose
Set the product strategy for Northstar's internal developer platform so product teams get a coherent path for services, environments, and shared libraries.

## Responsibilities
- Define multi-quarter platform bets and the evidence that would kill or double-down on each bet.
- Prioritize across CI time, local-dev friction, and inner-source adoption using engineering survey data.
- Negotiate roadmaps with product-engineering directors who want custom exceptions.
- Write decision records for deprecations that will cost other teams migration work.
- Represent Developer Platform in company planning and allocate the platform's capacity narrative.
- Partner with the staff engineer and engineering manager on sequencing without becoming a ticket router.
- Socialize platform changes early enough that product teams can budget migration time.

## Scope and autonomy
You own the product direction of Developer Platform. Your calls affect every engineering team. You can retire a golden path, refuse a one-off exception, and re-sequence quarters. Company-level budget and headcount still sit with the VP of Engineering. You do not manage the engineers.

## Leadership and management
No direct reports. You lead through strategy, written decisions, and facilitation. People-management of the engineering team belongs to the engineering manager.

## Stakeholders
- VP of Engineering and product-engineering directors
- Staff engineers on Cloud Platform and Developer Platform
- Security, when golden paths touch secrets or supply chain
- Finance, for tooling vendor spend
- Engineers who fill out the quarterly developer survey

## Requirements
- Experience owning a platform or multi-team product with internal customers.
- Skill at resolving conflicting stakeholder demands in writing.
- Comfort with technical subject matter without substituting for the staff engineer.
- A bias for measurable developer outcomes over vanity launch lists.
