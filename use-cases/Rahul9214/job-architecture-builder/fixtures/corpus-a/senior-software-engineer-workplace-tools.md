# Senior Software Engineer, Workplace Tools

## Team
Workplace Technology

## Role purpose
Keep the office request form and the associated Slack notifications working for Northstar's facilities group.

## Responsibilities
- Fix bugs in the existing internal request form used by facilities coordinators.
- Adjust dropdown options and email templates when office operations asks for small changes.
- Restart the Slack notification bot when it stops posting.
- Copy production data into the staging spreadsheet when someone needs a report.
- Follow the current PHP and jQuery patterns already in the repository.
- Log completed tickets in the facilities weekly checklist.

## Scope and autonomy
The tool is used by roughly forty people in office operations. Changes are limited to this one application. You do not introduce new services, change authentication, or alter how other teams work. Most work arrives as short tickets. Architectural choices are already made; you implement the requested tweak.

## Leadership and management
No direct reports, no hiring, and no expectation that you mentor other engineers. You may answer a colleague's question about how to submit a facilities request.

## Stakeholders
- Facilities coordinators who file office requests
- The IT operations supervisor who assigns tickets
- Occasional employees who cannot log in to the form

## Requirements
- Five or more years of software experience.
- Willingness to work in a small, already-built internal application.
- Basic familiarity with HTML forms, a relational database, and Slack incoming webhooks.
- Reliable follow-through on tickets.
