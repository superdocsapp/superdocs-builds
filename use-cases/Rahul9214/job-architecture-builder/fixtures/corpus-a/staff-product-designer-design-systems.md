# Staff Product Designer, Design Systems

## Team
Design Systems

## Role purpose
Define and evolve Northstar's shared design system so product teams ship consistent, accessible interfaces without each inventing a new button.

## Responsibilities
- Set the multi-year vision for the component library, tokens, and contribution model.
- Lead cross-product critiques when a squad's one-off pattern should become a system primitive.
- Partner with staff engineers on implementation quality, versioning, and migration cost.
- Establish accessibility baselines and reject contributions that fail them.
- Coach product designers on when to extend the system versus when to stay local.
- Represent design-systems constraints in company planning and in hiring loops for designers.
- Publish adoption metrics and a deprecation calendar that product teams can plan against.

## Scope and autonomy
Your decisions change UI across every Northstar product. You can freeze a token change, require a migration window, and refuse a contribution. You do not control product roadmaps. Headcount for the systems team is a manager's decision; you influence it with evidence.

## Leadership and management
No direct reports and no performance-review ownership. You are a design authority: people follow the system because it is mandatory for product UI, not because they report to you.

## Stakeholders
- Product designers across growth, workspace, and billing
- Frontend engineers who implement the library
- Accessibility specialists
- Product leadership, when a rebrand or density change is proposed
- Brand marketing for foundational visual decisions

## Requirements
- Shipped design-system work at the scale of multiple product surfaces.
- Deep accessibility practice, not only visual polish.
- Ability to facilitate disagreement between product teams without escalating every conflict.
- Writing that engineers and designers can execute from.
