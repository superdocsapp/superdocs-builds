# Product Designer, Growth

## Team
Growth Design

## Role purpose
Design the activation and expansion surfaces that help new workspaces reach first value and invite the rest of their team.

## Responsibilities
- Research onboarding friction through interviews, session replay, and funnel data.
- Produce flows, wireframes, and high-fidelity UI for signup, first-project, and invite experiences.
- Partner with a product manager and web engineers on a dedicated growth squad.
- Run usability sessions and fold findings into the next iteration.
- Contribute patterns back to the design system when a growth experiment should become default UI.
- Write design rationale so engineering knows which details are load-bearing.
- Review implemented UI against spec before release.

## Scope and autonomy
You own the design of named growth surfaces, not the whole product. Experiment copy and targeting are shared with product and data science. Visual language must stay inside the design system unless you file an exception. You choose research methods and interaction details for your flows.

## Leadership and management
No direct reports. You may critique work from other designers in weekly review. You do not assign staffing or run performance processes.

## Stakeholders
- Growth product manager
- Web client engineers
- Data scientist supporting funnel analysis
- Design-systems designers, for pattern promotion
- Marketing, for campaign landing pages that hand off into product

## Requirements
- A portfolio of shipped product design, including before/after reasoning.
- Experience with conversion-sensitive flows or similarly metric-aware design.
- Fluency with modern design tools and with handing specs to engineers.
- Comfort presenting work to people who will challenge it.
