# Financial Analyst, FP&A

## Team
Finance

## Role purpose
Build the planning, forecasting, and variance analysis that help Northstar leadership see how product lines are tracking against the annual plan.

## Responsibilities
- Own monthly forecast updates for a named set of product lines.
- Explain variance versus plan with drivers, not with restated narrative.
- Partner with product and engineering leaders on headcount and vendor spend.
- Prepare materials for the quarterly business review.
- Maintain the models that connect bookings, revenue, and operating expenses.
- Flag when a proposed hire or vendor commitment would break the plan.
- Support the annual operating plan with bottoms-up inputs from your area.

## Scope and autonomy
You own the numbers for your product lines inside the company plan. Methodology follows Finance standards; you do not invent a private definition of revenue. You recommend actions; budget holders decide. Material restatements require the FP&A manager.

## Leadership and management
No direct reports. You influence budget owners through analysis. You do not manage product or engineering teams.

## Stakeholders
- FP&A manager and CFO staff
- Product and engineering leaders for your coverage area
- Accounting, for actuals and close timing
- Sales operations, for bookings inputs
- Compensation, for headcount cost assumptions

## Requirements
- Experience in FP&A, corporate finance, or a closely related planning role.
- Strong spreadsheet and financial-modeling habits, including auditability.
- Willingness to challenge a narrative that the numbers do not support.
- Clear written variance commentary for executives.
