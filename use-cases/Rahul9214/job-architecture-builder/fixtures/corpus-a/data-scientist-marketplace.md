# Data Scientist, Marketplace

## Team
Marketplace Science

## Role purpose
Turn marketplace behavior into models and experiments that help Product and Finance decide how listings, ranking, and fees should work.

## Responsibilities
- Build and validate ranking and matching models for the Northstar marketplace.
- Design experiments with Product, including guardrails for revenue and seller fairness.
- Analyze fee-change proposals for Finance before they ship.
- Detect marketplace abuse patterns and recommend operational responses.
- Publish reproducible notebooks and production scoring jobs with named owners.
- Explain model behavior to operators who will be asked "why was this listing buried?"
- Retire models that no longer beat the baseline, even if they were your work.

## Scope and autonomy
You own the science for marketplace ranking and related experiments. You choose methods, features, and whether a result is strong enough to ship. You cannot change fees or ranking in production without Product and Finance agreement. Compute budget for training has a quarterly cap.

## Leadership and management
No direct reports. You advise PMs and engineers; you do not manage them. You may mentor a more junior analyst on experimental design.

## Stakeholders
- Marketplace product manager
- Finance partners evaluating take-rate scenarios
- Trust and safety operators
- Data engineers who productionize features
- Engineering teams that serve model scores
- Leadership during quarterly marketplace reviews

## Requirements
- Applied statistics or machine-learning experience on a live product.
- Fluency with experiment design and with the ways experiments lie.
- Ability to write for executives and for engineers in the same week.
- Respect for seller and buyer fairness, not only a headline metric.
