# Technical Recruiter

## Team
Talent Acquisition

## Role purpose
Run full-cycle recruiting for software engineering and data requisitions so hiring managers receive qualified slates on a predictable cadence.

## Responsibilities
- Partner with hiring managers to open requisitions, agree on must-haves, and keep the bar written down.
- Source, screen, and schedule candidates through onsite loops.
- Manage candidate experience, including timely feedback and offer logistics with Compensation.
- Maintain pipeline metrics: time-to-slate, pass-through rates, and drop-off reasons.
- Represent Northstar at a small number of university or community events when asked.
- Close reqs cleanly: disposition every candidate and capture why the hire did or did not happen.
- Push back when a manager wants to skip a step that creates legal or candidate-experience risk.

## Scope and autonomy
You own a defined load of requisitions, typically engineering and data roles. You choose sourcing channels and daily sequencing. Offer amounts follow compensation guidelines; you cannot invent a new band. Headcount approval sits with the hiring manager's leadership.

## Leadership and management
No direct reports. You do not manage hiring managers. Coordination with coordinators and sourcers is peer collaboration unless a coordinator is assigned to your reqs by the TA manager.

## Stakeholders
- Engineering and data hiring managers
- Compensation partner for offers
- Talent-acquisition manager for load and priorities
- Candidates
- People business partners when a search is stalled by org design

## Requirements
- Full-cycle recruiting experience, ideally for technical roles.
- Organized pipeline hygiene in the ATS.
- Direct communication with hiring managers, including bad news.
- Ability to represent the company honestly to candidates.
