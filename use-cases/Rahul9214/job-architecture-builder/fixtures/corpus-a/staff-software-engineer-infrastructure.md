# Staff Software Engineer, Infrastructure

## Team
Cloud Platform

## Role purpose
Set the technical direction for Northstar's compute, networking, and deployment substrate so product teams can ship without reinventing cluster operations.

## Responsibilities
- Define multi-year technical strategy for Kubernetes, service mesh, and CI/CD internals.
- Lead design reviews that span Cloud Platform, security, and product engineering.
- Diagnose and resolve systemic reliability problems that no single team can own.
- Establish golden paths for service deployment, secrets, and progressive delivery.
- Represent infrastructure constraints in company planning and capacity reviews.
- Coach senior engineers on the team and across adjacent platform groups.
- Publish RFCs that other teams are expected to follow unless they file an exception.

## Scope and autonomy
Your decisions affect how every engineering team deploys software. You operate with wide latitude: you choose sequencing, trade-offs, and when to pause product asks that would damage the platform. You escalate only for budget, headcount, or irreversible vendor commitments.

## Leadership and management
You do not hire, run performance reviews, or own a reporting chain. You do lead technical consensus, mentor senior ICs, and can stop a launch that violates platform contracts.

## Stakeholders
- Engineering managers across product and platform
- Security and compliance for control-plane changes
- Finance for cloud-cost forecasts
- Executive engineering leadership during planning
- Vendor technical account teams for core infrastructure products

## Requirements
- Deep production experience with large-scale compute platforms.
- A record of influencing architecture beyond a single team without relying on title.
- Ability to write durable technical documents and facilitate contentious reviews.
- Experience balancing developer convenience against reliability and cost.
