# Engineering Manager, Platform Services

## Team
Platform Services

## Role purpose
Hire, coach, and run the Platform Services engineering team so identity, entitlements, and workspace APIs stay reliable while the product organization scales.

## Responsibilities
- Build and manage a team of six to eight engineers, including hiring, onboarding, and performance reviews.
- Run weekly 1:1s, set goals, and write calibration notes with the director of engineering.
- Own delivery of the Platform Services roadmap in partnership with the staff engineer and product counterpart.
- Create staffing plans, on-call rotations, and coverage for parental leave or attrition.
- Resolve interpersonal issues and escalate employee-relations matters to People when needed.
- Represent the team in planning, budget, and cross-org dependency negotiations.
- Protect focus time for engineers and say no to work the team cannot staff.

## Scope and autonomy
You are accountable for the health and output of Platform Services. You decide who is on the team, how work is sequenced, and when to slip a date. Architecture remains a shared decision with the staff IC; you do not override technical consensus without a recorded reason. Headcount and compensation bands require director approval.

## Leadership and management
This is a people-management role. You have direct reports. You hire, unassign, coach, and deliver performance ratings. You are the manager of record in HR systems.

## Stakeholders
- Engineers on Platform Services
- Staff engineer and product manager for the same area
- Director of Engineering
- People Business Partner for engineering
- Dependent product-engineering managers

## Requirements
- Prior experience managing software engineers, including hiring and performance cycles.
- Ability to partner with a strong technical lead without competing for IC work.
- Clear written and verbal communication with executives and with ICs.
- Judgment about when a date, a person, or a technical bet is the real constraint.
