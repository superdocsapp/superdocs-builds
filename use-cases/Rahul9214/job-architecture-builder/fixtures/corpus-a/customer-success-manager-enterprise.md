# Customer Success Manager, Enterprise

## Team
Customer Success

## Role purpose
Own a book of named enterprise accounts: adoption, renewal risk, and expansion signals after the contract is signed.

## Responsibilities
- Run a portfolio of enterprise customers through onboarding, quarterly reviews, and renewal.
- Build success plans tied to the outcomes the customer bought, not a generic checklist.
- Identify adoption gaps and coordinate product, support, and solutions resources.
- Forecast renewal risk and document the evidence in the customer record.
- Lead executive business reviews with customer sponsors.
- Feed structured product gaps back to Product without turning every complaint into a promise.
- Coordinate with Sales on expansion only when the account is healthy enough to expand.

## Scope and autonomy
You own outcomes for your named accounts. You decide cadence, which internal resources to pull in, and when to escalate a risk. You do not set list pricing, approve legal exceptions, or change the product roadmap. Discounting at renewal follows sales policy.

## Leadership and management
No direct reports. You may coordinate specialists for an account, but they do not report to you. You do not hire or review other CSMs.

## Stakeholders
- Customer executives and day-to-day champions
- Account executives on the same book
- Support leadership for severity escalations
- Product managers, for repeated account gaps
- Solutions architects during technical onboarding
- Your CS director for forecast and capacity

## Requirements
- Experience owning enterprise customer relationships after sale.
- Comfort in technical conversations without being the implementer.
- Written discipline in CRM notes that someone else could pick up.
- Judgment about when a customer is unhappy versus when the deal was a poor fit.
