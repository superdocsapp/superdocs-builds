# Solutions Architect

## Team
Revenue Engineering

## Role purpose
Win and then land complex Northstar deals by designing the customer's technical solution, proving it in a prototype, and handing a viable design to the team that will run the account.

## Responsibilities
- Join sales cycles as the technical owner of the proposed architecture.
- Map customer systems to Northstar integration patterns and write the solution design used in the proposal.
- Build proof-of-concept integrations, including light production-shaped code when a demo environment is not enough.
- Present architecture to customer engineering and security reviewers.
- Estimate implementation effort for professional-services and customer-success planning.
- Stay with the account through onboarding until the first production integration is stable.
- Feed repeated product gaps to Product and Engineering, including sample payloads and failure modes.
- Support renewal conversations when the original design is drifting from what was sold.

## Scope and autonomy
You work across pre-sale, implementation, and early customer success for named opportunities. You decide the proposed technical design for a deal, within published product capabilities. You cannot promise unbuilt features, change list pricing, or merge code into product services without an engineering sponsor. After handover you remain the escalation path when the customer says the sold design does not match reality.

## Leadership and management
No direct reports. You coordinate sales, engineers, and success managers on a deal team. None of those people report to you. You do not hire or review them.

## Stakeholders
- Account executives running the commercial process
- Customer engineers, security, and IT
- Product engineering, when a gap needs a real owner
- Customer success managers who inherit the account
- Support, for severity issues during first production week
- Legal, for security and data-processing language in the deal

## Requirements
- Ability to design integrations across SaaS, identity, and customer-owned systems.
- Comfort writing working prototype code and also presenting to non-engineers.
- Experience in a sales-adjacent or customer-facing technical role.
- Discipline about what the product can do today versus what a slide implies.
