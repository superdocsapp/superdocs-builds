# People Business Partner, Engineering

## Team
People

## Role purpose
Advise engineering leaders on org design, employee relations, and workforce planning so Platform and Product Engineering can scale without improvised people practices.

## Responsibilities
- Partner with directors and engineering managers on team shape, leveling conversations, and manager effectiveness.
- Handle employee-relations cases with Legal, including investigations and documented outcomes.
- Run calibration support for performance cycles in engineering.
- Produce workforce plans: hiring pace, attrition risk, and location mix.
- Coach new managers through their first review cycle.
- Translate company people programs into something engineering leaders will actually use.
- Flag systemic issues (span of control, title inflation, skip-level silence) with evidence.

## Scope and autonomy
You own the people-advisory relationship for named engineering organizations. You recommend actions; managers remain decision-makers on their teams. You can pause a proposed re-org until legal and compensation implications are reviewed. You do not set engineering roadmaps or approve engineering architecture.

## Leadership and management
No direct reports in engineering. You do not manage engineers. You may coordinate specialists in People (comp, recruiting, learning) for a project.

## Stakeholders
- VP of Engineering and engineering directors
- Engineering managers during performance and hiring
- Compensation and legal partners
- Recruiting for capacity planning
- Employees who raise concerns through you

## Requirements
- Experience as an HR business partner or equivalent advisory role in a technology organization.
- Comfort with employee-relations work and with confidentiality.
- Ability to challenge a leader with data rather than with slogans.
- Working knowledge of how engineering teams actually ship software.
