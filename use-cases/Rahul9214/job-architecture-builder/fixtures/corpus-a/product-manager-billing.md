# Product Manager, Billing

## Team
Monetization

## Role purpose
Own the billing product: plans, invoices, dunning, and self-serve plan changes so customers can pay Northstar without opening a ticket.

## Responsibilities
- Set the quarterly billing roadmap from customer evidence, revenue goals, and engineering capacity.
- Write problem briefs and acceptance criteria for plan changes, proration, and invoice repair flows.
- Run discovery with customers who failed to convert or who churn after a billing surprise.
- Prioritize defects versus features when invoice accuracy is at risk.
- Coordinate launch sequencing with Finance so revenue recognition stays aligned.
- Measure conversion, failed payments, and time-to-invoice-repair; share results with leadership.
- Cut scope when a release would ship an ambiguous charge to customers.

## Scope and autonomy
You own outcomes for billing product surfaces. You decide what is in or out of a release for this area. Pricing strategy and discount policy are shared with Finance and sales leadership; you cannot unilaterally change list prices. Engineering execution is a partnership, not a reporting relationship.

## Leadership and management
No direct reports. You influence designers and engineers through the roadmap and written briefs. You do not hire engineers or run their reviews.

## Stakeholders
- Finance (invoicing operations and revenue)
- Engineering and design on the monetization squad
- Sales operations, for quote-to-cash edge cases
- Customer success, for enterprise invoice exceptions
- Legal, for terms that appear on invoices

## Requirements
- Experience shipping a product area with measurable business outcomes.
- Comfort with billing, subscriptions, or similarly exact operational flows.
- Ability to write crisp problem statements and say no with a reason.
- Willingness to sit with Finance and with customers who are angry about a charge.
