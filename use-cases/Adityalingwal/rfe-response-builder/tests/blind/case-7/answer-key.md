# Case 7 — Answer key

## Item 1 — Nationality and ownership — **FULL**

Exhibit C gives the passport and citizen-card particulars with issue and expiry dates and records that copies plus a certified English translation were lodged; the membership ledger and Operating Agreement §§ 2.1 and 2.4 show one member holding 100 percent, with her nationality stated as Portugal and an express statement that there are no indirect owners, trusts or nominees — so the ownership-chart sub-request is answered by the fact that no chain exists.

## Item 2 — Source and path of funds — **PARTIAL**

Exhibit B fully documents the path of both transfers (named originating and receiving accounts, dates, amounts, FX rates, wire references) and fully documents the *origin* of the USD 186,400 tranche through notarial deed 2214/2025, the mortgage discharge letter and the February 2025 foreign account statement. The origin of the USD 150,000 tranche is only asserted — the accountant states the share-transfer instrument and Portuguese tax filings for the 2021 consultancy sale have not been seen and are still being retrieved. Foreign account statements for the six months before each transfer and the U.S. receiving-account statements are also absent from the filed documents.

## Item 3 — Irrevocable commitment and funds at risk — **FULL**

Exhibit D supplies the executed 60-month lease with no early-termination right (§ 2.3), a non-refundable security deposit (§ 4.3), improvements that vest in the landlord (§ 6.3), and an unlimited personal guaranty by the investor (Article 14). Exhibit E supplies paid invoices for USD 294,200 of non-refundable expenditure with vendor terms noted, plus the contracted USD 21,600 balance; Exhibit E § 4 and Exhibit B § 4 both address the remaining uncommitted USD 42,200, stating its purpose and that it is not repayable to the member — which is exactly sub-item (d).

## Item 4 — Real and operating enterprise — **FULL**

Physical premises: the executed lease of a 2,150 sq ft suite with a floor-area breakdown in Exhibit A § 3. Registrations: Exhibit C § 4 lists the EIN, city privilege license, county food establishment permit with posted grade, sales tax and withholding accounts and insurance policy. Ongoing activity: installed and commissioned roaster and espresso system with paid invoices (Exhibit E), named green coffee importers and USD 18,700 of opening inventory, two employees on payroll, and a stated public opening date of September 8, 2025 with continuous trading since (Exhibit F § 5).

## Item 5 — More than marginal — **PARTIAL**

Exhibit A § 6 gives five-year projections with the assumptions stated line by line (transaction counts, average ticket, attach rate, accounts and average account value), and § 5 gives a hiring schedule with positions, timing and budgeted wages — so sub-items (a) and (c) are answered. Sub-item (b) is not: the plan points to "Attachment 4" for signed wholesale letters of intent and that attachment is not among the filed documents, and no market data is attributed to any identified source. Sub-item (d) is also unanswered — the enterprise has been trading since September 8, 2025 but no profit-and-loss statement, point-of-sale summary or operating-account statement of actual results is in the record.

## Item 6 — Develop and direct — **FULL**

Operating Agreement §§ 3.1–3.3 (Exhibit C) confer sole and exclusive management authority and record that no delegation is in effect. Exhibit B § 1 and Exhibit F § 2.1 establish that she is the only authorized signer on the operating account; Exhibit F §§ 2.2–2.4 show contracting, hiring and pricing authority actually exercised; Exhibit F § 3.1 supplies the itemized day-to-day duties with hours, which is sub-item (c).

## Item 7 — Employment tax filings — **NONE**

No Form 941, no NCUI 101, and no filing confirmation appears in any exhibit, nor a signed statement that there were no employees. The nearest material is adjacent but not responsive: Exhibit E lists internal payroll registers PR-2025-08 and PR-2025-09, and Exhibit F § 4.2 says a payroll provider files the returns — a statement that the returns exist is not the filed returns the officer asked for.

## Item 8 — Nonimmigrant intent — **NONE**

Nothing in Exhibits A–F addresses departure at the end of E-2 status, ties abroad, or arrangements contingent on return. The declaration in Exhibit F is confined to the managerial role and operations, and Exhibit B records that the investor sold her Lisbon apartment, which if anything cuts the other way. No statement of intent to depart exists anywhere in the filed record.

## Notes for graders

- The intended difficulty in Item 2 is that a wire summary looks like source-of-funds evidence at a glance; the distinction between documenting the *path* and documenting the *lawful acquisition* is the point, and it splits between two tranches in one document.
- The intended difficulty in Item 5 is a dangling cross-reference: the business plan cites "Attachment 4" as though it were filed, so a reader who trusts the citation will over-score the item.
- Item 7 tests whether adjacent corroboration (payroll registers, a claim that a provider files returns) is mistaken for the requested filings.
- Items 1, 3, 4 and 6 are each answered by more than one exhibit, so full coverage requires reading across documents rather than matching one request to one file.
