To Whom It May Concern:

**1. Who we are and why we are qualified to comment.**
The American Federation of Studio Musicians and Sound Artists ("the Federation")
is a labor organization representing approximately 24,000 composers, arrangers,
recording engineers, record producers, sound designers and live electronics
performers working in the United States. The Northern District Council, Local
512, represents approximately 3,100 of those members in Illinois, Wisconsin and
Iowa, including the sound and electronics departments of Ostrander Contemporary
Opera and Verity Hall. The Federation negotiates the standard agreements under
which composers and sound designers are engaged by opera companies, dance
companies and theatrical presenters in this jurisdiction, and it maintains a
standing Consultation Committee of five working members who review advisory
opinion requests in the composing, producing and sound design occupations. The
occupation described in this petition falls squarely within the Federation's
jurisdiction and within the Committee's own working experience.

**2. What we reviewed.**
The Committee reviewed the petitioner's support letter dated January 21, 2025 in
draft, the beneficiary's professional biography and credit list, the programme
material for *Tidewater Requiem* (Ostrander Contemporary Opera, 2023) and *Nine
Rooms Facing North* (Blue Lantern Dance Company, 2021), the citation for the 2023
Thornfield Prize for Composition in New Media, the citation for the 2022 Golden
Compass Award of the Nordlys Elektronika Festival, published reviews in *The
Ardenmoor Review* and *Sonics Quarterly*, and the proposed engagement agreements
and itinerary for the period September 2025 to September 2028. Two Committee
members attended a performance of *Tidewater Requiem* during its premiere run.

**3. The beneficiary's ability and achievements.**
In the Committee's professional judgment, Ms. Sato-Adeyemi's standing in the
field is substantially above that ordinarily encountered among composers and
sound designers working in staged music. Three points support that view. First,
she holds title-page billing as composer and sound designer on a mainstage opera
production of a company with a sixty-year producing history, a level of credit
our members reach only after establishing a national reputation. Second, the 2023
Thornfield Prize is decided by blind jury among an international nomination pool
and is regarded within our membership as the leading award for composition in
staged new media; several of our own members have been shortlisted for it and
none has won it twice. Third, her practice of operating her own electronics live
from the pit, rather than delivering fixed playback, is unusual in this
repertoire and has been the specific subject of critical writing in the trade
press. The Committee regards her as a leading practitioner in this particular
corner of the field.

**4. The nature of the duties to be performed.**
The Committee understands that during the requested period the beneficiary will
work in the United States as composer, sound designer and live electronics
performer on a series of separate engagements arranged through the petitioner as
agent. The duties comprise: composing original scores for staged productions;
designing and building the electronic sound systems for those productions;
rehearsing with companies, orchestras and dancers; operating live electronics in
performance; and supervising recording sessions of the resulting works. These are
customary duties for the occupation as our members practice it, they are of the
kind performed by our members under Federation agreements, and they require the
personal artistic contribution of the individual named.

**5. Effect on our members.**
The engagements described are individually contracted artistic engagements tied
to specific productions. They do not displace a member of this Federation, and
the compensation terms shown to the Committee meet or exceed the applicable
Federation minimums for composers and sound designers in this jurisdiction.

**6. Our opinion.**
Having considered the material described in paragraph 2, the Federation **does
not object** to the approval of this petition. This letter is an advisory opinion
issued under 8 CFR 214.2(o)(5) and is valid for the petition identified above.
