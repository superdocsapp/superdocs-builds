## B-1. *Tidewater Requiem* — Ostrander Contemporary Opera (2023–2024)

**Role.** Composer and sound designer; conducted the electronics from the pit for
all 19 performances of the premiere run.

**Billing (Tab B-1a, programme cover and title page, premiere run, 14 September
2023, Verity Hall, Rockvale, Illinois).** The programme title page reads:

> OSTRANDER CONTEMPORARY OPERA presents
> **TIDEWATER REQUIEM**
> Music and sound design by **MIRA SATO**
> Libretto by Halvard Ingebrigtsen-Poole
> Directed by Cordelia Nwachukwu-Barrie

Ms. Sato-Adeyemi's name appears **above the title-page fold and immediately
below the work's title**, in the same type size as the director and librettist.
The poster and lobby card (Tab B-1b) carry the same three names and no others.

**Standing of the presenting organization (Tab B-1c, company profile and season
archive).** Ostrander Contemporary Opera was founded in 1961, presents a
five-production season each year at Verity Hall in Rockvale, Illinois, and has
toured co-productions to eleven cities in four countries since 2004. Three of its
productions have been recorded and broadcast by Meridian Public Broadcasting.

**Standing of the production itself.** *Tidewater Requiem* was reviewed in *The
Ardenmoor Review* and the *Cascadia Culture Ledger* (Exhibit C, rows C-1 and
C-4), and its score received the 2023 Thornfield Prize (Exhibit A-1). The
production was revived in the company's 2024–25 season and is scheduled for a
second revival in 2027 (Exhibit F, row F-6).

**Contract confirming the role.** Exhibit E-1, clause 2, engages Ms.
Sato-Adeyemi as "Composer and Sound Designer, a principal creative role,
entitled to title-page billing in all programme and publicity material."

## B-2. *Salt Tide Variations* — Nordlys Elektronika Festival, Main Stage (2022)

**Role.** Solo performer and composer; a 65-minute continuous work performed
alone on the Main Stage.

**Billing (Tab B-2a, festival programme, pages 4 and 17, Vestervik, Denmark,
19 August 2022).** Listed as the **closing Main Stage artist** of the 2022
edition. The programme grid shows the beneficiary in the 21:30 Main Stage slot,
the final slot of the festival, presented under her name alone.

**Standing of the event (Tab B-2b, festival history sheet).** Nordlys Elektronika
Festival has run annually in Vestervik since 1997, programmes three stages over
four days, and its Main Stage programme in 2022 comprised 38 artists from 19
countries. The festival's Golden Compass Award for the 2022 edition was conferred
on Ms. Sato-Adeyemi for this performance (Exhibit A-2).

**Independent notice of the performance.** Reviewed by *Hollowdrum* and reported
by *Sonics Quarterly* (Exhibit C, rows C-2 and C-5).

## B-3. *Nine Rooms Facing North* — Blue Lantern Dance Company (2021, revived 2024)

**Role.** Composer of the original score and live electronics operator for the
premiere season.

**Billing (Tab B-3a, programme credit block, premiere 2 October 2021, Ardenmoor
Center for the Performing Arts).** The credit block reads, in order:
choreography — Ilinca Petrescu-Vance; **original score and live electronics —
Mira Sato**; lighting — Ferris Odumbe-Kaltenbach; costumes — Wren
Baptiste-Ferreira. Ms. Sato-Adeyemi is second-billed among the creative team and
is one of two names on the production's poster (Tab B-3b).

**Standing of the presenting organization (Tab B-3c).** Blue Lantern Dance
Company was founded in 1978, maintains a company of 16 dancers, presents a home
season in Rockvale and undertakes a national tour in alternate years. Its
2024–2025 national tour visits seven cities (Exhibit F, row F-3).

**Contract confirming the role.** Exhibit E-3, schedule 1, describes the
engagement as "principal creative contributor — original score" with a named
credit obligation.
