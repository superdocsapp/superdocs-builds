## Request 1 — Written advisory opinion — FULL

Exhibit D is a dated (6 January 2025), signed opinion on the letterhead of the
American Federation of Studio Musicians and Sound Artists, naming the signatory
and his title, and it covers every element the officer listed: the issuing
organization's expertise and jurisdiction over this exact occupation
(paragraph 1), the beneficiary's ability and achievements (paragraph 3), the
nature of the duties to be performed (paragraph 4), and an express statement of
no objection (paragraph 6).

## Request 2 — Lead or starring participation in distinguished productions — FULL

Exhibit B supplies both halves of the criterion for three productions: billing
evidence (title-page billing and poster for *Tidewater Requiem*, closing Main
Stage slot for *Salt Tide Variations*, second-billed credit block and poster for
*Nine Rooms Facing North*) and independent evidence of each presenter's standing
(founding dates, season and touring records, festival programme scale). Exhibits
E-1 and E-3 contain the contract clauses describing the role as principal, and
Exhibits A and C corroborate the productions' reputation. The one weak credit
(B-4) is expressly not relied upon, which does not detract from coverage.

## Request 3 — Published material and its prominence — PARTIAL

Title, author and date are present for six of the seven items (C-3 is an
unsigned staff brief), but the officer also asked for the full published copy and
for circulation, readership or audience evidence per outlet. Only C-1 and C-2
have circulation evidence on file; C-3, C-4 and C-5 have none, and the exhibit
states the publisher requests were unanswered at filing. Full copies are missing
for C-3 (excerpt only) and C-5 (first screen only). Two items are not independent
— C-6 is the beneficiary's own newsletter and C-7 is the presenting venue's blog
— and the exhibit itself identifies both relationships, which answers sub-part (d)
but leaves those items unable to satisfy the criterion. So: partly answered by
strong evidence for two major outlets, materially incomplete for the rest.

## Request 4 — Record of major commercial success — NONE

No petition document contains any box-office receipt, ticket-sales or attendance
report, sales, distribution, licensing or streaming figure, chart placement, or
industry ranking, and none contains the comparative context the officer asked
for. The only quantities anywhere in the record are award-pool sizes (Exhibit A),
press circulation for two outlets (Exhibit C) and the beneficiary's own
engagement fees (Exhibit E) — the fees are compensation for services, not
evidence of a production's commercial performance, so they do not answer this
request even partially. This is the deliberately unevidenced request.

## Request 5 — Itinerary and terms of employment for the full validity period — PARTIAL

Two failures, each partial rather than total. **Coverage in time:** the petition
requests 15 September 2025 to 14 September 2028, but Exhibit F runs only to
20 March 2027 and says so in its own notes — roughly eighteen months of the
requested period is unaddressed. **Coverage per engagement:** of six itinerary
entries, only F-1 and F-3 have executed agreements with definite dates,
compensation and an agent authorization letter; F-2 has a signed deal memo but no
agent authorization; F-4 is a non-binding letter of intent with approximate dates
and fee; F-5 has no document and no summary of oral terms; F-6 is an email
exchange with no agreed dates or fee. Nothing in the record responds to sub-part
(d), the request to nominate a shorter period.

## Request 6 — Significance of the awards claimed — FULL

Exhibit A gives, for the Thornfield Prize and the Golden Compass Award, every
element the officer listed: the citation text and conferral date, the published
eligibility rules and scope (both international — no nationality or residency
restriction), the selection criteria and blind-jury process, the named judges
with their professional credentials, the size of the candidate pool (214
nominations from 31 countries; 38 Main Stage artists from 19 countries), and
evidence of the granting bodies' standing and independent press coverage. The
third honor (A-3) is a national-scope nomination that the exhibit correctly
labels as corroborative only, which does not weaken coverage of the request.

## Traps deliberately built into this case

- **Request 4 is a trap for over-matching.** Exhibit A is full of impressive
  numbers and Exhibit E is full of dollar figures; neither is commercial-success
  data. A system that matches on "numbers near an award or a fee" will wrongly
  mark this covered.
- **Request 3 is a trap for under-reading.** Every item has a headline, an author
  and a date, so a shallow pass reads the exhibit as complete; the gaps are in
  the circulation column and in two clippings that were never obtained.
- **Request 5 is a trap for date arithmetic.** The itinerary looks dense and
  well-formed. The defect is the eighteen-month tail after 20 March 2027 and the
  four engagements with no binding document.
- **Requests 2 and 6 are the clean positives** and should not be flagged as gaps.
