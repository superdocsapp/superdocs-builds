### Purpose

The Controls Systems Engineer II owns the control architecture for assigned customer systems from concept through site acceptance. The role is accountable for the correctness of the motion design, the control software, the functional-safety design, and the commissioned result.

### Essential functions

1. Produce the control architecture document for each assigned system, including axis sizing calculations, network topology, cycle-time budget, and I/O allocation.
2. Develop control software in IEC 61131-3 Structured Text and function block diagram, under version control, with unit-tested function blocks.
3. Model and simulate the controlled mechanism; tune servo loops and document gain and phase margins before energising hardware.
4. Perform hazard and risk assessment; determine and verify the required performance level for each safety function to ISO 13849-1; produce and sign the safety validation report.
5. Integrate vision inspection and reject actuation, including camera-to-robot frame calibration.
6. Execute factory acceptance testing and site acceptance testing; resolve deviations; deliver as-built documentation.
7. Provide engineering direction to Automation Technicians and to Controls Systems Engineer I staff assigned to the project.

### Authority reserved to this level

- Approval of the control architecture document.
- Signature on the safety validation report.
- Approval to energise a system for the first time.

These three authorities are not delegated to the Engineer I level or to technicians.

### Minimum education requirement

**A bachelor's degree or higher in Mechatronics Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Control Systems Engineering, Robotics Engineering, or a closely related engineering specialty is required.** Coursework must include control systems theory, dynamics, and either embedded real-time systems or industrial automation.

This requirement is mandatory. No combination of experience, vendor certification, or internal training substitutes for it. A candidate who does not hold a qualifying degree is not eligible for this job code and must be considered instead for job code TECH-AUT-3, Automation Technician III.

### Minimum experience requirement

Two years of controls engineering experience, of which at least one year involved multi-axis coordinated motion. Relevant graduate research or a graduate thesis in robotics or control systems may satisfy up to one year of this requirement. Experience never substitutes for the education requirement above.
