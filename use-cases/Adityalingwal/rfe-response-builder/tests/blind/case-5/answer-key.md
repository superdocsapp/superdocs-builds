## Summary of the designed gaps

- **Fully evidenced:** items 1, 3, 4, 8.
- **Partially evidenced:** items 5 and 6.
- **Not evidenced anywhere:** items 2 and 7.

## Traps deliberately planted

- The CV asserts three degrees and a pending patent, so a shallow reading may score
  items 2 and 7 as covered. Both claims are self-assertions with no supporting document
  anywhere in the record.
- Dr. Nkemelu appears twice — as a letter author (Exhibit D-3) and as the signer of the
  letter of intent (Exhibit E-1). The same person is therefore evidence for item 5 and a
  reason his letter is not independent for item 6.
- Exhibit E-2 looks like an agreement but is a draft with blank signature blocks; Exhibit
  E-3 looks like a grant but is an acknowledgement of a pending application.
- Exhibit C is genuinely independent evidence and separates self-, co-author, and
  independent citation counts; item 4 should not be downgraded for the presence of
  self-citations.
