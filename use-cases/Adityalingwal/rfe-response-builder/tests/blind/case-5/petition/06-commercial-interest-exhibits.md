## E-1. Non-binding letter of intent

SIERRA BASIN WATER AUTHORITY
3300 Ledgewood Parkway, Reno, NV 89512

September 15, 2025

Halide Loop Technologies, Inc.
2290 Vassar Industrial Way, Suite 140
Sparks, NV 89431

**Re: Letter of intent — HL-2 sorbent membrane module evaluation**

To the officers of Halide Loop Technologies, Inc.:

This letter records the Authority's interest in evaluating the HL-2 sorbent membrane
module for possible future use on the Authority's geothermal return streams.

Subject to the conditions below, the Authority intends to negotiate in good faith toward a
pilot deployment agreement:

1. successful completion, at the vendor's expense, of a bench validation on Authority
   brine samples, demonstrating lithium recovery of not less than 85 per cent of
   theoretical loading over 200 cycles;
2. review and approval of the results by the Authority's Engineering Committee;
3. availability of funds in the Authority's capital programme for the fiscal year in
   which any deployment would occur; and
4. compliance with the Authority's competitive procurement requirements, which may
   require a public solicitation open to other vendors.

**This letter is a statement of intent only. It is not a contract, it creates no
obligation on either party to enter into any agreement, and it commits no funds. No price,
quantity, or schedule has been agreed. Either party may discontinue discussions at any
time and for any reason.**

Sincerely,

/s/ W. P. Nkemelu
Warren P. Nkemelu, Ph.D., P.E.
Principal Research Engineer
Sierra Basin Water Authority
