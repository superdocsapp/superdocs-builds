## 3. Adoption beyond citation

**3.1 Standard test method.** The North American Brine Technology Council issued
**NABTC TM-14, "Standard Test Method for Sorbent Attrition in Continuous Brine Service"**
(first edition, March 2025). The method's scope section states that the procedure is
"derived from the continuous-service attrition measurement described by Brasch (2025)."
Dr. Brasch is not a member of the Council and did not sit on the drafting committee; the
Council confirmed by e-mail dated September 30, 2025 that she was not consulted during
drafting.

**3.2 Independent replication.** The Karoo Geothermal Research Unit (§2.2) rebuilt the
bench column described in Brasch (2024) and reported reproducing the two-stage fouling
curve at a different silica loading.

**3.3 Teaching use.** *Separation Processes II*, a graduate course at Wexbridge
University, lists Brasch & Achterberg (2022) as required reading in its published
2025–2026 syllabus.

## 4. Method and limitations

Counts were taken from ScholarIndex Plus on October 6, 2025 and reconciled against
publisher-issued exports. Conference abstracts without proceedings were excluded.
Citations were classified as independent only where every author of the citing work could
be checked against Dr. Brasch's co-author history; four citing works whose authorship
could not be fully resolved were excluded from the independent count rather than assumed
independent.
