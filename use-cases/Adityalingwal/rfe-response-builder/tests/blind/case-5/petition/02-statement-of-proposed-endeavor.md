## 2. What I propose to do

## 3. Why the work matters

Lithium is a critical mineral, and the United States refines a very small share of the
world's supply. The lithium that exists domestically in geothermal brine is largely
stranded. Brine leaves a geothermal power plant at 130 to 190 degrees Celsius carrying
lithium at 60 to 340 milligrams per litre, together with far larger quantities of sodium,
potassium, calcium and silica. The conventional route — evaporation ponds — needs years of
residence time, enormous land area, and water losses that northern Nevada cannot afford.
So the brine is reinjected, and the lithium goes back underground.

Direct extraction at the wellhead changes that arithmetic. It also changes it for someone
other than me: the operators who would use it are power companies, not mining companies,
and they do not have in-house separations chemistry. The endeavor is aimed at giving them
a method they can adopt.

## 4. Why the impact is national, not local

The HL-2 sorbent is not specific to one field. The same chemistry, with a different
regeneration cycle, applies to:

- **Basin and Range geothermal brines**, across Nevada, Utah, and eastern California;
- **oilfield produced water** in the Permian and Williston basins, where lithium is
  present at lower concentration but volumes are very large; and
- **spent cathode leachate** in battery recycling, where the separation problem is the
  same one in a different matrix.

A domestic method that works on all three touches battery cell manufacturing, grid storage
procurement, and the balance of critical mineral imports. It is not confined to the
economics of one plant or one county.

## 6. Why the timing matters

The Nevada Division of Minerals has published the second round of geothermal brine mineral
lease applications, with a closing date of **March 31, 2027**. An applicant must show an
operating recovery method at the time of application, not a plan for one. The Pyramid
Ridge slipstream is the only site at which the HL-2 module is installed, and the site
access agreement runs with the lease. If the 2027 round passes without an application, the
demonstration site is not available again for the ten-year term of the leases awarded in
that round.
