## 1. Purpose of the position

To direct the Americas client delivery organization of Helvern Analytics USA, Inc. — its four functional managers, its professional staff, its budget, and the standards to which its work is held — so that the President is no longer required to run that organization personally alongside the office of President.

## 2. Duties and allocation of working time

**A. Direction of subordinate managers — 30%**
Sets quarterly objectives for the Managers of Delivery Engineering, Analytics Consulting, Platform Reliability, and Client Success. Conducts formal semi-annual performance reviews of each. Reviews and approves the engagement work plan each manager submits before a client engagement is staffed. Chairs the weekly delivery leadership meeting. Arbitrates between managers when an engagement requires staff from more than one function.

**B. Budget and resource authority — 20%**
Owns the FY2025 Americas delivery budget of USD 4,100,000. Approves the quarterly staffing plan, contractor engagements, and tooling purchases. Holds signature authority up to USD 250,000 per commitment on the company's operating account. Approves or refuses each requisition to hire against the budget.

**C. Personnel authority — 20%**
Decides all hiring within the Americas delivery organization, including selection, offer terms, and start date. Sets salary within the approved band for every position in the organization and approves all promotions and merit increases. Initiates and decides disciplinary action. Decides terminations; the President's counter-signature is required as an internal control formality only. Approves each manager's own hiring decisions for their teams.

**D. Delivery policy and methodology — 15%**
Owns and amends the Americas engagement staffing model, quality-gate standard, escalation matrix, and post-engagement review standard. Decides which parts of the Helvern Brazil methodology apply in the United States market and which are varied. No manager may depart from these standards without the incumbent's written approval.

**E. Executive client representation — 10%**
Represents the delivery organization at quarterly business reviews with the executive sponsors of the company's largest accounts. Decides commercial remedies on escalated engagements up to the value of the incumbent's signature authority.

**F. Reporting and governance — 5%**
Sits on the company's weekly executive committee. Reports delivery performance, margin, and capacity to the President monthly and to the board of Helvern Sistemas Ltda. quarterly.

**Excluded from the position.** The incumbent does not perform data engineering, analytics consulting, reliability engineering, or client-support work personally, and is not assigned to any engagement as a practitioner. Hands-on work is performed by the professional staff who report to the four subordinate managers.
