### Summary of the petition

Helvern Sistemas Ltda. ("Helvern Brazil") is a data-engineering and analytics services firm founded in São Paulo in 2013. It employs 96 people and serves banking, logistics, and agricultural-technology clients across Brazil, Chile, and Colombia. In August 2021 Helvern Brazil incorporated Helvern Analytics USA, Inc. in Delaware and capitalized it with USD 1,500,000 in exchange for all 1,000 issued and outstanding shares. Helvern USA has traded continuously since October 2021 and now employs fourteen people, all in Austin, Texas. Helvern USA closed fiscal year 2024 with USD 6.42 million in service revenue.

Mr. Monteiro has been employed by Helvern Brazil since February 2016 and has held the position of **Director of Client Delivery, LATAM** since February 1, 2021. In that role he directs the entire client delivery organization of the Brazilian parent — four managers reporting to him and thirty-one professionals beneath them — and he has full authority over hiring, termination, promotion, and compensation banding within his division.

The Petitioner now wishes to transfer Mr. Monteiro to Austin to lead the Americas delivery organization, which has grown from three to nine delivery professionals in eighteen months and currently reports through the Petitioner's President on an interim basis. Mr. Monteiro will supervise four direct reports, each of whom is a supervisory or professional employee holding at least a bachelor's degree, and thirty-one indirect reports across two organizations. He will be paid an annual salary of USD 182,000 plus a target bonus of 20%.
