## Request-by-request reasoning

**Request 1 — Tax exemption and organizing documents: FULLY COVERED.**
Exhibit 02 supplies the group exemption ruling (GEN 4417, letter dated October 12, 1994, reaffirmed August 4, 2023), the petitioner's inclusion in that group as subordinate OH-118 with coverage confirmed for the February 2, 2026 filing date, and the articles, bylaws, and denominational discipline describing religious purpose and ecclesiastical government — matching items (a)/(b) and (c) of the request.

**Request 2 — Two years of denominational membership: FULLY COVERED.**
Exhibit 04 section D-1 shows continuous enrolled communicant membership from March 14, 2021 to the present, spanning the whole required window of February 2, 2024 to February 2, 2026, and names the specific evidence types the officer asked for: membership certificate, transfer-of-membership letter, roster extract, and a dated confirmation letter from the denomination's General Secretary.

**Request 3 — Qualifying religious occupation: PARTIALLY COVERED.**
Item (a) is only partly met: exhibits 01 and 05 give a detailed duty list and an hour-by-hour schedule from which percentages could be derived, but no percentage-of-time figures are stated as requested. Item (b) is not met at all: the schedule contains roughly 9.5 of 35 weekly hours (about 27%) of office administration, bookkeeping support, vendor invoicing, facility booking, supply ordering, maintenance scheduling, cleaning, and chair and equipment setup, and no document explains why these are not primarily administrative or support work. Item (c) is not met: exhibit 04 documents the *credentialing* requirements for a Covenant Cantor, but no denominational handbook, position standard, or governing-body statement establishes that this combined music-and-catechesis position is recognized as a religious occupation of the Fellowship rather than a local creation. Item (d) is fully met by exhibit 01 section 3 (reports to the Senior Pastor and, on liturgical order, to the Worship Council).

**Request 4 — Compensation: FULLY COVERED.**
Exhibit 03 supplies all three requested elements: past compensation for the same and comparable positions with W-2 and Form 941 payroll evidence (item a), Board Resolution 2025-14 of November 14, 2025 authorizing the $38,000 salary and $600 monthly housing allowance (item b), and the specific FY2026 budget lines 5120–5122 totalling $54,540 set aside for the position (item c).

**Request 5 — Beneficiary's qualifications: FULLY COVERED.**
Exhibit 04 sections D-2 through D-4 give the denomination's own written requirements for the office (Order and Discipline, Chapter 9, section 2), documentary proof that the beneficiary met each one — diploma, transcript, catechetical instructor's certificate, Convocation examination record, Certificate of Commission, and current 2026 renewal — and a General Secretary's letter of good standing and endorsement, matching items (a), (b), and (c).

**Request 6 — Financial ability to compensate: PARTIALLY COVERED.**
Exhibit 06 provides the adopted FY2026 budget with FY2025 actuals and, at F-2 item 5, the signed statement that as a church the Assembly is not required to file Form 990 — which satisfies item (c). But neither alternative in items (a) and (b) is satisfied: F-2 item 4 states expressly that no external audit or independent review is performed, and no bank statements for the twelve months preceding the filing date appear anywhere in the petition. The request is answered on paper but not with the account evidence the officer specified.

**Request 7 — Physical premises: NOT COVERED.**
No petition document contains a deed, lease, rental agreement, or property-tax record, any photograph of the exterior, signage, worship space or work area, any floor plan or diagram, or any shared-use agreement. The address 4180 Wetherby Road appears only on the letterhead of exhibit 01 and as the work location in exhibit 05, which shows where the beneficiary would work but nothing about the petitioner's occupancy or control of the premises. Every subpart (a) through (d) is unanswered.

**Request 8 — Prior R-1 time and immigration history: NOT COVERED.**
Nothing in the six petition documents mentions any prior entry to, stay in, or status held in the United States, and no passport pages, Form I-94 records, or Forms I-797 are listed anywhere. Exhibit 04 section D-5 places the beneficiary's service in Nigeria from 2023 onward, which is background rather than the accounting the officer demanded; even the negative statement invited by subpart (d) — that the beneficiary has never been present in the United States — is absent from the record.
