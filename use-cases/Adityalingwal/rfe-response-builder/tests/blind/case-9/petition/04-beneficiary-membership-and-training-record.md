## D-2. Religious training required by the Fellowship for the office of Cantor

*The Order and Discipline of the Fourth Covenant Fellowship* (revised 2019), Chapter 9, section 2 sets the requirements for commissioning as a Covenant Cantor:

1. Enrolled communicant membership of not less than two years;
2. Completion of the Diploma in Covenant Cantorial Studies, or an equivalent course approved by the Council of Elders;
3. Completion of the Catechetical Instructor's Course;
4. Examination before a regional Convocation in doctrine, the sung liturgy, and the Covenant Catechism;
5. Commissioning by the Council of Elders and annual renewal of the commission.
