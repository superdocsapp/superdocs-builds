## Item-by-item

**Item 1 — U.S. citizenship of the petitioner: FULL.**
The cover letter's Exhibit B lists both a certified Ohio birth certificate (State File No.
91-0044718) and the biographic page of an unexpired U.S. passport, either of which alone
satisfies the request; the petitioner's declaration also states birth in Toledo, Ohio on
June 14, 1991.

**Item 2 — Commingled finances: FULL.**
`04-joint-financial-evidence-summary.md` supplies every category the officer listed: a
joint checking account with the signature card, account number, opening date of June 2,
2023 and fifteen months of statements showing both salaries and shared spending; a joint
savings account; a joint 2023 Form 1040 plus an IRS transcript; an auto policy naming both
spouses; life and retirement beneficiary designations naming the spouse; and a joint auto
loan with a jointly titled vehicle.

**Item 3 — Shared residence for the whole period: PARTIAL.**
Joint documentary residence evidence exists only from August 2024 onward — the Harlowe
Bend lease naming both tenants and two utility statements (Exhibit I). For the requested
April 2023 – July 2024 window the record has only the 2023 joint tax return listing both
spouses at 219 Wexford Mill Lane, rent transfers from the joint account, and the parties'
own statements that the earlier lease was in the petitioner's name alone; the officer
expressly discounted statements standing alone, and nothing covers April–May 2023 at all.

**Item 4 — Photographs: FULL.**
`05-photographs-index.md` lists fourteen photographs individually labelled with date,
place, and the name of every person shown; they run from November 2021 to August 2024 and
ten of them include family, friends, neighbours, or other third parties, meeting each of
the officer's three conditions.

**Item 5 — Two complete third-party affidavits: PARTIAL.**
Only one of the two declarations in `06-third-party-declarations.md` meets the officer's
list: Kwame Boateng gives his full name, date and place of birth, address, how and how
long he has known the couple, and signs under penalty of perjury. Dolores Weatherby's
declaration omits her date of birth and place of birth and carries no penalty-of-perjury
attestation, so by the notice's own terms it will not be given weight and the two-affidavit
minimum is unmet.

**Item 6 — Termination of the beneficiary's prior marriage: NONE.**
No petition document contains, references, or indexes a divorce decree, annulment, or
death certificate for any prior spouse of the beneficiary — the cover letter's exhibit
index runs A through K without one, and neither declaration mentions a previous marriage,
so nothing in the filing addresses this request in any form.
