## Legal standard

To establish a bona fide marriage, the petitioner must show by a preponderance of the
evidence that the marriage was entered into in good faith and not for the purpose of
evading the immigration laws. *Matter of Laureano*, 19 I&N Dec. 1 (BIA 1983). The
evidence enclosed — joint financial accounts, a joint tax return, joint insurance
designations, a shared residence, photographs spanning nearly three years, and
declarations from persons who know the couple — satisfies that standard.
