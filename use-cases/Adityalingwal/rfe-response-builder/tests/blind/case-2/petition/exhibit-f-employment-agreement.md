# EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 30 September 2025 between:

**VOLTARIS GRID DYNAMICS, INC.**, a Delaware corporation having its principal place of business at
4180 Harrowgate Parkway, Suite 900, Belmont Ridge, TX 75094 (the "Company"); and

**DR. ANSELM FERREIRA-DUARTE**, an individual residing at 1207 Sable Crossing, Apartment 4B,
Belmont Ridge, TX 75093 (the "Employee").

## 1. Position

1.1 The Company employs the Employee as **Principal Research Engineer, Storage Safety Systems**,
reporting to the Vice President of Research, currently Dr. Marisol Trentham-Baye.

1.2 The position is full time and exempt. The Employee shall devote his full working time to the
Company, save for the outside academic and professional activities permitted under clause 6.

## 2. Term

2.1 The term of this Agreement begins on **1 June 2026** and ends on **31 May 2029**, unless
terminated earlier under clause 8.

2.2 This Agreement supersedes the Employee's prior employment agreement with the Company dated
4 January 2021, which shall terminate on 31 May 2026.

2.3 This Agreement covers the whole of the period 1 June 2026 to 31 May 2029 without interruption.
Employment beyond 31 May 2029 requires a further written agreement between the parties.

## 3. Duties

3.1 The Employee shall:

  (a) direct the Company's research programme in cell-level fault detection for stationary
      lithium-iron-phosphate storage, including the continued development of drift-indexed
      impedance methods and of the HeliosGuard detection model;
  (b) lead the Storage Safety Systems research group, comprising nine engineers and two research
      technicians as at the date of this Agreement;
  (c) design and supervise validation testing at Company facilities and, where a customer permits,
      at customer installations;
  (d) prepare and submit the Company's technical contributions to standards bodies, including the
      North American Storage Safety Council;
  (e) publish research findings in peer-reviewed venues, subject to clause 7; and
  (f) advise the Company's product engineering organisation on the transfer of research results
      into shipped products.

3.2 The Employee shall have no sales, business development, or customer account responsibility.

## 4. Place of work

4.1 The Employee's principal place of work is the Company's research facility at 4180 Harrowgate
Parkway, Suite 900, Belmont Ridge, TX 75094.

4.2 The Employee will additionally perform services, on an intermittent basis and at the Company's
direction, at the Company's validation laboratory at 55 Kestrel Bend Industrial Park, Ardenholt,
TX 76210.

4.3 Travel to customer installations, to standards body meetings, and to academic conferences is
expected and is estimated by the Company at not more than fifteen per cent of working time. No
services will be performed at a third-party worksite under the supervision or control of a third
party.

## 5. Compensation and benefits

5.1 **Base salary.** The Company shall pay the Employee an annual base salary of **USD 268,000**,
payable semi-monthly in arrears, less lawful deductions and withholdings. The base salary shall be
reviewed annually and shall not be reduced during the term.

5.2 **Signing bonus.** The Company shall pay the Employee a one-time signing bonus of **USD
45,000** within thirty days of the start date, repayable on a pro-rata basis if the Employee
resigns without good reason within twelve months of the start date.

5.3 **Annual bonus.** The Employee is eligible for an annual performance bonus with a target of
twenty per cent of base salary, determined by the Company's Compensation Committee against
research objectives agreed each January.

5.4 **Equity.** Subject to approval by the Board of Directors, the Company shall grant the Employee
**14,000 restricted stock units** under the Voltaris Grid Dynamics, Inc. 2019 Equity Incentive
Plan, vesting as to twenty-five per cent on the first anniversary of the start date and as to the
remainder in twelve equal quarterly instalments thereafter.

5.5 **Benefits.** The Employee is eligible for the Company's group medical, dental and vision
plans, its 401(k) plan with an employer match of four per cent of eligible compensation, life and
disability cover at two times base salary, and twenty-five days of paid annual leave in addition
to Company holidays.

5.6 **Research allowance.** The Company shall provide an annual allowance of USD 18,000 for
conference attendance, professional society membership, and publication charges.

## 6. Permitted outside activities

6.1 The Employee may, without further consent, serve as a peer reviewer or editorial board member
for scholarly journals, serve on the technical committees of standards bodies and professional
societies, and serve on grant review panels, provided such activity does not materially interfere
with his duties and does not involve a competitor of the Company.

6.2 Any paid outside consulting requires the prior written consent of the Vice President of
Research.

## 7. Intellectual property and publication

7.1 All inventions, discoveries and works of authorship made by the Employee in the course of his
employment and relating to the Company's business are the property of the Company.

7.2 The Company acknowledges that publication is integral to the Employee's field and shall not
unreasonably withhold consent to publish. The Company may delay a publication by not more than
sixty days to permit the filing of a patent application.

## 8. Termination

8.1 The Company may terminate this Agreement for cause immediately on written notice.

8.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause on sixty days' written notice.

8.3 On termination by the Company without cause, the Company shall pay the Employee six months'
base salary as severance, conditional on a release of claims.

8.4 The parties acknowledge that the Employee's employment is contingent upon his maintaining
lawful work authorisation in the United States, and that the Company has filed a petition for O-1A
classification on his behalf. Should that petition be denied, this Agreement shall terminate
without liability on either side.

## 9. General

9.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas.

9.2 This Agreement, together with the Company's Employee Handbook and the Proprietary Information
and Inventions Agreement executed on the same date, constitutes the entire agreement between the
parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions. No amendment is effective
unless in writing and signed by both parties.

9.3 As at the date of this Agreement no amendment, addendum or side letter has been executed.
