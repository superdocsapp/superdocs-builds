This firm represents Voltaris Grid Dynamics, Inc. ("Voltaris" or "the Petitioner") in connection
with the enclosed Form I-129 petition seeking O-1A classification on behalf of Dr. Anselm
Ferreira-Duarte. Dr. Ferreira-Duarte is a research engineer in grid-scale battery safety whose
work on early detection of thermal runaway in lithium-iron-phosphate storage arrays has been
adopted by operators outside his own employers and has been written into a North American safety
standard. This letter sets out the factual and legal basis for the petition and is supported by
the exhibits indexed at the end.

## 1. The Petitioner

Voltaris Grid Dynamics, Inc. is a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at
4180 Harrowgate Parkway, Suite 900, Belmont Ridge, TX 75094. Founded in 2016, Voltaris designs
and supplies safety and monitoring systems for utility-scale battery energy storage installations.
As of September 2025, Voltaris employed 312 people, of whom 84 work in research and engineering,
and reported audited revenue of USD 96.4 million for fiscal year 2024. Voltaris systems are
installed at 61 storage sites across eleven states.

## 2. The Beneficiary

Dr. Ferreira-Duarte is a citizen of Brazil. He holds a Doctor of Philosophy in Electrical
Engineering from the Universidade Metropolitana de Curitiba, conferred in 2015, and a Master of
Science in Applied Physics from the same institution, conferred in 2011.

From 2015 to 2021 he was a Senior Research Fellow at the Kessendra Institute of Applied
Energetics in São Paulo. From 2021 to the present he has been employed by Voltaris in Belmont
Ridge, Texas, most recently as Principal Research Engineer, Storage Safety Systems.

His principal scientific contribution is Drift-Indexed Impedance Spectroscopy ("DIIS"), a
measurement technique that identifies the electrochemical signature of an incipient internal short
circuit in a lithium-iron-phosphate cell from small drifts in the cell's impedance response,
before any temperature rise is observable at the module surface. He is also the lead author of
HeliosGuard, the open detection model that applies DIIS across a full storage array.

## 3. The offered position

Voltaris offers Dr. Ferreira-Duarte the position of Principal Research Engineer, Storage Safety
Systems, at its Belmont Ridge, Texas research facility, for the period June 1, 2026 through
May 31, 2029. The executed employment agreement is submitted at **Exhibit F**. The position is a
continuation of work in his area of extraordinary ability. The agreement provides an annual base
salary of USD 268,000, a signing bonus of USD 45,000, and a restricted stock grant.

## 4. Legal standard

Section 101(a)(15)(O)(i) of the Act provides for classification of a person who has extraordinary
ability in the sciences, demonstrated by sustained national or international acclaim. Under
8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii), extraordinary ability may be shown by evidence of a major, internationally
recognized award, or by at least three of the eight forms of evidence enumerated at
8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii)(B). Dr. Ferreira-Duarte satisfies five.

## 5. Criteria satisfied

### 5.1 Nationally or internationally recognized prizes or awards — (B)(1)

In 2024 Dr. Ferreira-Duarte received the Halvorsen Medal in Energy Storage Engineering from the
Continental Society for Energy Storage Engineering. The award evidence packet at **Exhibit C**

### 5.2 Original scientific contributions of major significance — (B)(5)

DIIS and HeliosGuard are original contributions whose significance is documented at **Exhibits
