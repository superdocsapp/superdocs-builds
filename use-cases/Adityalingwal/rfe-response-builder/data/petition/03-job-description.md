# Exhibit B — Detailed job description

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE — created for the SuperDocs engineering task.

Position: Software Engineer (Data Platforms)
Employer: Brightlayer Analytics Inc.
Location: Oakland, California (headquarters)
Reports to: Director of Platform Engineering

## Day-to-day duties and time allocation

- **35% — Ingestion pipeline design and implementation.** Design and build
  the platform's stream-ingestion services: partitioning strategies,
  exactly-once delivery across the broker layer, back-pressure handling,
  and schema-evolution rules. Requires degree-level knowledge of
  distributed-systems consistency models and queueing theory.
- **25% — Storage-layer performance engineering.** Model and tune columnar
  storage layouts, compression choices, and index structures against
  measured query plans on customer-scale datasets. Requires degree-level
  knowledge of database internals and algorithmic complexity analysis.
- **20% — Telemetry anomaly detection.** Build and calibrate the
  statistical scoring that flags abnormal pipeline behavior: sampling
  design, seasonality adjustment, and false-positive budgeting. Requires
  degree-level knowledge of probability and statistics.
- **10% — Design review and mentorship.** Author design documents for the
  ingestion roadmap and review the designs of other platform engineers.
- **10% — Incident response for the ingestion layer.** Diagnose production
  incidents, write post-incident analyses, and land the preventive fixes.

## Minimum requirements

Bachelor's degree in computer science, computer engineering, or a closely
related quantitative field, plus demonstrated coursework or equivalent
grounding in distributed systems, database systems, and statistics.
