April 2, 2026

To: U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (fictional sample)

Re: H-1B petition for Mr. Arjun Mehta — Software Engineer (Data Platforms)

Dear Officer:

I am the Vice President of Engineering at Brightlayer Analytics Inc. I write
to describe the position offered to Mr. Arjun Mehta and the terms of his
employment.

## The position and why it requires a degree

The Software Engineer (Data Platforms) position is responsible for the
design and reliability of the ingestion layer of Brightlayer's observability
platform. The role requires theoretical knowledge of distributed systems,
database internals, and statistical sampling methods: the engineer designs
exactly-once delivery semantics across message brokers, tunes columnar
storage layouts against measured query plans, and builds anomaly-scoring
models for pipeline telemetry. Brightlayer requires at least a bachelor's
degree in computer science, computer engineering, or a closely related
quantitative field for this position, and every engineer currently working
on the ingestion layer holds at least such a degree. The knowledge applied
in this role — consistency models, query optimization, probability theory
applied to sampling — is taught at degree level and is not attainable
through short-course training.

## Supervision and control

Mr. Mehta will work at our Oakland headquarters and will report directly to
Ms. Dana Whitfield, Director of Platform Engineering, who sets his work
queue, conducts his weekly one-on-one reviews, and approves his deliverables
before release. Work is assigned through Brightlayer's internal sprint
planning process and reviewed through the company's code-review system.
Brightlayer provides all equipment, source-code access, and development
infrastructure. Brightlayer alone sets Mr. Mehta's salary, benefits, and
performance targets, and holds the exclusive right to hire, discipline, and
dismiss him. Mr. Mehta will not be placed at any client site; his work is
performed on Brightlayer's own product.

## Terms of employment

Mr. Mehta is offered a full-time position at an annual salary of $142,000,
which meets the prevailing wage identified in the certified Labor Condition
Application enclosed as Exhibit D.

I declare that the statements above are true and correct to the best of my
knowledge.

Sincerely,

Marcus Delgado (fictional)
