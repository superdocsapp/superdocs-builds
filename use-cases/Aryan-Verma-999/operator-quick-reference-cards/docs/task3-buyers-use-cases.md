# Task 3 — buyers and real-world use cases

These are realistic buyer hypotheses, not customer claims or outreach records.

| buyer/persona | industry | source document | operator task | risk if wrong | why LineCard fits | likely purchase trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| plant maintenance lead | food processing | sanitation/conveyor OEM manual | clear belt jam and restart | injury, contamination, downtime | lockout steps and torque citations | near-miss or audit finding |
| packaging line supervisor | packaging | cartoner setup/service manual | clear feeder obstruction | pinch injury, scrap | short card beside fast-change machine | changeover training burden |
| manufacturing engineer | automotive assembly | torque-tool/fixture manual | replace fixture and verify torque | loose joint or damaged part | exact values and condition conflicts | new station launch |
| reliability technician | industrial maintenance | pump/compressor service manual | isolate, inspect, return equipment | stored-energy release | source-linked stop/restart sequence | recurring repair issue |
| warehouse controls lead | warehouse automation | sortation/AMR maintenance manual | clear divert or conveyor fault | unexpected motion | flow branches and escalation gate | operator turnover |
| equipment engineer | semiconductor equipment | tool access/maintenance manual | recover wafer-handling jam | contamination, tool damage | strict native-text evidence | new tool qualification |
| process safety manager | chemical processing | pump/valve line-break procedure | isolate blocked transfer line | exposure, release, fire | safety wording cannot weaken | PSM/MOC review |
| field service manager | utilities | switchgear OEM manual | controlled inspection/reset | arc flash or outage | traceable fleet cards | service standardization |
| quality manager | medical-device manufacturing | assembly-cell work instruction | recover feeder fault | patient-impacting defect | evidence and independent verifier | regulated training evidence |
| pressroom operations manager | commercial printing | press setup/maintenance manual | clear stock misfeed/restart | crush injury, misregistration | task retrieval across long manuals | onboarding and shift handoff |
