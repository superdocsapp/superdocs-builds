Master Services Agreement

Between: Northwind Facilities Group Ltd ("the Supplier") and Harbour Lane Analytics Ltd ("the Client"). Both parties are fictional and this document exists only as tutorial material.

1. Scope of services

The Supplier will provide planned and reactive maintenance across the Client's three sites, to the standards set out in Schedule A. Work outside Schedule A is quoted separately and begins only on written instruction.

2. Term and termination

This agreement runs for twenty four months from the commencement date. Either party may terminate for material breach on thirty days written notice, where the breach remains unremedied at the end of that period.

3. Charges and payment

Charges are invoiced monthly in arrears. Payment falls due thirty days from the invoice date. The Supplier may suspend non-critical work on any amount that remains unpaid sixty days after its due date.

4. Service levels

Response times are measured from the time a ticket is raised in the Client's portal to the time an engineer is on site. Priority one tickets carry a four hour response target, measured during business hours.

5. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales, and both parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of its courts.
