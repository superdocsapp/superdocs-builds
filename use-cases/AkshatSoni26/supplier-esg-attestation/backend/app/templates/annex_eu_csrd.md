## ANNEX R-EU: EUROPEAN UNION STATUTORY & CSRD COMPLIANCE ADDENDUM

**Jurisdiction Applicability:** European Union (EU) & European Economic Area (EEA)  
**Governing Directives:** EU CSRD (Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive), CSDDD (Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive), EUDR (Deforestation-Free Products), REACH (EC 1907/2006).

---

### Clause EU-1: Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence (CSDDD)
1. **Supply Chain Human Rights Mapping**:  
   - Supplier certifies adherence to Directive (EU) 2024/1760. State whether an annual adverse human rights impact assessment is executed.
2. **Deforestation-Free Commodity Provenance (EUDR)**:  
   - For timber, rubber, cattle, soy, palm oil, cocoa, or coffee: confirm geolocation coordinates of production plots and due diligence statements under Regulation (EU) 2023/1115.
3. **REACH / RoHS Chemical SVHC Disclosures**:  
   - Confirm all shipped articles contain $< 0.1\%$ weight-by-weight of substances on the ECHA Candidate List of Substances of Very High Concern (SVHC).
