## ANNEX T1: TIER 1 STRATEGIC ESG AUDIT QUESTIONNAIRE

**Supplier Category:** Tier 1 (Direct Material / Strategic Partner)  
**Mandatory Evidence Requirements:** Third-party ISO audit certificates, GHG calculation inventories, and sub-tier provenance logs.

---

### Section E1: Comprehensive Climate & Environmental Disclosures
1. **Scope 1 & 2 Emissions Accounting**:  
   - Do you calculate and report your Scope 1 and Scope 2 GHG emissions in MT CO2e annually?  
   - *State exact annual figure and reporting standard (e.g., GHG Protocol).*
2. **Scope 3 Upstream Supply Chain Tracking**:  
   - Have you mapped and calculated Scope 3 Category 1 (purchased goods) emissions?
3. **Renewable Energy & Efficiency**:  
   - What percentage of your facility's total electricity consumption is sourced from certified renewables?
4. **Environmental Management System (EMS)**:  
   - Is your facility ISO 14001 certified? (Attach certificate expiration date).
5. **Effluent and Hazardous Chemical Disclosures**:  
   - Provide your waste diversion rate (%) and confirm zero discharge of ZDHC hazardous chemicals.

---

### Section S1: Advanced Labor & Human Rights Due Diligence
1. **Recruitment Fee & Passport Retention Audit**:  
   - Do you conduct unannounced audits of recruitment agencies to ensure zero worker-paid fees and zero passport withholding?
2. **Overtime & Rest Days**:  
   - What is the maximum recorded continuous working hours in peak seasons? Are rest days guaranteed every 7 days?
3. **Health & Safety Management**:  
   - Is your primary manufacturing facility ISO 45001 or equivalent safety standard certified?

---

### Section G1: Strategic Governance & Chain of Custody
1. **Sub-Tier Transparency**:  
   - Do you maintain a 100% mapped bill of materials (BOM) trace to smelters/refiners or raw material origin?
2. **Independent Anti-Corruption Auditing**:  
   - Do you conduct third-party background checks and anti-corruption screening on all tier-2 suppliers?
