## ANNEX R-APAC: ASIA-PACIFIC REGIONAL LABOR & DISCHARGE ADDENDUM

**Jurisdiction Applicability:** Asia-Pacific (APAC)  
**Governing Standards:** ILO Core Labor Standards, National Environmental Quality Standards (Effluent & Air).

---

### Clause APAC-1: Ethical Recruitment & Migrant Worker Rights
1. **Employer-Pays Principle**:  
   - Direct and contract migrant workers must not pay any recruitment, visa, travel, or placement fees. Any previously levied fees must be reimbursed within 30 days of hiring.
2. **Passport Retention Ban**:  
   - Workers must retain personal physical custody of all passports, national identity cards, and work permits at all times.
3. **Statutory Discharge Permitting**:  
   - Supplier must maintain continuous real-time monitoring of biological chemical oxygen demand (BOD/COD) for industrial wastewater before municipal release.
