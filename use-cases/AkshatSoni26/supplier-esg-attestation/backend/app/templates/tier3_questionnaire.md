## ANNEX T3: TIER 3 COMMODITY & INDIRECT SUPPLIER QUESTIONNAIRE

**Supplier Category:** Tier 3 (Commodities / Logistics / Indirect Services)  
**Mandatory Evidence Requirements:** Business license, tax registration, signed compliance attestation.

---

### Section 1: Basic Environmental & Statutory Compliance
1. Do you hold all required statutory environmental and municipal operating permits?
2. Do you comply with local commercial transport, emissions, and packaging waste disposal laws?

---

### Section 2: Core Labor Standards & Non-Discrimination
1. Do you certify zero use of child labor and zero use of involuntary prison or forced labor?
2. Do you guarantee timely payment of legally mandated minimum wages to all workers?

---

### Section 3: Business Integrity & Anti-Corruption
1. Do you certify zero tolerance for bribery, improper gifts, extortion, or fraudulent billing?
2. Are you able to confirm you have had no formal enforcement sanctions or anti-corruption convictions in the past 3 years?
