# GLOBAL SUPPLIER CODE OF CONDUCT & ETHICAL CHARTER
**Document Version:** 2026.1  
**Applicability:** All approved suppliers, vendors, sub-contractors, and manufacturing partners.

---

### 1. Environmental Stewardship
1.1 **Decarbonization & Climate Strategy**: Suppliers must actively measure and reduce energy consumption and greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions.  
1.2 **Pollution Prevention & Resource Conservation**: Waste generation, wastewater discharges, and air emissions must be minimized, controlled, and treated before discharge.  
1.3 **Hazardous Substances**: All chemicals and hazardous materials must be safely identified, stored, handled, and disposed of in strict accordance with local and international environmental mandates.

---

### 2. Social Responsibility & Labor Rights
2.1 **Freely Chosen Employment & Anti-Slavery**: Forced, bonded, indentured, or involuntary prison labor is strictly prohibited. Workers must not be required to surrender passports, identity documents, or pay recruitment fees.  
2.2 **Child Labor Prohibition**: No person under the legal minimum working age (or under 15 years old) shall be employed under any circumstances.  
2.3 **Working Hours & Compensation**: Working hours must not exceed 48 regular hours per week plus maximum 12 overtime hours. Overtime must be voluntary and paid at premium rates. Compensation must meet or exceed the statutory living wage.  
2.4 **Occupational Health & Safety (OHS)**: Suppliers must provide safe, hygienic work environments with emergency exits, clean drinking water, adequate PPE, and robust fire safety protocols.

---

### 3. Business Integrity & Governance
3.1 **Zero Tolerance for Bribery & Corruption**: Bribes, kickbacks, improper gifts, or facilitating payments are strictly forbidden.  
3.2 **Whistleblower Protection**: Suppliers must provide anonymous, retaliation-free grievance channels for employees and external stakeholders.  
3.3 **Transparency & Traceability**: Suppliers must maintain auditable operational records and disclose sub-tier supply chain origins upon request.

---

### 4. Attestation & Sign-off
By submitting responses to the accompanying ESG Questionnaire, the supplier formally certifies compliance with this Code of Conduct.
