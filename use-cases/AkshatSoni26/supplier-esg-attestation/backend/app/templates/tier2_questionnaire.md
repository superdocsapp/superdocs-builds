## ANNEX T2: TIER 2 MANUFACTURING & PROCESSING ESG QUESTIONNAIRE

**Supplier Category:** Tier 2 (Direct Manufacturing / Sub-assembly)  
**Mandatory Evidence Requirements:** Facility safety inspections, energy consumption utility records, worker grievance records.

---

### Section E2: Facility Environmental Standards
1. **Energy Tracking**:  
   - Do you track annual kilowatt-hour (kWh) and fuel usage at your production sites?  
   - What percentage of your power is from renewable sources?
2. **Waste Management & Circularity**:  
   - Do you sort solid and chemical waste prior to licensed disposal?
3. **Water Treatment**:  
   - Do you operate or use a municipal wastewater treatment plant for industrial effluent?

---

### Section S2: Factory Workplace & Fair Labor Standards
1. **Working Hours & Overtime Compliance**:  
   - Confirm standard weekly hours are $\le 48$ hours, and maximum weekly overtime does not exceed 12 hours.
2. **Living Wage Guarantee**:  
   - Confirm all production and contract workers receive at or above the local legal minimum wage with timely paystubs.
3. **Fire & Emergency Safety**:  
   - Are emergency exits unlocked, unobstructed, and inspected quarterly?

---

### Section G2: Operational Integrity
1. **Anti-Bribery Policy**:  
   - Do all supervisory and procurement staff complete annual anti-corruption training?
2. **Anonymous Grievance Box / Hotline**:  
   - Is there an active worker grievance mechanism with documented response times?
