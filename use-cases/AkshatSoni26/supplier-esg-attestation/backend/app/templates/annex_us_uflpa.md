## ANNEX R-US: UNITED STATES TRADE & STATUTORY ADDENDUM

**Jurisdiction Applicability:** United States & North America  
**Governing Laws:** Uyghur Forced Labor Prevention Act (UFLPA, P.L. 117-78), Dodd-Frank Act Section 1502 (Conflict Minerals), OSHA 29 CFR 1910.

---

### Clause US-1: Forced Labor & UFLPA Rebuttable Presumption
1. **Supply Chain Traceability to Raw Inputs**:  
   - Supplier explicitly warrants that zero components, raw materials (silicon, cotton, tomatoes, aluminum, PVC), or sub-assemblies originate directly or indirectly from the Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region (XUAR) or entities on the UFLPA Entity List.
2. **Conflict Minerals Sourcing (3TG)**:  
   - Supplier must submit an annual Conflict Minerals Reporting Template (CMRT) verifying conflict-free smelters for Tantalum, Tin, Tungsten, and Gold.
3. **OSHA Compliance**:  
   - Confirm adherence to OSHA workplace standards with zero uncorrected willful safety violations.
