Delivery: before the lease is executed.

FLOODING DISCLOSURE

This disclosure is provided under Texas Property Code section 92.0135.

"Flooding" means a general or temporary condition of partial or complete
inundation of the dwelling caused by (i) the overflow of inland or tidal
waters, (ii) the unusual and rapid accumulation of runoff or surface
water from any source, or (iii) mudslides caused by flooding.

"100-year floodplain" means an area subject to a one percent or greater
chance of flooding in any given year, as shown on current Federal
Emergency Management Agency flood maps.
