D-3. Bed Bug Information Notice

Delivery: before the tenancy begins.

BED BUG INFORMATION

Information about bed bugs: Bed bugs are small, flat, wingless insects,
about the size of an apple seed, that feed on the blood of humans and
animals. They are reddish-brown in colour and are most active at night.

Bed bug bites may cause no reaction in some people, and itching, welts or
swelling in others. Bed bugs are not known to transmit disease.

Common signs and symptoms of a possible bed bug infestation:
- Small red to reddish-brown fecal spots on mattresses, box springs,
bed frames, mattress pads, bedding, upholstery or walls.
- Molted bed bug skins, white sticky eggs, or empty eggshells.
- Very heavily infested areas may have a characteristically sweet odour.
- Red, itchy bite marks, commonly on the face, neck, arms, hands or
other body parts, though not everyone reacts to bites.

Tenants are encouraged to report suspected infestations promptly to the
landlord in writing. Landlord will respond as required by California law.
