1. Parties and Premises

This Lease is made between Balcones Ridge Residential LLC ("Landlord") and Priya Raghunathan, Daniel Okonkwo ("Tenant") for the premises at 4412 Burnet Road, Unit 213, Austin, TX 78756 (the "Premises").

2. Term and Rent

The tenancy is a fixed term beginning 2026-09-01 and ending 2027-08-31. Rent of $2,400.00 is due on day 1 of each month. Landlord holds a security deposit of $4,800.00.

The Premises is let unfurnished. Pets are permitted subject to any separate pet agreement.

3. Texas Required Terms

L-1. Smoke Alarms — Landlord Duty and Non-Waiver

Smoke Alarms. Landlord has installed smoke alarms in the Premises as
required by Subchapter F of Chapter 92 of the Texas Property Code, and
will inspect and repair them as that subchapter requires. Tenant will not
disconnect or intentionally damage a smoke alarm, or remove its battery
without immediately replacing it with a working battery.

Tenant's rights under Subchapter F may not be waived, and any provision of
this Lease that purports to waive them is void.

Authority: Tex. Prop. Code §§ 92.251–92.262 (see § 92.006 on waiver) (verified 2025-08-01, review by 2027-03-31)

L-2. Tenant's Repair Remedies — Required Notice of Procedure

Repairs. Tenant must give Landlord written notice of a condition that
materially affects the physical health or safety of an ordinary tenant,
and must not be delinquent in rent at the time notice is given. Landlord
will make a diligent effort to repair the condition within a reasonable
time after receiving that notice, as required by Subchapter B of Chapter
92 of the Texas Property Code.

Address for repair notices: ____________________

Authority: Tex. Prop. Code §§ 92.052, 92.056, 92.0561 (verified 2025-08-01, review by 2027-03-31)

L-3. Security Deposit Refund — Thirty Days

Security Deposit Refund. Landlord will refund the security deposit, less
any lawful deductions, no later than 30 days after Tenant surrenders the
Premises and gives Landlord a written forwarding address. If Landlord
retains any part of the deposit, Landlord will give Tenant a written
itemised description of the deductions, except where Tenant owes rent at
surrender and there is no controversy about the amount owed.

Tenant's forwarding address: ____________________

Authority: Tex. Prop. Code §§ 92.103, 92.104, 92.109 (verified 2025-08-01, review by 2027-03-31)

L-4. No Statutory Security Deposit Cap (Jurisdiction Note)

Security Deposit Amount. Texas law does not limit the amount of a
residential security deposit. The deposit stated in this Lease is set by
agreement between the parties and remains subject to Landlord's
accounting and refund duties under Chapter 92 of the Texas Property Code.

Authority: Tex. Prop. Code ch. 92, subch. C (§§ 92.101–92.109) — refund and accounting duties, no cap on amount (verified 2025-08-01, review by 2027-03-31)

L-5. Municipal Rent Control Preempted (Jurisdiction Note)

Rent Adjustment. No municipal rent control ordinance applies to the
Premises. Rent adjustments are governed by the terms of this Lease and by
Chapter 92 of the Texas Property Code.

Authority: Tex. Loc. Gov't Code § 214.902 (verified 2025-08-01, review by 2027-03-31)
