# Meridian Advisory Services — Investment Advisory Agreement (Template v2)

This Agreement is entered into between Meridian Advisory Services ("the Adviser") and the undersigned client ("the Client").

1. Scope of Services
   The Adviser shall provide investment advisory services as described in the attached fee schedule.

2. Fees
   Fees shall be charged per the mode and rate disclosed to the Client in writing, consistent with SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013, Regulation 15A.

3. Conflicts of Interest
   The Adviser shall disclose any material conflict of interest arising in the course of advisory services.

4. Term and Termination
   This Agreement remains in force until terminated by either party with 30 days' written notice.

5. Record Keeping
   The Adviser shall maintain records of advice given in accordance with applicable regulation.

6. Use of AI Tools in Advice
   Where the Adviser uses AI-assisted tools in preparing or presenting advice, the extent of that use will be disclosed to the Client in a separate disclosure page, and the Adviser remains solely responsible for the advice given regardless of any AI tool's involvement.
