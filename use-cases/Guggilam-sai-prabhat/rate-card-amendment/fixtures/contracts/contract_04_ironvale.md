IRONVALE CARRIER EXCHANGE — MASTER INTERCONNECTION AGREEMENT
Agreement ID: IVX-MIA-2020-771
Parties: Ironvale Carrier Exchange Ltd ("Ironvale") and Porto Nexus Telecomunicações Lda ("Porto Nexus")
Contract Date: 2 November 2020

--------------------------------------------------------------
PART I — GENERAL TERMS
--------------------------------------------------------------

Clause I.1 Purpose. This Master Interconnection Agreement governs the exchange of wholesale voice traffic between Ironvale and Porto Nexus.

Clause I.2 Term. This Agreement is effective on the Contract Date and remains in force for sixty (60) months.

Clause I.3 Rate Schedule. The rates applicable at signing are those in the attached Rate Schedule (Appendix A), effective on the Contract Date.

--------------------------------------------------------------
PART II — COMMERCIAL TERMS
--------------------------------------------------------------

Clause II.1 Invoicing. Monthly invoicing, net 30 payment terms.

Clause II.2 Currency. All rates are quoted and invoiced in EUR.

Clause II.3 Rate Amendments — Mutual Consent Required.
(a) Notwithstanding any other provision of this Agreement, no amendment, variation, or modification of the Rate Schedule in Appendix A shall be valid or binding on either Party unless it is:
    (i) reduced to writing;
    (ii) signed by an authorized representative of BOTH Ironvale and Porto Nexus; and
    (iii) expressly identified as an amendment to this Agreement.
(b) Neither Party may unilaterally impose a rate change by notice alone. A notice proposing a rate change is an invitation to negotiate only and has no binding effect until the mutual written consent described in Clause II.3(a) is obtained.
(c) Either Party may initiate discussion of a rate amendment at any time by giving the other Party at least thirty (30) days' notice of its intent to negotiate, but the thirty (30) day period is a negotiation window only, not a unilateral amendment mechanism.

Clause II.4 Effective Date of Amendments. Any amendment executed under Clause II.3 shall specify its own effective date, which shall not be earlier than the date of the last signature.

--------------------------------------------------------------
PART III — OPERATIONAL TERMS
--------------------------------------------------------------

Clause III.1 Quality of Service. Ironvale shall maintain ASR ≥ 38% measured on a rolling 30-day basis.

Clause III.2 Fraud Management. Both Parties shall implement reasonable fraud detection controls.

--------------------------------------------------------------
PART IV — TERM AND TERMINATION
--------------------------------------------------------------

Clause IV.1 Termination for Convenience. Either Party may terminate on one hundred twenty (120) days' written notice.

Clause IV.2 Termination for Cause. Either Party may terminate immediately upon the other Party's insolvency or repeated material breach.

--------------------------------------------------------------
PART V — GENERAL PROVISIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------

Clause V.1 Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of Portugal.

Clause V.2 Entire Agreement. This Agreement, including Appendix A, constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties regarding its subject matter.
