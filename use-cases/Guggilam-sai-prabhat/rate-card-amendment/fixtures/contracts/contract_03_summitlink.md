# WHOLESALE SMS AND VOICE INTERCONNECT AGREEMENT

**Identifier:** SL-WIC-2022-0093
**Parties:** SummitLink Networks LLC ("SummitLink") and Del Rio Communications S.A. ("Del Rio")
**Contract Date:** September 9, 2022

---

### 1. Recitals

WHEREAS SummitLink operates a wholesale SMS and voice hub; and WHEREAS Del Rio wishes to purchase termination services;

NOW THEREFORE the Parties agree as follows.

### 2. Definitions
*(standard definitions omitted for brevity in this schedule; see Master Definitions Exhibit)*

### 3. Term
3.1. Initial term: forty-eight (48) months from the Contract Date.

### 4. Pricing

4.1. Current rates are fixed as set forth in Exhibit 1 (Rate Schedule).

4.2. **Pricing Lock.** The Parties agree that the rates set forth in Exhibit 1 shall remain fixed and unamendable for a period of thirty-six (36) months from the Contract Date (the "Lock Period"), expiring **September 9, 2025**. During the Lock Period, neither Party may propose, demand, or implement any change to the rates in Exhibit 1, notwithstanding any other provision of this Agreement.

4.3. Following expiration of the Lock Period, either Party may propose rate amendments upon forty-five (45) days' written notice, subject to Section 4.4.

4.4. No rate amendment proposed under Section 4.3 shall take effect unless the receiving Party affirmatively accepts the proposed rates in writing; silence shall not constitute acceptance.

### 5. Volume Commitments
5.1. Del Rio commits to a minimum of 500,000 minutes and 10,000,000 SMS transactions per month.

### 6. Quality Standards
6.1. SummitLink shall maintain SMS delivery rate of at least 97%.

### 7. Suspension and Termination
7.1. SummitLink may suspend Services for non-payment exceeding forty-five (45) days.
7.2. Either Party may terminate for convenience on one hundred eighty (180) days' notice, but such termination may not take effect before the end of the Lock Period defined in Section 4.2.

### 8. Force Majeure
8.1. Standard force majeure provisions apply.

### 9. Governing Law and Venue
9.1. This Agreement is governed by the laws of Texas, USA. Venue lies in Travis County.

### 10. Entire Agreement
10.1. This Agreement, together with its Exhibits, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties.
