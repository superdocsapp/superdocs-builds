# INTERCONNECTION AND WHOLESALE VOICE TERMINATION AGREEMENT

**Agreement No.:** NSC-IX-2021-0447
**Parties:** Northstar Wholesale Carrier Services, Inc. ("Northstar") and Meridian Global Transit Ltd. ("Meridian")
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2021

---

## Article 1 — Definitions

1.1 "Services" means the origination, transit, and termination of wholesale voice traffic between the Parties' networks as further described in Schedule A.

1.2 "Rate Card" means the then-current schedule of per-minute rates attached as Schedule A, as may be amended under Article 7.

1.3 "Effective Date" means the date first written above.

## Article 2 — Term

2.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of twenty-four (24) months, renewing automatically thereafter for successive twelve (12) month periods unless terminated per Article 9.

## Article 3 — Services and Routing

3.1 Northstar shall terminate traffic delivered by Meridian in accordance with the routing tables set forth in Schedule B.

3.2 Meridian shall provide traffic forecasts on a monthly basis.

## Article 4 — Rates and Billing

4.1 Rates for the Services are set forth in the Rate Card attached as Schedule A, effective as of the Effective Date.

4.2 Invoices shall be issued monthly in arrears, payable net thirty (30) days.

## Article 5 — Quality of Service

5.1 Northstar shall maintain an Answer Seizure Ratio (ASR) of no less than 40% on aggregate traffic, measured monthly.

## Article 6 — Taxes

6.1 Each Party is responsible for taxes assessed on its own revenues.

## Article 7 — Rate Amendments

7.1 Either Party may propose an amendment to the Rate Card by delivering written notice to the other Party at least **thirty (30) calendar days** prior to the proposed effective date of the new rates.

7.2 The notice shall specify the proposed new rates and the proposed effective date, and shall be delivered by email to the designated commercial contact identified in Schedule C, with confirmation of receipt.

7.3 Unless the receiving Party objects in writing within ten (10) business days of receipt, the proposed rate amendment shall become effective automatically on the date specified in the notice.

7.4 Rate amendments delivered under this Article 7 do not require the countersignature of both Parties; notice alone, absent timely objection, is sufficient to bind both Parties to the revised Rate Card.

## Article 8 — Pricing Stability

8.1 There is no minimum pricing lock period under this Agreement. Rates may be amended at any time in accordance with Article 7, subject only to the thirty (30) day notice requirement.

## Article 9 — Termination

9.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon ninety (90) days' written notice.

## Article 10 — Miscellaneous

10.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

10.2 Notices under this Agreement shall be sent to the addresses in Schedule C.
