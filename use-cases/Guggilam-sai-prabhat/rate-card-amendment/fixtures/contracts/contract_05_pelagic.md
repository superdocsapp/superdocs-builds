PELAGIC CROSS-BORDER CARRIER SERVICES AGREEMENT
Reference: PCB-2018-0056-R2
Parties: Pelagic Cross-Border Carrier Services Sdn Bhd ("Pelagic") and Harrowgate Telecom Wholesale LLC ("Harrowgate")
Contract Date: 23 January 2018
(This document reflects Restatement 2, incorporating Amendment Letters 1 and 2 dated 2019 and 2021, which are NOT attached to this copy.)

---

§1. BACKGROUND

§1.1 Pelagic and Harrowgate have interconnected since 2014 under a prior agreement (the "2014 Agreement"), which this Agreement restates and, in part, supersedes. Certain provisions of the 2014 Agreement survive per §10.4 below.

§2. SERVICES

§2.1 Pelagic terminates traffic for Harrowgate on the routes in the Rate Table (see Schedule 1, as subsequently modified by Amendment Letter 2 — not attached hereto).

§3. TERM

§3.1 This Agreement is effective as of the Contract Date and continues until superseded or terminated.

§4. RATES AND AMENDMENTS

§4.1 The rates in effect are those most recently agreed between the Parties in writing, whether in Schedule 1, a subsequent amendment letter, or a course-of-dealing rate confirmation exchanged via email.

§4.2 Rate changes may be proposed by either Party. Where Schedule 1 conflicts with a later amendment letter or email confirmation, the later document controls, except where §9.3 applies, in which case the original Schedule 1 rates control unless both Parties have separately reconfirmed the change in a signed writing after the date of this restatement.

§4.3 Notice of a proposed rate change must be given "within a commercially reasonable period" before the desired effective date. What constitutes a commercially reasonable period is not otherwise defined in this Agreement and may vary depending on the nature of the change.

§4.4 [Reserved — see Amendment Letter 1, §2, which modifies this Section but is not attached to this copy.]

§5. QUALITY OF SERVICE

§5.1 Pelagic shall use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain service quality consistent with industry norms.

§6. INVOICING

§6.1 Monthly invoicing. Payment terms as agreed from time to time between the Parties' finance teams (no fixed term stated in this restatement).

§7. CONFIDENTIALITY

§7.1 Standard mutual confidentiality obligations apply.

§8. SUSPENSION

§8.1 Either Party may suspend Services on notice in the event of suspected fraud.

§9. INTERPRETATION AND CONFLICTS

§9.1 In the event of conflict between this Agreement and any Schedule, this Agreement controls, except as provided in §9.3.
§9.2 In the event of conflict between this Agreement and any amendment letter, the amendment letter controls as to the specific subject matter it addresses.
§9.3 Where §9.1 and §9.2 would produce different results for the same provision, the Parties' course of dealing over the preceding twelve (12) months shall be used to determine intent; if course of dealing is also unclear, the provision shall be treated as unresolved pending mutual clarification.

§10. TERM, TERMINATION, AND SURVIVAL

§10.1 Either Party may terminate on notice; the notice period is "as set out in the 2014 Agreement," which is not attached to this copy.
§10.2 [Intentionally left blank in this restatement.]
§10.4 Certain provisions of the 2014 Agreement survive this restatement, specifically those listed in Schedule 3 (not attached to this copy).

§11. GOVERNING LAW

§11.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Malaysia.
