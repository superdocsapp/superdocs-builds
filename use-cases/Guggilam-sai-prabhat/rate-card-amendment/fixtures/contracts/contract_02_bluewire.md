BLUEWIRE INTERNATIONAL TRANSIT SERVICES AGREEMENT
Contract Reference: BW/2019/TRANSIT/118-C
Between: Bluewire International Transit Services B.V. ("Bluewire")
And: Cape Aurora Telecom (Pty) Ltd. ("Cape Aurora")
Dated: 14 June 2019

================================================================
SECTION 1. SCOPE OF AGREEMENT
================================================================

1(a) Bluewire agrees to provide international voice transit services to Cape Aurora on the routes listed in Annex 1 (Rate Table).

1(b) Cape Aurora agrees to route a minimum committed volume of 2,000,000 minutes per month.

================================================================
SECTION 2. DURATION
================================================================

2(a) This Agreement is effective from 14 June 2019 and continues for thirty-six (36) months, after which it converts to an evergreen month-to-month arrangement absent notice of non-renewal.

================================================================
SECTION 3. RATES
================================================================

3(a) Rates applicable to the Services are those set out in Annex 1, incorporated herein by reference.

3(b) Rates are quoted in USD per minute, billed in six-second increments after an eighteen-second minimum call duration.

================================================================
SECTION 4. RATE REVISIONS AND AMENDMENTS
================================================================

4(a) Bluewire may revise the rates in Annex 1 by delivering not less than **sixty (60) calendar days'** advance written notice to Cape Aurora's designated contract manager.

4(b) Any such revision notice must be delivered by courier or registered post to the notice address in Section 9, and must reference this Agreement's Contract Reference number.

4(c) Cape Aurora may, within thirty (30) days of receiving a revision notice, elect to terminate the affected routes without penalty rather than accept the revised rates.

4(d) No amendment to this Section 4 or to Annex 1 shall be effective unless made in accordance with the notice procedure above; email notice alone is NOT sufficient under this Agreement.

================================================================
SECTION 5. SERVICE LEVELS
================================================================

5(a) Bluewire targets a Post-Dial Delay (PDD) of under 6 seconds on 95% of call attempts.

================================================================
SECTION 6. INVOICING AND PAYMENT
================================================================

6(a) Invoices are issued on the 5th business day of each month for the prior month's traffic. Payment terms are net twenty-one (21) days.

================================================================
SECTION 7. CONFIDENTIALITY
================================================================

7(a) Each Party shall keep the terms of this Agreement, including the Rate Table, confidential.

================================================================
SECTION 8. TERMINATION
================================================================

8(a) Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within thirty (30) days of written notice of the breach.

================================================================
SECTION 9. NOTICES
