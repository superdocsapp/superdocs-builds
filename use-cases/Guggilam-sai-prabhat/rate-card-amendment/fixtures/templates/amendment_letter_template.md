# RATE AMENDMENT NOTICE — TEMPLATE

*This is a template for use by the wholesale contracts team. Bracketed fields must be completed before sending. Do not send with placeholder text remaining.*

---

**Date:** [NOTICE_DATE]

**To:** [COUNTERPARTY_NOTICE_CONTACT], [COUNTERPARTY_LEGAL_NAME]
**From:** [OUR_NOTICE_CONTACT], [OUR_LEGAL_NAME]

**Re: Notice of Rate Amendment under Agreement [CONTRACT_IDENTIFIER], dated [CONTRACT_DATE]**

Dear [COUNTERPARTY_CONTACT_NAME],

Pursuant to [AMENDMENT_CLAUSE_REFERENCE] of the above-referenced Agreement between [OUR_LEGAL_NAME] and [COUNTERPARTY_LEGAL_NAME] dated [CONTRACT_DATE] (the "Agreement"), this letter constitutes formal written notice of a proposed amendment to the rates set forth in [RATE_SCHEDULE_REFERENCE].

**1. Proposed Change**
The rates applicable to the destinations listed in Attachment A shall be revised as set forth therein, replacing the rates currently in effect under [RATE_SCHEDULE_REFERENCE].

**2. Proposed Effective Date**
The revised rates shall take effect on **[PROPOSED_EFFECTIVE_DATE]**, which is not less than [REQUIRED_NOTICE_PERIOD] days from the date of this notice, as required under [AMENDMENT_CLAUSE_REFERENCE].

**3. Basis for Amendment**
This notice is delivered under the amendment mechanism specified in the Agreement. Depending on the governing clause, one of the following applies — the sender must confirm which applies to this Agreement before sending:

- [ ] **Notice-only amendment**: Under this Agreement's terms, this rate change becomes effective on the Proposed Effective Date unless the recipient objects in writing within the objection window specified in the Agreement. No countersignature is required.
- [ ] **Mutual written consent required**: Under this Agreement's terms, this notice is an invitation to negotiate only. The proposed rates in Attachment A do not take effect unless and until both Parties execute a signed written amendment.
- [ ] **Pricing lock in effect**: Before sending this notice, confirm that no pricing-lock or price-protection period under the Agreement is currently in force. If a lock period is in force, this notice must not be sent until the lock period has expired.

**4. Recipient Action Required**
[ ] No action required unless objecting (notice-only Agreements).
[ ] Countersignature required — see Attachment B, Amendment Signature Page.

**5. Attachments**
- Attachment A: Revised Rate Schedule
- Attachment B: Amendment Signature Page (if mutual consent is required)

Please contact [OUR_NOTICE_CONTACT] with any questions regarding this notice.

Sincerely,

[SENDER_NAME]
[SENDER_TITLE]
[OUR_LEGAL_NAME]

---

**Internal drafting checklist (remove before sending):**
1. Confirm the Agreement's exact notice-period requirement (30, 45, 60, or other days) and that [PROPOSED_EFFECTIVE_DATE] satisfies it.
2. Confirm whether a pricing lock or consent requirement applies before drafting this letter — do not rely on this template alone to make that determination.
3. Confirm the correct notice delivery method required by the Agreement (email, courier, registered post) — delivery method defects can invalidate notice.
4. If the source contract is ambiguous or internally inconsistent regarding amendment rights, escalate to Legal before sending any notice.
