United States Citizenship and Immigration Services
Request for Evidence — Case Reference: CASE-2002 (synthetic)

The petitioner has established a marriage-based petition on behalf of the
beneficiary. Before a decision can be made, the following additional
evidence is required:

Issue 1: The record does not contain sufficient evidence of a bona fide
marital relationship beyond the joint bank statements already submitted.
Provide additional documentation such as joint lease agreements, joint
utility bills, or affidavits from third parties with personal knowledge
of the relationship.

Issue 2: The marriage certificate submitted was not accompanied by a
certified English translation. Provide a certified translation of the
marriage certificate along with the translator's certification of
accuracy and competency.

Issue 3: The petitioner's evidence of financial support does not clearly
establish income sufficient to meet the applicable poverty guidelines.
Provide the petitioner's most recent federal tax return or an
explanation of alternative qualifying income.

Failure to respond with the requested evidence within the stated period
may result in denial of the petition based on the record as it currently
stands.
