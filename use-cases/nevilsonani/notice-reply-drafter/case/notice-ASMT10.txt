Sir/Madam,

This is to inform that during scrutiny of the returns furnished by you for
the aforesaid tax period, the following discrepancies have been noticed:

Discrepancy 1: Outward taxable supplies declared in FORM GSTR-1 amount to
Rs. 48,20,000 whereas outward taxable supplies declared in FORM GSTR-3B for
the same period amount to Rs. 45,10,000. The difference of Rs. 3,10,000
results in an apparent short payment of tax of Rs. 9,300 (CGST Rs. 4,650 +
SGST Rs. 4,650) at the applicable rate of 3%.

Discrepancy 2: Input tax credit availed in FORM GSTR-3B (Table 4A) amounts
to Rs. 2,80,000 whereas credit available as per FORM GSTR-2B for the said
period amounts to Rs. 2,35,000. Excess availment of Rs. 45,000 appears to
contravene section 16(2)(aa) of the CGST Act, 2017.

Discrepancy 3: Interest under section 50 on the delayed payment of tax for
the month of May 2026 (return filed on 28-Jun-2026 against due date of
20-Jun-2026) does not appear to have been discharged.

You are hereby directed to furnish explanation for the above discrepancies
within THIRTY (30) days from the date of service of this notice. In case no
satisfactory explanation is furnished, proceedings under section 73 or
section 74 of the Act may be initiated.
