Data Processing Agreement

This Data Processing Agreement (the “DPA”) forms part of the Master Services Agreement dated 14 March 2026 between Cobalt Freight Systems Ltd (the “Controller”) and Northwind Analytics Ltd (the “Processor”), and applies to the processing of personal data carried out by the Processor on behalf of the Controller.

1. Scope of Processing

The Processor shall process personal data only for the purpose of providing the Services and only on the documented instructions of the Controller. It is hereby expressly acknowledged and agreed between the parties that the Processor shall not process personal data for its own purposes, including for the training of machine learning models, without the separate written consent of the Controller.

The categories of data subject, the categories of personal data, and the nature and duration of the processing are set out in Annex A.

2. Confidentiality of Personnel

The Processor shall ensure that each person authorised to process the personal data is subject to a duty of confidentiality, whether contractual or statutory, and has received appropriate training on the handling of personal data. The Processor shall maintain a record of authorised personnel and shall make that record available to the Controller on request.

3. Security Measures

The Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure or access. Such measures shall include encryption of personal data in transit and at rest, role-based access control, and logging of access to production systems retained for not less than twelve (12) months.

4. Sub-processing

The Processor shall not engage a sub-processor without the Controller's prior written authorisation. Where authorisation is given, the Processor shall impose on the sub-processor data protection obligations no less protective than those set out in this DPA, and shall remain fully liable to the Controller for the performance of that sub-processor's obligations.

The Processor shall give the Controller not less than thirty (30) days' written notice of any intended addition or replacement of a sub-processor, during which period the Controller may object on reasonable data protection grounds.

5. Data Subject Rights

Taking into account the nature of the processing, the Processor shall assist the Controller by appropriate technical and organisational measures, insofar as this is possible, in fulfilling the Controller's obligation to respond to requests from data subjects exercising their rights. The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay if it receives such a request directly, and shall not respond to that request itself unless instructed to do so by the Controller.

6. Personal Data Breach

The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting personal data processed under this DPA. The notification shall describe the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects affected, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed to address it.

7. Deletion and Return

On termination of the Services, the Processor shall, at the Controller's election, delete or return all personal data processed on the Controller's behalf, and shall delete existing copies unless retention is required by applicable law. The Processor shall certify deletion in writing within thirty (30) days of the Controller's request. Signed on behalf of the parties on 05/06/2026.
