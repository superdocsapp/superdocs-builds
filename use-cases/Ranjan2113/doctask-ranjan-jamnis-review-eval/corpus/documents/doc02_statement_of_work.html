Statement of Work — SOW-2026-014

This Statement of Work is issued under and governed by the Master Services Agreement dated 14 March 2026 between Cobalt Freight Systems Ltd (the “Client”) and Northwind Analytics Ltd (the “Vendor”). Capitalised terms not defined here have the meaning given to them in the Master Services Agreement.

1. Project Description

The Vendor shall design, build and deliver a freight utilisation reporting platform for the Client's UK depot network. It is acknowledged and agreed by and between the parties hereto that the platform shall ingest telematics data from the Client's existing fleet management system and shall produce daily utilisation reporting at depot, region and national level.

2. Deliverables

The Vendor shall deliver: (a) a data ingestion pipeline covering all twelve (12) UK depots; (b) a reporting dashboard accessible to up to fifty (50) named Client users; (c) a written handover document describing the architecture and operational runbook; and (d) two half-day training sessions for Client staff.

Each deliverable shall be deemed accepted when the Client confirms in writing that it meets the acceptance criteria in Schedule 1, or fifteen (15) business days after delivery if the Client has raised no written objection.

3. Timeline

Work shall commence on 6 April 2026. The Vendor shall deliver the ingestion pipeline by 29 May 2026, the reporting dashboard by 10 July 2026, and shall complete handover and training by 07/08/2026.

The parties acknowledge that the timeline assumes the Client provides access to the fleet management system's API within ten (10) business days of commencement. Delay in providing such access shall extend each subsequent milestone by an equivalent period.

4. Charges

The total fixed price for the Services described in this Statement of Work is one hundred and eighty thousand pounds (£180,000), exclusive of VAT. This sum shall be invoiced in three equal instalments of sixty thousand pounds (£60,000) on commencement, on acceptance of the reporting dashboard, and on completion of handover respectively.

Expenses shall be reimbursed at cost where agreed in advance in writing by the Client, and shall not exceed five per cent (5%) of the total fixed price without the Client's prior written approval.

5. Client Responsibilities

The Client shall make available a nominated project sponsor, provide timely access to the systems and data described in Schedule 2, and respond to written requests for decisions within five (5) business days. The Client's failure to do so shall not constitute a breach of this Statement of Work but may affect the timeline as described in Clause 3.

6. Change Control

Any change to the scope, deliverables, timeline or charges set out in this Statement of Work shall be documented in a written change request signed by an authorised representative of each party. No change shall take effect until so signed. The Supplier shall provide an impact assessment covering cost and timeline within five (5) business days of receiving a change request.

7. Key Personnel

The Vendor shall assign Dr Amara Okafor as technical lead and Jonas Petrelli as delivery manager for the duration of this Statement of Work. The Vendor shall not replace either individual without the Client's prior written consent, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld, except where the individual leaves the Vendor's employment.
