Master Services Agreement

This Master Services Agreement (the “Agreement”) is entered into as of 14 March 2026 (the “Effective Date”) by and between Cobalt Freight Systems Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales with company number 09182736, having its registered office at 44 Harbour Road, Bristol BS1 5TY (the “Client”), and Northwind Analytics Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales with company number 11223344, having its registered office at 8 Fenwick Street, Manchester M2 3QQ (the “Vendor”).

1. Scope of Services

The Vendor shall provide to the Client the data analytics and reporting services described in Schedule A (the “Services”). It is hereby agreed and understood by and between the parties hereto that the Vendor shall be responsible for the performance and delivery of all of the Services in accordance with the standards set out herein. The Vendor shall assign a named account lead who shall serve as the primary point of contact for the Client throughout the Term.

The Supplier shall not subcontract any material portion of the Services without the prior written consent of the Client, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld or delayed.

2. Term

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for an initial period of twenty-four (24) months (the “Initial Term”), unless terminated earlier in accordance with Clause 7. Following the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive periods of twelve (12) months each, unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal not less than sixty (60) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

3. Fees and Payment

In consideration of the Services, the Client shall pay the Vendor the fees set out in Schedule B. The Vendor shall invoice the Client monthly in arrears. The Client shall pay each correctly rendered invoice within thirty (30) days of the date of receipt of such invoice.

Any sum not paid when due shall bear interest at the rate of four per cent (4%) per annum above the base rate of the Bank of England from time to time, accruing daily from the due date until the date the Client shall recieve confirmation of settlement in full.

All fees stated are exclusive of value added tax, which shall be added to each invoice at the prevailing rate.

4. Service Levels

The Vendor shall meet the service levels set out in Schedule C. Where the Vendor fails to meet a service level in any calendar month, the Client shall be entitled to the service credits specified in Schedule C as its sole financial remedy for such failure. Service credits shall be applied against the next invoice issued following the month in which the failure occurred.

5. Confidentiality

Each party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information of the other party disclosed to it under or in connection with this Agreement, and shall not use such Confidential Information other than for the purpose of performing its obligations under this Agreement. This obligation shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years.

6. Data Protection

Each party shall comply with all applicable data protection legislation in respect of any personal data processed in connection with this Agreement. Where the Vendor processes personal data on behalf of the Client, the Vendor shall act only on the documented instructions of the Client and shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect such personal data. The Vendor shall notify the Client without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours, upon becoming aware of any personal data breach.

7. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving not less than ninety (90) days’ written notice to the other party. The Client may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the Vendor commits a material breach of this Agreement which is not remedied within thirty (30) days of written notice requiring it to be remedied.

On termination of this Agreement for any reason, the Vendor shall promptly return or securely destroy all Confidential Information of the Client in its possession, and shall provide the Client with a written certificate confirming it has done so.

8. Limitation of Liability

Nothing in this Agreement shall limit or exclude either party’s liability for death or personal injury caused by its negligence, for fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, or for any other liability which cannot lawfully be limited or excluded.

Subject to the foregoing, the total aggregate liability of the Vendor arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise, shall not exceed an amount equal to one hundred and fifty per cent (150%) of the total fees paid by the Client under this Agreement in the twelve (12) month period immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

Neither party shall be liable to the other for any indirect or consequential loss, or for any loss of profit, loss of revenue, or loss of anticipated savings, in each case whether arising in contract, tort or otherwise.

9. Governing Law

This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the law of England and Wales, and the parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales. Executed by the duly authorised representatives of the parties on 03/04/2026.
