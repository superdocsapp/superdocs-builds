Service Level Agreement — Schedule C

This Schedule C sets out the service levels applicable to the Services provided by Northwind Analytics Ltd (the “Vendor”) to Cobalt Freight Systems Ltd (the “Client”) under the Master Services Agreement dated 14 March 2026.

1. Service Availability

The Vendor shall make the reporting platform available not less than 99.5% of the time, measured monthly, excluding Scheduled Maintenance. It is agreed and understood by and between the parties hereto that availability shall be measured by the Vendor's monitoring system at five (5) minute intervals from at least two independent geographic locations.

Scheduled Maintenance shall not exceed four (4) hours in any calendar month and shall be carried out between 22:00 and 06:00 UK time on a Saturday or Sunday, with not less than five (5) business days' notice to the Client.

2. Support Response Times

The Vendor shall respond to support requests within the following target times, measured from receipt of the request during Business Hours:

Priority 1 (service unavailable): respond within one (1) hour, restore within four (4) hours. Priority 2 (major function impaired): respond within four (4) hours, restore within one (1) business day. Priority 3 (minor fault or query): respond within one (1) business day, restore within five (5) business days.

Business Hours means 08:00 to 18:00 UK time on business days. Priority 1 requests shall be accepted outside Business Hours via the emergency contact number set out in Annex B.

3. Data Freshness

Telematics data ingested from the Client's fleet management system shall be reflected in the reporting dashboard within thirty (30) minutes of receipt. Where the Vendor's ingestion pipeline is unable to process data within that period, the Vendor shall notify the Client's nominated contact and provide an estimated time to resolution.

4. Service Credits

Where the Vendor fails to meet the availability target in any calendar month, the Client shall be entitled to a service credit calculated as a percentage of the monthly charges for that month: availability of 99.0% to 99.49% attracts a credit of five per cent (5%); availability of 98.0% to 98.99% attracts a credit of ten per cent (10%); availability below 98.0% attracts a credit of fifteen per cent (15%).

Service credits shall be applied against the next invoice issued after the month in which the failure occurred, and are the Client's sole financial remedy for a failure to meet a service level.

5. Reporting

The Vendor shall provide the Client with a written service report within ten (10) business days of the end of each calendar month, setting out measured availability, the number of support requests by priority, response and restoration times achieved, and any service credits due.

6. Continuous Improvement

The parties shall meet quarterly to review service performance against these service levels. The Supplier shall present a summary of any recurring faults, their root causes, and the remedial actions taken or planned. The service levels in this Schedule may be revised by written agreement between the parties following any such review.

7. Exclusions

The Vendor shall not be responsible for a failure to meet a service level to the extent that the failure arises from: Scheduled Maintenance; a fault in the Client's own systems or network; the Client's failure to provide access or information reasonably requested by the Vendor; or an event beyond the Vendor's reasonable control. This Schedule takes effect on 04/05/2026.
