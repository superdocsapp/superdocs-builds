# Status Page and Customer Comms Runbook

Owner: Support Engineering. This runbook is fictional and describes Northwind Systems, a company that does not exist.

## When to post

Post to the status page when customer-visible impact has lasted more than ten minutes, or immediately on any Sev-1 regardless of duration. Waiting for certainty before posting is the most common comms failure.

## Who posts

The Communications Lead posts. If no Communications Lead has been named, the Incident Commander posts. Nobody else posts, ever, because two voices on a status page reads as a company that does not know what is happening.

## What to say

Say what is affected, what customers will experience, what is being done, and when the next update comes. Do not name internal systems, do not speculate on cause, and never give a restoration estimate you cannot defend.

## Cadence

Update every thirty minutes on a Sev-1 until resolution, then post a resolution note. Mirror each update in #incident-war-room so the incident channel and the public timeline never disagree.
