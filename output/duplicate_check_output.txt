Effectiveness of Lifestyle Interventions for
Type 2 Diabetes Remission: A Systematic
Review
Abstract
Background: Type 2 diabetes remission through lifestyle intervention has gained clinical interest.
This review synthesizes evidence on intervention effectiveness.
Methods
We searched multiple databases for relevant studies published between 2015 and 2025.
