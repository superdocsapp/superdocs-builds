<h1 data-chunk-id="60b4df61-09f7-4b73-b49d-cfc85850bddd">Effectiveness of Lifestyle Interventions for Type 2 Diabetes Remission: A Systematic Review</h1>
<h2 data-chunk-id="46f22439-3882-402a-b3a8-3ae902a8bc50">Abstract</h2>
<p data-chunk-id="56a60a5a-5773-43a2-91eb-d4fe596eb6f9">Background: Type 2 diabetes remission through lifestyle intervention has gained clinical interest. This review synthesizes evidence on intervention effectiveness.</p>
<h2 data-chunk-id="cf6ae231-951f-4f4a-8ca0-740c5f6a6886">Methods</h2>
<p data-chunk-id="77723058-cc58-40f8-8209-bf35c8f29861">We searched multiple databases for relevant studies published between 2015 and 2025.</p>
