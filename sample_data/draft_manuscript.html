Effectiveness of Lifestyle Interventions for Type 2 Diabetes Remission: A Systematic Review

Abstract

Background: Type 2 diabetes remission through lifestyle intervention has gained clinical interest. This review synthesizes evidence on intervention effectiveness.

Methods

We searched multiple databases for relevant studies published between 2015 and 2025.

Results

We identified relevant studies meeting inclusion criteria.

Discussion

The findings suggest lifestyle interventions may support diabetes remission in select populations.
